# Electric Charge Ring

![Electric Charge Ring](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/10185145.png)

Ring, rare 
 
 
This ring, composed of living lightning, is forged from elemental dust compressed into a gem around the lens eye of a construct, washed in the acidic glands of a monster before being set into an animus of an elemental.
 

When you cast a spell using a spell slot that deals Lightning damage, choose one target that failed its first saving throw against that spell. That creature is Shocked until the start of your next turn.
 

Crafting. Creating an Electric Charge Ring requires a Rare Workshop and the following Rare Components:
 
 

[Animus] Animus from an Elemental
 

[Dust] Elemental Dust
 

[Organ] Eye from a Construct
 

[Organ] Glands
 

Monsters of Drakkenheim, pg. 385
