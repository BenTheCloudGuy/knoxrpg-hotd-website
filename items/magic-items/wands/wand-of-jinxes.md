# Wand of Jinxes

![Wand of Jinxes](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/10185346.png)

Wand, rare (requires attunement by a Warlock)
 
 
This wand is carved from magical bark, with the hair of an undead creature in its core and finished in goopy ooze.
 

Once per turn when you deal damage with a Warlock cantrip to a creature, you can make that creature Jinxed (1d4) until the start of your next turn.
 

Crafting. Creating a Wand of Jinxes requires a Rare Workshop and the following Rare Components:
 
 

[Animus] Any Animus
 

[Fluid] Goopy Ooze
 

[Hair] Hair from an Undead
 

[Hide] Bark
 

Monsters of Drakkenheim, pg. 394
