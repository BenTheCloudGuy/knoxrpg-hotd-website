# Psi Crystal (Int 16+)

![Psi Crystal (Int 16+)](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/2188394.jpeg)

Wondrous Item, uncommon (requires attunement by a creature with an Intelligence score of 3 or higher)
 
 
This crystal grants you telepathy with a range of 120 feet for as long as you remain attuned to it. See the introduction of the Monster Manual for rules on how this telepathy works.
 

The crystal also glows with a purplish bright light in a 15-foot radius and dim light for an additional 15 feet while you are attuned to it.

Icewind Dale: Rime of the Frostmaiden, pg. 315
