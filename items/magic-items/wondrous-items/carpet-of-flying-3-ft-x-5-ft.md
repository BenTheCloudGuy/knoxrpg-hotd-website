# Carpet of Flying (3 ft. x 5 ft.)

Wondrous Item, very rare 
 
 
You can make this carpet hover and fly by taking a Magic action and using the carpet’s command word. It moves according to your directions if you are within 30 feet of it.
 

This carpet is 3 feet by 5 feet and has a Fly Speed of 80 feet. It can carry up to 400 pounds, but its Fly Speed becomes 40 feet if it carries more than 200 pounds.

Dungeon Master’s Guide, pg. 242
