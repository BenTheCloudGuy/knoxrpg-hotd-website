# Psi Crystal (Int 3-7)

![Psi Crystal (Int 3-7)](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/2188395.jpeg)

Wondrous Item, uncommon (requires attunement by a creature with an Intelligence score of 3 or higher)
 
 
This crystal grants you telepathy with a range of 15 feet for as long as you remain attuned to it. See the introduction of the Monster Manual for rules on how this telepathy works.
 

The crystal also glows with a purplish dim light out to a range of 5 feet while you are attuned to it.

Icewind Dale: Rime of the Frostmaiden, pg. 315
