# Lantern of Tracking (Undead)

Wondrous Item, common 
 
 
This hooded lantern burns for 6 hours on 1 pint of oil, shedding bright light in a 30-foot radius and dim light for an additional 30 feet.
 

While the lantern is within 300 feet of any creature of the Undead type, its flame turns bright green. The lantern doesn’t pinpoint the creature’s exact location, however.

Icewind Dale: Rime of the Frostmaiden, pg. 314
