# Manual of Occult Anatomy (Rare)

Wondrous Item, rare (requires attunement by a Warlock)
 
 
Filled with diagrams showing humans with alien features, this rare book of strange medical lore is written in monster-blood ink infused with draconic dust and then etched onto parchment pages made from aberration skin. The manuscript is bound in a scale-clad cover.
 

You can use this manual as a Spellcasting Focus for your Warlock spells. When you use the manual as a Spellcasting Focus, you gain a +1 bonus to spell attack rolls and spell save DCs of your Warlock spells.
 

In addition, the book contains the knowledge of an Warlock Eldritch Invocation up to a 6th level, which you gain while you are attuned to it.
 

Crafting. Creating a Manual of Occult Anatomy (Rare) requires a rare Workshop and Components. The Components required are:
 
 

[Animus] Any Animus
 

[Dust] Any Dragon Dust
 

[Fluid] Monster Blood from a Monstrosity
 

[Hide] Scales
 

[Hide] Skin from an Aberration
