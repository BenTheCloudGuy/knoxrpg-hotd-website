# Wolfskin Cloak

Wondrous Item, uncommon (requires attunement)
 
 
While wearing this cloak, as an action, you can assume the form of a dire wolf, gaining its bite attack, speed, and AC. You also gain its Keen Hearing and Smell trait. You retain your own ability scores, hit points, and saving throws.

Book of Ebon Tides, pg. 197
