# Prism Glasses

![Prism Glasses](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/9445389.png)

Wondrous Item, common 
 
 
This pair of magical glasses fits snugly against your face without needing to rest over your ears. The lenses are made using miniscule prisms that amplify color in dim conditions. If you have darkvision while wearing these glasses, you perceive color for the first 15 feet you see in darkness, instead of shades of gray. In addition, a colorblind creature wearing these glasses can perceive color normally.

The Griffon’s Saddlebag: Book Two, pg. 139
