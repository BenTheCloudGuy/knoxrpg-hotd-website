# Hazewind Cape

![Hazewind Cape](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/10185176.png)

Wondrous Item, uncommon 
 
 
This crimson and gold hooded cape is fitted with the feathers of fallen Harpies and dyed in their blood, this grants the wearer some resistance to the songs of Harpies. The cape is then infused with the dust of a fey creature which grants it a powerful anti-charm effect powered by an animus.
 

While wearing this cape, you have Advantage on saving throws against the Charmed and Frightened conditions.
 

Crafting. Creating a Hazewind Cape requires an Uncommon Workshop and the following Uncommon Components:
 
 

 Animus from a [monsters]Harpy
 

[Dust] Fey Dust
 

[Fluid] Monster Blood from a Harpy
 

[Hair] Feathers from a Harpy
 

[Hide] Skin
