# Chunk of Oozing Fungus

![Chunk of Oozing Fungus](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/10241324.png)

Wondrous Item, rare (requires attunement)
 
 
This fist-sized fungal growth is a pallid color, is oozy and lumpy, and frequently fills the air with spores. The fungus has the following properties.
 

Spore Cloud. As a Magic action, you can throw the fungus up to 30 feet. The fungus is destroyed and explodes into a cloud of spores in a 30-foot Sphere centered on that point. Each creature other than you in the area must make a DC 14 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a target takes 3d8 Poison damage and has the Poisoned condition until the start of your next turn. A creature that fails this save by 5 or more also has the Incapacitated condition.
 

On a successful save, a target takes half damage only.
 

Regrow. You can grow another Chunk of Oozing Fungus by coughing green-black spores into your hand as a Bonus Action. When you do so, you must succeed on a DC 10 Constitution saving throw or your Hit Point maximum decreases by 5.
 

Fungal Curse. You have a −1 penalty to Constitution saving throws. This penalty increases by 1 immediately after you succeed or fail on the saving throw from the Regrow property.
