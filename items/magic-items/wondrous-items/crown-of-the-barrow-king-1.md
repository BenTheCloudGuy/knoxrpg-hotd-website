# Crown of the Barrow King, +1

![Crown of the Barrow King, +1](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/10241325.png)

Wondrous Item, rare (requires attunement)
 
 
This crown of twisted iron has five sharp, tall spikes that are crusted with dried blood. None who see it doubt the malice and corruption that the Barrow King imbued into it. While you wear the crown, you gain a bonus determined by the crown’s rarity to spell attack rolls and to your spell save DC for Necromancy spells and spells that deal Necrotic damage, and you can use the following properties.
 

Demand of the Dead (Rare+). You can cast Speak with Dead from the crown.
 

Grave Grasp (Rare+). You know the Chill Touch spell. Your spell attack bonus with it is +9 (Rare).
 

Necromancer’s Call (Rare+). You can cast Animate Dead from the crown. It can be used to cast this spell three times, regaining all expended uses daily at dusk.

The Crooked Moon Part One: Player Options & Campaign Setting, pg. 598
