# Runestone of the Wild Titan, +1

![Runestone of the Wild Titan, +1](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/10241380.png)

Wondrous Item, rare (requires attunement)
 
 
This mighty granite runestone is etched with complex glyphs on much of its angular surface. Though an incredible burden to carry, it thrums with the power of the wild titan. The stone weighs 50 pounds.
 

While holding the stone, you gain a bonus to Strength and Constitution saving throws determined by the runestone’s rarity, and you can use the following properties.
 

Crushing Weight (Rare+). When you are within 5 feet of a creature with the Prone condition, you can take a Bonus Action to set the stone on the creature to give it the Restrained condition. The creature can take an action to make a Strength saving throw (DC = 16 + the stone's bonus), pushing the stone free and ending the Restrained condition on a success.
