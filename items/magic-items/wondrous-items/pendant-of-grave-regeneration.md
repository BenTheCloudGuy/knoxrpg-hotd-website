# Pendant of Grave Regeneration

![Pendant of Grave Regeneration](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/8451145.png)

Wondrous Item, very rare (requires attunement)
 
 
While you wear this pendant, your skin becomes pale and cold to the touch, and you regain 5 hit points at the start of your turn if you have at least 1 hit point.
 

Curse. Attuning to this pendant extends its curse to you. Until the curse is broken with a remove curse spell or similar magic, you can’t take it off, and your creature type is Undead instead of your normal type.

Flee, Mortals!, pg. 350
