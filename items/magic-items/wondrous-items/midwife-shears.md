# Midwife Shears

![Midwife Shears](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/10241371.png)

Wondrous Item, rare (requires attunement)
 
 
These scissors are shaped to resemble a stork. Their blades are forever wickedly sharp, and the threads they cut are more than merely physical.
 

Cut the Unseen Thread. While holding the scissors, you can cast Dispel Magic (+4 bonus) or Remove Curse from them. Once either spell is cast from the scissors, that spell can’t be cast from them again until the next dusk.
 

Curse of the Severed Thread. When you make a saving throw to avoid being pushed or to avoid the Prone condition, you have a -1 penalty to the roll. This penalty increases by 1 each time you use the Cut the Unseen Thread property. If you fail such a save by 5 or more, you are pushed (1d6) x 5 feet straight away from the source of the save, in addition to any other distance pushed.

The Crooked Moon Part One: Player Options & Campaign Setting, pg. 611
