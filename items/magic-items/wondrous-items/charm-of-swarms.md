# Charm of Swarms

![Charm of Swarms](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/10185132.png)

Wondrous Item, uncommon 
 
 
This charm resembles a large gnat. It is made of the spores of dead mushrooms; the mandibles of an insectoid creature are placed upon it. The charm is coated in an acidic bile and held together by dried mucus. It smells of bugs and rot.
 

When an enemy within 5 feet of you scores a Critical Hit on an attack made against you, you can cause the enemy to become Burning (2d6 Necrotic). While the target is Burning in this way, you have Advantage on attacks against the target.
 

Crafting. Creating a Charm of Swarms requires an Uncommon Workshop and the following Uncommon Components:
 
 

[Animus] Any Animus
 

[Fluid] Acidic Bile
 

[Fluid] Mucus
 

[Natural Weapon] Spores
 

[Natural Weapon] Mandibles
