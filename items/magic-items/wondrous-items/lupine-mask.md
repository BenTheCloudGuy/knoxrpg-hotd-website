# Lupine Mask

![Lupine Mask](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/9445280.png)

Wondrous Item, uncommon 
 
 
While wearing this dark ceramic mask, you have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks relying on hearing and smell, as well as on Wisdom (Animal Handling) checks made to interact with dogs and doglike beasts, such as foxes, jackals, and wolves.

The Griffon’s Saddlebag: Book Two, pg. 117
