# Xorian Wenge Focus

Wondrous Item, common (requires attunement)
 
 
A Xorian wenge focus is a rod, staff, or wand cut from a tree infused with extraplanar energy. If you're a spellcaster, you can use this as a spellcasting focus.
 

When you cast a spell that deals psychic damage using this item as your spellcasting focus, you gain a +1 bonus to one damage roll of the spell.

Wayfinder's Guide to Eberron
