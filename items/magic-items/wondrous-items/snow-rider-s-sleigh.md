# Snow Rider's Sleigh

![Snow Rider's Sleigh](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/9445507.png)

Wondrous Item, common 
 
 
This magic sled moves effortlessly over land as if it were covered in fresh snow and sends small flurries of illusory snowflakes behind it as it travels.

The Griffon’s Saddlebag: Book Two, pg. 164
