# Goblin Chisel

Wondrous Item, uncommon 
 
 
Used in the construction of a small goblin community in the Paletree Rise, this chisel is made of hardened grick beak. While using the goblin chisel, your proficiency bonus is doubled for any ability check you make that uses your proficiency with Mason’s Tools or Woodcarver’s Tools.

Tales from the Shadows, pg. 175
