# Painted Bone Stone

Wondrous Item, uncommon 
 
 
The painted stones are magical and emanate different auras of magic, depending on the color. If the stones are taken from the Whispering Plains, they instantly lose their magical properties. Returning a stone to the Plains does not bring back its magic.
 

Only one stone can affect a creature at a time. Each stones has one charge. With a bonus action, a creature holding one of the stones can expend its single charge, activating the corresponding effect below:
 
 
 

 
Color 
Emanation 
Effect 

 
 
 

 
Bone 
Necromancy 
For 1 minute, each time you damage a creature with a weapon or spell attack, you regain an amount of hit points equal to your proficiency bonus. 

 
 

Tales from the Shadows, pg. 176
