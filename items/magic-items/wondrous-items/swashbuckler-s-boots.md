# Swashbuckler’s Boots

![Swashbuckler’s Boots](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/10185339.png)

Wondrous Item, uncommon 
 
 
While wearing these supple leather boots, Opportunity Attack actions against you have Disadvantage.
 

Crafting. Creating Swashbuckler's Boots requires an Uncommon Workshop and the following Uncommon Components:
 
 

[Animus] Any
 

[Dust] Fey Dust
 

[Fluid] Elemental Fluid
 

[Hide] Any
 

Monsters of Drakkenheim, pg. 392
