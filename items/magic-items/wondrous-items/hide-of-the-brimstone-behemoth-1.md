# Hide of the Brimstone Behemoth, +1

![Hide of the Brimstone Behemoth, +1](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/10241343.png)

Wondrous Item, rare (requires attunement)
 
 
This cloak was made from the hide of a reptilian magma Fiend, incomparable in its destructive power. Flame now shies away from it, for one who has mastered such a titanic creature can master its element. While wearing this cloak, you gain a bonus to Armor Class and Constitution saving throws determined by the cloak’s rarity, and you can use the following properties.
 

Deep-Dweller (Rare+). You have Resistance to Fire damage and a Burrow Speed equal to your Speed. Once during your turn when you emerge from burrowing, each creature in a 10-foot Emanation originating from you must make a Dexterity saving throw (DC = 16 + the hide’s bonus), taking 2d8 Fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.
 

Infernal Roar (Rare+). You can cast Fear (save DC = 16 + the hide’s bonus) from the cloak. This property can be used three times, regaining all expended uses daily at dusk.
