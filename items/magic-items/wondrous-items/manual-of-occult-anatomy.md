# Manual of Occult Anatomy

Wondrous Item, varies (requires attunement by a Warlock)
 
 
Filled with diagrams showing humans with alien features, this book of strange medical lore is written in monster-blood ink infused with draconic dust and then etched onto parchment pages made from aberration skin. The manuscript is bound in a scale-clad cover.
 

You can use this manual as a Spellcasting Focus for your Warlock spells. When you use the manual as a Spellcasting Focus, you gain a bonus to spell attack rolls and spell save DCs of your Warlock spells. The bonus is determined by the book's rarity (see the table below).
 

In addition, the book contains the knowledge of an Warlock Eldritch Invocation up to a level determined by the manual's rarity and chosen when the item is created, which you gain while you are attuned to it.
