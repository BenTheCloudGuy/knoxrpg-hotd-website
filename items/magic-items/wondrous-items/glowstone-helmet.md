# Glowstone Helmet

Wondrous Item, uncommon 
 
 
Glowstone helmets are usually of gnomish manufacture. While wearing this helm, you can use an action to cast a particular cantrip associated with that helmet (often dancing lights, light, mending, or sometimes produce flame). When combined with a frostglow weapon or a find the path spell, this helmet allows you to sense the direction and distance to the nearest ley line, shadow road, radiant well, and similar arcane landmarks.

Book of Ebon Tides, pg. 196
