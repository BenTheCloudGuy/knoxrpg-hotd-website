# Amulet of Proof against Detection and Location

![Amulet of Proof against Detection and Location](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/4569.jpeg)

Wondrous Item, uncommon (requires attunement)
 
 
While wearing this amulet, you can’t be targeted by Divination spells or perceived through magical scrying sensors unless you allow it.

Dungeon Master’s Guide, pg. 150
