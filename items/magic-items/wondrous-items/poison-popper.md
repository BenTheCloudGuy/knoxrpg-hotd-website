# Poison Popper

![Poison Popper](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/8451146.png)

Wondrous Item, uncommon 
 
 
You can throw this inedible fruit at a point you can see within 60 feet of you, causing it to explode and release a cloud of noxious gas in a 10-foot-radius sphere centered on that point. The cloud spreads around corners and lingers for 1 minute. A moderate wind (at least 10 miles per hour) disperses the cloud after 4 rounds. A strong wind (at least 20 miles per hour) disperses it after 1 round.
 

The cloud’s area is lightly obscured, and when a creature enters it for the first time on a turn or starts their turn there, they must make a DC 13 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 14 (4d6) poison damage and is poisoned for 1 minute (save ends at end of turn). While poisoned in this way, a creature’s speed is halved. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and suffers no other effect.

Flee, Mortals!, pg. 356
