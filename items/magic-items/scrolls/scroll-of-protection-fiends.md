# Scroll of Protection (Fiends)

![Scroll of Protection (Fiends)](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/9229009.png)

Scroll, rare 
 
 
Using a Magic action to read the scroll creates a 5-foot Emanation originating from you. For 5 minutes, Fiends can’t enter or affect anything in the area. However, if you move in such a way that a Fiend would be inside the area, the effect ends.
 

As a Magic action, a creature within 5 feet of the Emanation can attempt to overcome it, which forces the creature to make a DC 15 Charisma saving throw. On a successful save, the creature ceases to be affected by the Emanation.

Dungeon Master’s Guide, pg. 302
