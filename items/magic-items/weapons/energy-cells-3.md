# Energy Cells, +3

Weapon (any ammunition), very rare 
 
 
Futuristic firearms use Energy Cells that become depleted but could possibly be recharged with the proper equipment. You have a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this piece of magic ammunition. Once it hits a target, the ammunition is no longer magical.
