# Lunar Spear

Weapon (spear), uncommon 
 
 
Made by shadow goblins to keep larger foes at a distance, lunar lances are often given to goblins on battlements or other vantage points. They weigh literally nothing but are as sharp as any dagger. This spear adds 10 feet to your reach when attacking with it.

Proficiency with a Spear allows you to add your proficiency bonus to the attack roll for any attack you make with it.
 

 

This weapon has the following mastery property. To use this property, you must have a feature that lets you use it.
 

Sap. If you hit a creature with this weapon, that creature has Disadvantage on its next attack roll before the start of your next turn.
