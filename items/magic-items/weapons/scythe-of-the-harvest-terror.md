# Scythe of the Harvest Terror

![Scythe of the Harvest Terror](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/10241387.png)

Weapon (halberd), varies (requires attunement)
 
 
This menacing scythe with a rusted blade was wielded by the Harvest Terror, a demon of secrets and fear. The scythe appears old and worn, with a handle wrapped in ancient, weathered cloth. The blade is etched with hatch marks, tallying its kills. You gain a bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls with this magic weapon determined by its rarity. While holding the scythe, you can use the following properties.
 

 Feed on Fear (Rare+). Once during your turn, when you attack with the scythe and hit a creature that has the Frightened condition, it takes an extra 1d10 (Legendary: 2d10) Psychic damage.
 

Sweep of Dread (Rare+). You can cast Fear (save DC = 16 + the scythe’s bonus, no Concentration required) from the scythe. Once this property is used, it can’t be used again until the next dusk.
 

Harvest of the Hidden Word (Very Rare+). As a Bonus Action, you can force one creature you can see that has the Frightened condition to make a Charisma saving throw (DC = 16 + the scythe’s bonus). On a failed save, you learn one fact that it deliberately keeps secret. The creature is then immune to this property for 24 hours.
 

Seed of Terror (Very Rare+). Immediately after you hit a creature with the scythe, you can take a Bonus Action to force one creature within 30 feet of the creature you hit to make a Wisdom saving throw (DC = 16 + the Scythe’s bonus). On a failed save, the creature gains the Frightened condition until the end of your next turn.
 

Lurking Terror (Legendary). When a creature you can see that has the Frightened condition ends its turn within 60 feet of you, you can take a Reaction to teleport up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space the Frightened creature can see.
