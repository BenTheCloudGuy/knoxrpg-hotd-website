# Shortbow of the Cobra

![Shortbow of the Cobra](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/9445487.png)

Weapon (shortbow), rare (requires attunement)
 
 
This metallic shortbow resembles the shape of a mighty cobra and is covered in spots of patina. When you fire an arrow from the shortbow, you can choose to coat the arrow in a thin layer of acid. A target hit with the acid-coated arrow takes an extra 1d4 acid damage. Hit or miss, a nonmagical coated arrow is destroyed after the attack.
 

In addition, the shortbow has 4 charges and regains 1d4 expended charges daily at dawn. When you take the Attack action using this shortbow, you can expend 1 of its charges to replace one of your weapon attacks with a special attack. This special attack duplicates the effect of the acid arrow spell and uses the same attack bonus as your normal weapon attacks with the shortbow.

Proficiency with a Shortbow allows you to add your proficiency bonus to the attack roll for any attack you make with it.
 

 

This weapon has the following mastery property. To use this property, you must have a feature that lets you use it.
 

Vex. If you hit a creature with this weapon and deal damage to the creature, you have Advantage on your next attack roll against that creature before the end of your next turn.
