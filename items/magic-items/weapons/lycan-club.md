# Lycan Club

Weapon (club), rare 
 
 
Made from the claws, teeth, and bones of lycanthropes, this club is particularly effective against other lycanthropes or vampires. You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this club.
 

Additionally, on a successful attack against a lycanthrope, the target is forced back into its human form unless it succeeds on a DC 10 Charisma saving throw. Also, damage done to vampires with lycan weapons cannot be regenerated until the creature finishes a short rest.

Proficiency with a Club allows you to add your proficiency bonus to the attack roll for any attack you make with it.
 

 

This weapon has the following mastery property. To use this property, you must have a feature that lets you use it.
 

Slow. If you hit a creature with this weapon and deal damage to it, you can reduce its Speed by 10 feet until the start of your next turn. If the creature is hit more than once by weapons that have this property, the Speed reduction doesn’t exceed 10 feet.
