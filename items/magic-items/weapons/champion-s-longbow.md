# Champion’s Longbow

Weapon (longbow), uncommon 
 
 
Attack rolls made using this magic longbow can score a Critical Hit on a roll of 19 or 20 on the d20.
 

Crafting. Creating a Champion’s Longbow requires an Uncommon Workshop and the following Uncommon Components:
 
 

[Animus] Any
 

[Bones] Bones
 

[Fluid] Sap
 

[Hide] Plates
 

Proficiency with a Longbow allows you to add your proficiency bonus to the attack roll for any attack you make with it.
 

 

This weapon has the following mastery property. To use this property, you must have a feature that lets you use it.
 

Slow. If you hit a creature with this weapon and deal damage to it, you can reduce its Speed by 10 feet until the start of your next turn. If the creature is hit more than once by weapons that have this property, the Speed reduction doesn’t exceed 10 feet.
 

Monsters of Drakkenheim, pg. 383
