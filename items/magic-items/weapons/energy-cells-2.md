# Energy Cells, +2

Weapon (any ammunition), rare 
 
 
Futuristic firearms use Energy Cells that become depleted but could possibly be recharged with the proper equipment. You have a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this piece of magic ammunition. Once it hits a target, the ammunition is no longer magical.
