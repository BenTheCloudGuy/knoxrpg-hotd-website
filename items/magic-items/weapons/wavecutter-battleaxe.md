# Wavecutter Battleaxe

![Wavecutter Battleaxe](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/9445603.png)

Weapon (battleaxe), rare 
 
 
This battleaxe has a single pearl set at the center of its blade. You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. When you take the Attack action using this battleaxe, you can forgo one of your attacks to swing the battleaxe in front of you and speak its command word. When you do, the battleaxe emits a crashing wave of magical water. The wave is 10 feet tall and extends from you in a 30-foot cone, after which it spreads out across the ground in all directions, extinguishing unprotected flames in the area within 30 feet of it before the water vanishes. Each creature within the cone must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 4d8 bludgeoning damage and is knocked prone. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and isn't knocked prone. This property can't be used again until the next dawn.

Proficiency with a Battleaxe allows you to add your proficiency bonus to the attack roll for any attack you make with it.
 

 

This weapon has the following mastery property. To use this property, you must have a feature that lets you use it.
 

Topple. If you hit a creature with this weapon, you can force the creature to make a Constitution saving throw (DC 8 plus the ability modifier used to make the attack roll and your Proficiency Bonus). On a failed save, the creature has the Prone condition.
