# Wavecutter Handaxe

![Wavecutter Handaxe](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/9445605.png)

Weapon (handaxe), rare 
 
 
This handaxe has a single pearl set at the center of its blade. You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. When you take the Attack action using this handaxe, you can forgo one of your attacks to swing the handaxe in front of you and speak its command word. When you do, the handaxe emits a crashing wave of magical water. The wave is 10 feet tall and extends from you in a 30-foot cone, after which it spreads out across the ground in all directions, extinguishing unprotected flames in the area within 30 feet of it before the water vanishes. Each creature within the cone must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 4d8 bludgeoning damage and is knocked prone. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and isn't knocked prone. This property can't be used again until the next dawn.

Proficiency with a Handaxe allows you to add your proficiency bonus to the attack roll for any attack you make with it.
 

 

This weapon has the following mastery property. To use this property, you must have a feature that lets you use it.
 

Vex. If you hit a creature with this weapon and deal damage to the creature, you have Advantage on your next attack roll against that creature before the end of your next turn.
