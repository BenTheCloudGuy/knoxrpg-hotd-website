# Lycan Pike

Weapon (pike), rare 
 
 
Made from the claws, teeth, and bones of lycanthropes, this pike is particularly effective against other lycanthropes or vampires. You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this pike.
 

Additionally, on a successful attack against a lycanthrope, the target is forced back into its human form unless it succeeds on a DC 10 Charisma saving throw. Also, damage done to vampires with lycan weapons cannot be regenerated until the creature finishes a short rest.

Proficiency with a Pike allows you to add your proficiency bonus to the attack roll for any attack you make with it.
 

 

This weapon has the following mastery property. To use this property, you must have a feature that lets you use it.
 

Push. If you hit a creature with this weapon, you can push the creature up to 10 feet straight away from yourself if it is Large or smaller.
