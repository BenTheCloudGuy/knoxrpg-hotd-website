# Rat Tooth Dagger

![Rat Tooth Dagger](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/10185329.png)

Weapon (dagger), rare (requires attunement by a Rogue)
 
 
When you hit with an attack as part of an Attack action using this magic dagger and apply your Sneak Attack to the damage roll, you can make an additional attack using this dagger as part of the same action. If that attack hits and meets the criteria for your Sneak Attack, you can apply half your Sneak Attack dice (rounded down) to the attack's damage.
 

Crafting. Creating a Rat Tooth Dagger requires a Rare Workshop and the following Rare Components:
 
 

[Animus] Any Animus
 

[Fluid] Venom
 

[Hide] Bark
 

[Natural Weapons] Claws from a Ratling
 

Proficiency with a Dagger allows you to add your proficiency bonus to the attack roll for any attack you make with it.
 

 

This weapon has the following mastery property. To use this property, you must have a feature that lets you use it.
 

Nick. When you make the extra attack of the Light property, you can make it as part of the Attack action instead of as a Bonus Action. You can make this extra attack only once per turn.
 

Monsters of Drakkenheim, pg. 391
