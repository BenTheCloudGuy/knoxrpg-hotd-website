# Weapon of Certain Death, Boomerang

Weapon (boomerang), rare 
 
 
When you damage a creature with an attack using this magic boomerang, the target can’t regain hit points until the start of your next turn.

The boomerang is a ranged weapon, and any creature proficient with the javelin is also proficient with this weapon.
 

On a miss, a boomerang returns to the thrower's hand.

Explorer's Guide to Wildemount, pg. 270
