# Shortsword of Retribution

Weapon (shortsword), rare (requires attunement by a Paladin)
 
 
The metal of this shortsword is forged in the dust of celestials, with a hilt wrapped in fiend skin leather. The eye of a dragon is placed upon the hilt and polished in monster blood.
 

When you use this magic weapon to attack a creature and roll a 20 on the d20 for the attack roll, you can cast Divine Smite as part of the same action used to make the attack without expending a spell slot.
 

Crafting. Creating a Shortsword of Retribution requires a Rare Workshop and the following Rare Components:
 
 

[Animus] Any Animus
 

[Dust] Celestial Dust
 

[Fluid] Monster Blood
 

[Hide] Skin from a Fiend
 

[Organ] Eye from a Dragon
 

Proficiency with a Shortsword allows you to add your proficiency bonus to the attack roll for any attack you make with it.
 

 

This weapon has the following mastery property. To use this property, you must have a feature that lets you use it.
 

Vex. If you hit a creature with this weapon and deal damage to the creature, you have Advantage on your next attack roll against that creature before the end of your next turn.
