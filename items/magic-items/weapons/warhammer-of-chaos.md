# Warhammer of Chaos

![Warhammer of Chaos](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/10185348.png)

Weapon (warhammer), legendary (requires attunement)
 
 
A warhammer of chaos is crafted by melting down the magical plates of otherworldly constructs, infused with both the fluid and dust of elementals. The warhammer is then soaked in the grey matter of an aberrant brain. This steel hammer is fitted into a shaft made from fiend skin leather and the ribs of a dragon.
 

You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this warhammer. This warhammer has the Thrown property with a normal range of 30 feet and a long range of 90 feet. When you hit with an attack using this weapon, choose Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Poison, or Thunder. The hammer deals 2d8 extra damage of the chosen type.
 

When you hit with a ranged attack using this warhammer, if you roll the same number on two or more of the d8s, the hammer ricochets to a different target of your choice within 30 feet of the target. Make an attack roll against the new target, and make a new damage roll if the attack hits. After making this attack, the hammer flies back to your hand.
 

Crafting. Creating the Warhammer of Chaos requires a Legendary Workshop and the following Legendary Components:
 
 

[Animus] Any Animus
 

[Bones] Spine from a Dragon
 

[Dust] Elemental Dust
 

[Fluid] Elemental Fluid
 

[Hide] Plates from a Construct
 

[Hide] Skin from a Fiend
 

[Organ] Brain from an Aberration
 

Proficiency with a Warhammer allows you to add your proficiency bonus to the attack roll for any attack you make with it.
 

 

This weapon has the following mastery property. To use this property, you must have a feature that lets you use it.
 

Push. If you hit a creature with this weapon, you can push the creature up to 10 feet straight away from yourself if it is Large or smaller.
