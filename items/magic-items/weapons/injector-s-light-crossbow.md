# Injector’s Light Crossbow

Weapon (crossbow, light), rare (requires attunement)
 
 
This light crossbow is covered in metal plating, decorative and refined pointed metal tips along an oak wood body, and foregrip. The barrel and limb are reinforced with the steel plates from a construct, with a stained leather grip made from a monster's hide. Its hair strung bowstring and leather fasteners hold a series of syringes in place. The crossbow's stock has a series of various tubes extruding from it that connect to an alchemical chamber on the side of the weapon. A mechanical wheel feeds the darts into place upon filling them, auto-loading the crossbow for easy firing.
 

You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic light crossbow. This crossbow ignores the Loading property, and you don't need a free hand to load a piece of ammunition into it.
 

Once per turn when you hit a creature with a ranged attack using this crossbow, you can apply one of the following poisons.
 

Virulent. The target takes an extra 2d6 Poison damage.
 

Weakening. The target must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, it is Weakened until the start of your next turn.
 

Immobilizing. The target must make a DC 15 Strength saving throw. On a failed save, it is Immobilized until the start of your next turn.
 

Crafting. Creating an Injector’s Light Crossbow requires a Rare Workshop and the following Rare Components:
 
 

[Animus] Any Animus
 

[Fluid] Chemical Solution
 

[Hair] Hair
 

[Hide] Plates
 

[Hide] Skin
 

 Needles from an [monsters]Injector
 

Proficiency with a Light Crossbow allows you to add your proficiency bonus to the attack roll for any attack you make with it.
 

 

This weapon has the following mastery property. To use this property, you must have a feature that lets you use it.
 

Slow. If you hit a creature with this weapon and deal damage to it, you can reduce its Speed by 10 feet until the start of your next turn. If the creature is hit more than once by weapons that have this property, the Speed reduction doesn’t exceed 10 feet.
