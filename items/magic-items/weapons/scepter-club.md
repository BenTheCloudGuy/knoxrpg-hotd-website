# Scepter Club

Weapon (club), rare (requires attunement)
 
 
By speaking this club’s command word as a bonus action, you activate a harsh purple light. While this light shines, you have advantage on Charisma (Intimidation) checks made to interact with beasts, dragons, fey, giants, humanoids, and monstrosities.
 

In addition, any foe or object you strike with a scepter club glows, shedding dim light in a 10-foot radius. Any attack roll against an affected creature or object has advantage if the attacker can see it, and the affected creature or object can’t benefit from being invisible.

Proficiency with a Club allows you to add your proficiency bonus to the attack roll for any attack you make with it.
 

 

This weapon has the following mastery property. To use this property, you must have a feature that lets you use it.
 

Slow. If you hit a creature with this weapon and deal damage to it, you can reduce its Speed by 10 feet until the start of your next turn. If the creature is hit more than once by weapons that have this property, the Speed reduction doesn’t exceed 10 feet.
