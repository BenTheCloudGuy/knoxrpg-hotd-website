# Longbow of the Cobra

![Longbow of the Cobra](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/9445273.png)

Weapon (longbow), rare (requires attunement)
 
 
This metallic longbow resembles the shape of a mighty cobra and is covered in spots of patina. When you fire an arrow from the longbow, you can choose to coat the arrow in a thin layer of acid. A target hit with the acid-coated arrow takes an extra 1d4 acid damage. Hit or miss, a nonmagical coated arrow is destroyed after the attack.
 

In addition, the longbow has 4 charges and regains 1d4 expended charges daily at dawn. When you take the Attack action using this longbow, you can expend 1 of its charges to replace one of your weapon attacks with a special attack. This special attack duplicates the effect of the acid arrow spell and uses the same attack bonus as your normal weapon attacks with the longbow.

Proficiency with a Longbow allows you to add your proficiency bonus to the attack roll for any attack you make with it.
 

 

This weapon has the following mastery property. To use this property, you must have a feature that lets you use it.
 

Slow. If you hit a creature with this weapon and deal damage to it, you can reduce its Speed by 10 feet until the start of your next turn. If the creature is hit more than once by weapons that have this property, the Speed reduction doesn’t exceed 10 feet.
