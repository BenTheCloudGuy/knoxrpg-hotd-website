# Jinxed Rum

![Jinxed Rum](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/10241357.png)

Potion, uncommon 
 
 
When you drink this brightly colored rum, your luck takes a strange turn for 1 hour. For the duration, when you make a D20 Test, roll 1d4. On a 2-4, add the number to your roll. On a 1, roll an extra d20 and use the lowest roll for the test.
 

This potion is stored in a glass rum bottle marked with crossbones.
 

Heightened Potency. Roll 1d6 instead of 1d4.

The Crooked Moon Part One: Player Options & Campaign Setting, pg. 615
