# Draught of the Red Hunger

![Draught of the Red Hunger](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/10241334.png)

Potion, legendary 
 
 
When you drink this potion, you gain a ravenous thirst for blood and gain the effect of the Vampiric Touch spell (level 6 version, no Concentration required) for 1 minute. You can repeat the attack on subsequent turns as a Bonus Action instead of a Magic action.
 

This liquid is a shimmering red deeper and more intense than natural blood.
 

Heightened Potency. For the duration, as a Magic action, you can gain the effects of the Gaseous Form spell (no Concentration required) while you aren’t in sunlight. You can also revert to your normal form as a Magic action.

The Crooked Moon Part One: Player Options & Campaign Setting, pg. 614
