# Sight of Sin

![Sight of Sin](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/10241395.png)

Potion, rare 
 
 
When you drink this potion, you gain the effect of a Detect Magic spell (no Concentration required) for 10 minutes, and during that time, you have Advantage on Wisdom (Insight) checks to discern hidden motives or deception.
 

This potion is stored in a round glass flask. An iron band wraps around the bottle, ornamented with a goat’s skull.
 

Heightened Potency. For the duration, you also gain the effect of the See Invisibility spell.

The Crooked Moon Part One: Player Options & Campaign Setting, pg. 616
