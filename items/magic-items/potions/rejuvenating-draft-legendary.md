# Rejuvenating Draft (Legendary)

![Rejuvenating Draft (Legendary)](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/9445402.png)

Potion, legendary 
 
 
When you drink this potion, you regain all spent Hit Dice. When you drink the legendary variant of a rejuvenating draft, your age is reduced by 1d4 years (to a minimum of 1 year old).

The Griffon’s Saddlebag: Book Two, pg. 144
