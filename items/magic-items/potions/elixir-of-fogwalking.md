# Elixir of Fogwalking

![Elixir of Fogwalking](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/10241335.png)

Potion, rare 
 
 
When you drink this potion, your breath billows out in clouds of fog. For the next 10 minutes, fog and smoke don’t hinder your vision, you can cast Fog Cloud up to three times (no Concentration required), and you can cast Misty Step up to three times as long as your teleportation begins or ends in an area of fog.
 

This bottle has a base like cobblestone. The glass widens near the top before narrowing again into an iron cap, resembling a lantern. The liquid inside is a murky gray.
 

Heightened Potency. The potion’s duration becomes 1 hour, and when you use this potion to cast Misty Step, you also gain Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage until the start of your next turn.

The Crooked Moon Part One: Player Options & Campaign Setting, pg. 614
