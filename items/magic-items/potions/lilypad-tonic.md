# Lilypad Tonic

![Lilypad Tonic](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/10241362.png)

Potion, common 
 
 
When you drink this potion, you gain the effect of the Polymorph spell (no Concentration required), turning you into a Frog.
 

This potion is found in a clay jar shaped and painted like a large frog, with a cork stopper in its back.
 

Heightened Potency. The potion has six doses instead of one.

The Crooked Moon Part One: Player Options & Campaign Setting, pg. 615
