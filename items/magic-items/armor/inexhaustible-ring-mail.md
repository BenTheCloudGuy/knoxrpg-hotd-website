# Inexhaustible Ring Mail

![Inexhaustible Ring Mail](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/10185188.png)

Armor (ring mail), very rare (requires attunement by a Fighter)
 
 
This ring mail is made from fey leathers and magical plates, infused with the chemical solutions found in some constructs and empowered with the heart of a gorgon.
 

While attuned to this ring mail, you gain an additional use of your Second Wind class feature. In addition, when you use your Second Wind, you regain an additional 3d6 Hit Points and can remove one of the following conditions from yourself: Blinded, Charmed, Dazed, Deafened, Frightened, Immobilized, Poisoned, Slowed, or Staggered.
 

Crafting. Creating Inexhaustible Ring Mail requires a Very Rare Workshop and the following Very Rare Components:
 
 

[Animus] Any Animus
 

[Fluid] Chemical solution from a Construct
 

[Hide] Skin from a Fey
 

[Hide] Plates
 

 Heart from a [monsters]Gorgon
 

This armor is leather armor with heavy rings sewn into it. The rings help reinforce the armor against blows from swords and axes. Ring mail is inferior to chain mail, and it's usually worn only by those who can't afford better armor.

Monsters of Drakkenheim, pg. 388
