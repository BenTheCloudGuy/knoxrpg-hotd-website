# Ebony Beetle Armor (Hide)

![Ebony Beetle Armor (Hide)](../images/ebony-beetle-armor.png)

Armor (hide), rare 
 
 
This glossy black hide armor is made from enchanted carrion beetle chitin by the darakhul. If the armor normally imposes disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks or has a Strength requirement, the ebony beetle version of the armor doesn’t. While wearing the armor, you are immune to slashing damage. It is half the weight of comparable metal armor.

This crude armor consists of thick furs and pelts.

Book of Ebon Tides, pg. 197
