# Umbral Armor (Studded Leather)

Armor (studded leather), rare 
 
 
This studded leather armor is a dull gray, marked with flecks of gold or silver and sometimes filigreed in luminous purple swirls. While wearing this studded leather armor, you emit a trail of shadows that obscures your passage, wiping away tracks and hiding your movement. All attempts to track you are made with disadvantage, and all ranged attacks against you are also made with disadvantage. However, bright light (such as from sunlight or the daylight spell) negates both effects and makes this armor easy to see. In these conditions, all attacks made against you gain a +2 to the attack roll.

Made from tough but flexible leather, studded leather is reinforced with close-set rivets or spikes.

Book of Ebon Tides, pg. 199
