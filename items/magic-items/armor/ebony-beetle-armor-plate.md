# Ebony Beetle Armor (Plate)

![Ebony Beetle Armor (Plate)](../images/ebony-beetle-armor.png)

Armor (plate), rare 
 
 
This glossy black plate armor is made from enchanted carrion beetle chitin by the darakhul. If the armor normally imposes disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks or has a Strength requirement, the ebony beetle version of the armor doesn’t. While wearing the armor, you are immune to slashing damage. It is half the weight of comparable metal armor.

Plate consists of shaped, interlocking metal plates to cover the entire body. A suit of plate includes gauntlets, heavy leather boots, a visored helmet, and thick layers of padding underneath the armor. Buckles and straps distribute the weight over the body.

Book of Ebon Tides, pg. 197
