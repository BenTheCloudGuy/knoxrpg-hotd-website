# Hag-Stitched Troll Armor (Leather)

![Hag-Stitched Troll Armor (Leather)](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/10185173.png)

Armor (leather), rare (requires attunement)
 
 
You gain a +1 bonus to Armor Class while wearing this magic leather armor.
 

This magic leather armor has 50 charges. It regains all expended charges each day at dawn. While wearing this armor and attuned to it, at the start of your turn you can expend between 1 and 10 charges from the armor (no action required). You regain 1 Hit Point for each charge expended in this way. If you take Acid or Fire damage while wearing this armor, it loses 1 charge for each point of Acid or Fire damage taken.
 

Crafting. Creating Hag-Stitched Troll Armor requires a Rare Workshop and the following Rare Components:
 
 

[Animus] Animus from a Giant
 

[Bones] Skulls of any two Trolls
 

[Fluid] Monster Blood from any Troll
 

[Fur] Stitches
 

[Hide] Skin of any Troll
 

The breastplate and shoulder protectors of this armor are made of leather that has been stiffened by being boiled in oil. The rest of the armor is made of softer and more flexible materials.

Monsters of Drakkenheim, pg. 387
