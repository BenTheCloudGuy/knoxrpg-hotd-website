# Frozen Leather Armor

![Frozen Leather Armor](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/10185154.png)

Armor (leather), rare (requires attunement)
 
 
This leather armor channels cold energy through carefully manufactured veins of elemental fluid that run along the inside of its frame. Elemental dust is mixed with the gold in its forming, and contaminated ichor is used as a catalyst to magnify the magical effects. The animus gem placed centrally upon it must be infused with the energy of a frost giant.
 

While attuned to this leather armor you have Resistance to Cold damage and are Immune to the Frozen condition.
 

This magical leather armor has six charges, and regains all expended charges each day at dawn. When you are hit by a melee attack, you can use your Reaction and expend 1 charge from the armor to freeze your attacker in pace. The creature must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or be Frozen (DC 15).
 

Crafting. Creating Frozen Leather Armor requires a Rare Workshop and the following Rare Components:
 
 

 Animus from a [monsters]Frost Giant
 

[Dust] Elemental Dust
 

[Fluid] Contaminated Ichor
 

[Fluid] Elemental Fluid
 

The breastplate and shoulder protectors of this armor are made of leather that has been stiffened by being boiled in oil. The rest of the armor is made of softer and more flexible materials.
