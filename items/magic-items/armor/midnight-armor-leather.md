# Midnight Armor (Leather)

Armor (leather), very rare (requires attunement)
 
 
Spun of pure shadow and invisible in sunlight, armor of this type is typically of shadow goblin or derro construction. While wearing it, you gain a +1 bonus to AC and resistance to radiant damage. In addition, you have advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.

The breastplate and shoulder protectors of this armor are made of leather that has been stiffened by being boiled in oil. The rest of the armor is made of softer and more flexible materials.

Book of Ebon Tides, pg. 198
