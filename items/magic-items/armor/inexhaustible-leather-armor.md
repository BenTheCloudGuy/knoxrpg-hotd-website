# Inexhaustible Leather Armor

![Inexhaustible Leather Armor](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/10185185.png)

Armor (leather), very rare (requires attunement by a Fighter)
 
 
This leather armor is made from fey leathers and magical plates, infused with the chemical solutions found in some constructs and empowered with the heart of a gorgon.
 

While attuned to this leather armor, you gain an additional use of your Second Wind class feature. In addition, when you use your Second Wind, you regain an additional 3d6 Hit Points and can remove one of the following conditions from yourself: Blinded, Charmed, Dazed, Deafened, Frightened, Immobilized, Poisoned, Slowed, or Staggered.
 

Crafting. Creating Inexhaustible Leather Armor requires a Very Rare Workshop and the following Very Rare Components:
 
 

[Animus] Any Animus
 

[Fluid] Chemical solution from a Construct
 

[Hide] Skin from a Fey
 

[Hide] Plates
 

 Heart from a [monsters]Gorgon
 

The breastplate and shoulder protectors of this armor are made of leather that has been stiffened by being boiled in oil. The rest of the armor is made of softer and more flexible materials.

Monsters of Drakkenheim, pg. 388
