# Moonstone Banded Armor (Chain Mail)

Armor (chain mail), rare (requires attunement)
 
 
This chain mail armor is made of woven moonlight by the shadow goblins. It weighs half as much as comparable armor and always glows with a gentle radiance. While wearing this armor, you have advantage on saving throws against spells and effects involving radiant, cold, or necrotic damage.

Made of interlocking metal rings, chain mail includes a layer of quilted fabric worn underneath the mail to prevent chafing and to cushion the impact of blows. The suit includes gauntlets.

Book of Ebon Tides, pg. 198
