# Ghost Armor (Padded)

Armor (padded), rare (requires attunement)
 
 
While wearing this padded armor, you can speak its command word as a bonus action to move incorporeally for 1 round. You can move through other creatures and objects as if they were difficult terrain. You take 5 (1d10) force damage if you end your turn inside an object. You can use this ability a number of rounds per day equal to your level.

Padded armor consists of quilted layers of cloth and batting.

Book of Ebon Tides, pg. 198
