# Frozen Ring Mail

![Frozen Ring Mail](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/10185157.png)

Armor (ring mail), rare (requires attunement)
 
 
This ring mail channels cold energy through carefully manufactured veins of elemental fluid that run along the inside of its frame. Elemental dust is mixed with the gold in its forming, and contaminated ichor is used as a catalyst to magnify the magical effects. The animus gem placed centrally upon it must be infused with the energy of a frost giant.
 

While attuned to this ring mail you have Resistance to Cold damage and are Immune to the Frozen condition.
 

This magical ring mail has six charges, and regains all expended charges each day at dawn. When you are hit by a melee attack, you can use your Reaction and expend 1 charge from the armor to freeze your attacker in pace. The creature must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or be Frozen (DC 15).
 

Crafting. Creating Frozen Ring Mail requires a Rare Workshop and the following Rare Components:
 
 

 Animus from a [monsters]Frost Giant
 

[Dust] Elemental Dust
 

[Fluid] Contaminated Ichor
 

[Fluid] Elemental Fluid
 

This armor is leather armor with heavy rings sewn into it. The rings help reinforce the armor against blows from swords and axes. Ring mail is inferior to chain mail, and it's usually worn only by those who can't afford better armor.

Monsters of Drakkenheim, pg. 386
