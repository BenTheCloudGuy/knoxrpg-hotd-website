# Ebony Beetle Armor (Chain Mail)

![Ebony Beetle Armor (Chain Mail)](../images/ebony-beetle-armor.png)

Armor (chain mail), rare 
 
 
This glossy black chain mail armor is made from enchanted carrion beetle chitin by the darakhul. If the armor normally imposes disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks or has a Strength requirement, the ebony beetle version of the armor doesn’t. While wearing the armor, you are immune to slashing damage. It is half the weight of comparable metal armor.

Made of interlocking metal rings, chain mail includes a layer of quilted fabric worn underneath the mail to prevent chafing and to cushion the impact of blows. The suit includes gauntlets.

Book of Ebon Tides, pg. 197
