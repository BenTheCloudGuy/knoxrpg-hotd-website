# Ebony Beetle Armor (Breastplate)

![Ebony Beetle Armor (Breastplate)](../images/ebony-beetle-armor.png)

Armor (breastplate), rare 
 
 
This glossy black breastplate is made from enchanted carrion beetle chitin by the darakhul. If the armor normally imposes disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks or has a Strength requirement, the ebony beetle version of the armor doesn’t. While wearing the armor, you are immune to slashing damage. It is half the weight of comparable metal armor.

This armor consists of a fitted metal chest piece worn with supple leather. Although it leaves the legs and arms relatively unprotected, this armor provides good protection for the wearer's vital organs while leaving the wearer relatively unencumbered.

Book of Ebon Tides, pg. 197
