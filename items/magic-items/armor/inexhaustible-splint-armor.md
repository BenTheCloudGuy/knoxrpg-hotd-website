# Inexhaustible Splint Armor

![Inexhaustible Splint Armor](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/10185190.png)

Armor (splint), very rare (requires attunement by a Fighter)
 
 
This splint armor is made from fey leathers and magical plates, infused with the chemical solutions found in some constructs and empowered with the heart of a gorgon.
 

While attuned to this splint armor, you gain an additional use of your Second Wind class feature. In addition, when you use your Second Wind, you regain an additional 3d6 Hit Points and can remove one of the following conditions from yourself: Blinded, Charmed, Dazed, Deafened, Frightened, Immobilized, Poisoned, Slowed, or Staggered.
 

Crafting. Creating Inexhaustible Splint Armor requires a Very Rare Workshop and the following Very Rare Components:
 
 

[Animus] Any Animus
 

[Fluid] Chemical solution from a Construct
 

[Hide] Skin from a Fey
 

[Hide] Plates
 

 Heart from a [monsters]Gorgon
 

This armor is made of narrow vertical strips of metal riveted to a backing of leather that is worn over cloth padding. Flexible chain mail protects the joints.

Monsters of Drakkenheim, pg. 388
