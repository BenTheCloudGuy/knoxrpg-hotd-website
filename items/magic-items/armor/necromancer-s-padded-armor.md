# Necromancer’s Padded Armor

![Necromancer’s Padded Armor](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/10185322.png)

Armor (padded), rare (requires attunement by a spellcaster)
 
 
This suit of padded armor is constructed from the bones of the dead and outfitted over black woven fabric. The armor has two skulls as pauldrons upon the shoulders, a reinforced spine, and a rib cage that covers the front, and the entire structure is ornately decorated with the fangs of fallen beasts. The bones are magically imbued with otherworldly energy, powered by a powerful undead animus.
 

While attuned to this padded armor, you are proficient with this armor even if you lack proficiency with light armor.
 

While you are within 30 feet of an Undead creature under your control, you gain Resistance to all damage except Radiant. However, each time you take damage, choose an Undead creature under your control within 30 feet of you. That creature takes the same amount of damage, which can't be reduced or prevented in any way.
 

Crafting. Creating Necromancer’s Padded Armor requires a Rare Workshop and the following Rare Components:
 
 

[Animus] Undead Animus
 

[Bones] Ribs
 

[Bones] Skull
 

[Bones] Spine
 

[Fluid] Contaminated Ichor
 

[Natural Weapon] Teeth
 

Padded armor consists of quilted layers of cloth and batting.

Monsters of Drakkenheim, pg. 390
