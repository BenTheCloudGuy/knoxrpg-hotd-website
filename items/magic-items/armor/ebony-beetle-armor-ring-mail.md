# Ebony Beetle Armor (Ring Mail)

![Ebony Beetle Armor (Ring Mail)](../images/ebony-beetle-armor.png)

Armor (ring mail), rare 
 
 
This glossy black ring mail armor is made from enchanted carrion beetle chitin by the darakhul. If the armor normally imposes disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks or has a Strength requirement, the ebony beetle version of the armor doesn’t. While wearing the armor, you are immune to slashing damage. It is half the weight of comparable metal armor.

This armor is leather armor with heavy rings sewn into it. The rings help reinforce the armor against blows from swords and axes. Ring mail is inferior to chain mail, and it's usually worn only by those who can't afford better armor.

Book of Ebon Tides, pg. 197
