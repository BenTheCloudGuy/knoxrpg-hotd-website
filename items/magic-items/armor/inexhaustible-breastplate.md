# Inexhaustible Breastplate

![Inexhaustible Breastplate](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/10185180.png)

Armor (breastplate), very rare (requires attunement by a Fighter)
 
 
This breastplate is made from fey leathers and magical plates, infused with the chemical solutions found in some constructs and empowered with the heart of a gorgon.
 

While attuned to this breastplate, you gain an additional use of your Second Wind class feature. In addition, when you use your Second Wind, you regain an additional 3d6 Hit Points and can remove one of the following conditions from yourself: Blinded, Charmed, Dazed, Deafened, Frightened, Immobilized, Poisoned, Slowed, or Staggered.
 

Crafting. Creating Inexhaustible Breastplate requires a Very Rare Workshop and the following Very Rare Components:
 
 

[Animus] Any Animus
 

[Fluid] Chemical solution from a Construct
 

[Hide] Skin from a Fey
 

[Hide] Plates
 

 Heart from a [monsters]Gorgon
 

This armor consists of a fitted metal chest piece worn with supple leather. Although it leaves the legs and arms relatively unprotected, this armor provides good protection for the wearer's vital organs while leaving the wearer relatively unencumbered.

Monsters of Drakkenheim, pg. 388
