# Shield of Marius Renathyr

![Shield of Marius Renathyr](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/10241391.png)

Armor (shield), varies (requires attunement)
 
 
Crafted from polished gold and white enamel and adorned with an intricate design of a rose encircled by thorny vines, this Shield was once owned by Marius Renathyr, a knight-errant destined for a terrible fate. While holding this Shield, you gain a bonus to AC determined by the Shield’s rarity, in addition to the Shield’s normal bonus to AC, and you can use the following properties.
 

Valorous Guard (Rare+). When a creature within 5 feet of you is hit by an attack roll, you can take a Reaction to add 2 + the Shield’s bonus to the creature’s AC, potentially causing the attack to miss. When a creature within 5 feet of you fails a Dexterity saving throw, you can take a Reaction to add 2 + the Shield’s bonus to the creature’s total, potentially causing it to succeed.
 

Honor of the Vanguard (Very Rare+). When you roll Initiative and an ally you can see is within 30 feet of an enemy, you can teleport to an unoccupied space within 5 feet of that ally and gain Temporary Hit Points equal to 5 times the Shield’s bonus.
 

Righteous Wrath (Very Rare+). When you use the Valorous Guard property to cause an attack to miss, the next time you hit that attacker with a weapon or Unarmed Strike before the end of your next turn, it takes an extra 1d8 (Legendary: 3d8) Radiant damage.
 

As Terrible as the Dawn (Legendary). You can cast Flame Strike (save DC 19) from the Shield. If you are in the spell’s area, you regain 5d6 Hit Points instead of taking damage. Once the spell is cast from the Shield, it can’t be cast from it again until the next dusk.
