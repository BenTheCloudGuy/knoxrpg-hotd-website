# Frozen Chain Shirt

![Frozen Chain Shirt](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/10185151.png)

Armor (chain shirt), rare (requires attunement)
 
 
This chain shirt channels cold energy through carefully manufactured veins of elemental fluid that run along the inside of its frame. Elemental dust is mixed with the gold in its forming, and contaminated ichor is used as a catalyst to magnify the magical effects. The animus gem placed centrally upon it must be infused with the energy of a frost giant.
 

While attuned to this chain shirt you have Resistance to Cold damage and are Immune to the Frozen condition.
 

This magical chain shirt has six charges, and regains all expended charges each day at dawn. When you are hit by a melee attack, you can use your Reaction and expend 1 charge from the armor to freeze your attacker in pace. The creature must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or be Frozen (DC 15).
 

Crafting. Creating Frozen Chain Shirt requires a Rare Workshop and the following Rare Components:
 
 

 Animus from a [monsters]Frost Giant
 

[Dust] Elemental Dust
 

[Fluid] Contaminated Ichor
 

[Fluid] Elemental Fluid
 

Made of interlocking metal rings, a chain shirt is worn between layers of clothing or leather. This armor offers modest protection to the wearer's upper body and allows the sound of the rings rubbing against one another to be muffled by outer layers.
