# Moonstone Banded Armor (Plate)

Armor (plate), rare (requires attunement)
 
 
This plate armor is made of woven moonlight by the shadow goblins. It weighs half as much as comparable armor and always glows with a gentle radiance. While wearing this armor, you have advantage on saving throws against spells and effects involving radiant, cold, or necrotic damage.

Plate consists of shaped, interlocking metal plates to cover the entire body. A suit of plate includes gauntlets, heavy leather boots, a visored helmet, and thick layers of padding underneath the armor. Buckles and straps distribute the weight over the body.

Book of Ebon Tides, pg. 198
