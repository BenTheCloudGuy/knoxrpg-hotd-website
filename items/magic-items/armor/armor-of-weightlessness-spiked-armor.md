# Armor of Weightlessness, Spiked Armor

Armor (spiked armor), uncommon (requires attunement)
 
 
This armor has 5 charges. While you wear it, you can use a bonus action to expend 1 or more charges to cast one of the following spells from the armor, targeting yourself: Jump (1 charge) or Levitate (2 charges).
 

This armor regains 1d4 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn.

Spiked armor is a rare type of medium armor made by dwarves. It consists of a leather coat and leggings covered with spikes that are usually made of metal.

The Book of Many Things, pg. 65
