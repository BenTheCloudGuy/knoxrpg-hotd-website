# Ebony Beetle Armor (Splint)

![Ebony Beetle Armor (Splint)](../images/ebony-beetle-armor.png)

Armor (splint), rare 
 
 
This glossy black splint armor is made from enchanted carrion beetle chitin by the darakhul. If the armor normally imposes disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks or has a Strength requirement, the ebony beetle version of the armor doesn’t. While wearing the armor, you are immune to slashing damage. It is half the weight of comparable metal armor.

This armor is made of narrow vertical strips of metal riveted to a backing of leather that is worn over cloth padding. Flexible chain mail protects the joints.

Book of Ebon Tides, pg. 197
