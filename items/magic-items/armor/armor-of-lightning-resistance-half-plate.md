# Armor of Lightning Resistance, Half Plate

![Armor of Lightning Resistance, Half Plate](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/5015.jpeg)

Armor (half plate), rare (requires attunement)
 
 
You have Resistance to Lightning damage while you wear this armor.

Half plate consists of shaped metal plates that cover most of the wearer's body. It does not include leg protection beyond simple greaves that are attached with leather straps.
