# Armor of Poison Resistance, Splint

![Armor of Poison Resistance, Splint](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/5046.jpeg)

Armor (splint), rare (requires attunement)
 
 
You have Resistance to Poison damage while you wear this armor.

This armor is made of narrow vertical strips of metal riveted to a backing of leather that is worn over cloth padding. Flexible chain mail protects the joints.
