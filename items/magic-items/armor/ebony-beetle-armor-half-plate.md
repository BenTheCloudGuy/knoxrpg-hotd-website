# Ebony Beetle Armor (Half Plate)

![Ebony Beetle Armor (Half Plate)](../images/ebony-beetle-armor.png)

Armor (half plate), rare 
 
 
This glossy black half plate armor is made from enchanted carrion beetle chitin by the darakhul. If the armor normally imposes disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks or has a Strength requirement, the ebony beetle version of the armor doesn’t. While wearing the armor, you are immune to slashing damage. It is half the weight of comparable metal armor.

Half plate consists of shaped metal plates that cover most of the wearer's body. It does not include leg protection beyond simple greaves that are attached with leather straps.

Book of Ebon Tides, pg. 197
