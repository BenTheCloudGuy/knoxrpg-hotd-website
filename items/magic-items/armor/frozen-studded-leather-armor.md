# Frozen Studded Leather Armor

![Frozen Studded Leather Armor](https://cloudgeekcusgaming01.blob.core.windows.net/images/magic-items/10185160.png)

Armor (studded leather), rare (requires attunement)
 
 
This studded leather armor channels cold energy through carefully manufactured veins of elemental fluid that run along the inside of its frame. Elemental dust is mixed with the gold in its forming, and contaminated ichor is used as a catalyst to magnify the magical effects. The animus gem placed centrally upon it must be infused with the energy of a frost giant.
 

While attuned to this studded leather armor you have Resistance to Cold damage and are Immune to the Frozen condition.
 

This magical studded leather armor has six charges, and regains all expended charges each day at dawn. When you are hit by a melee attack, you can use your Reaction and expend 1 charge from the armor to freeze your attacker in pace. The creature must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or be Frozen (DC 15).
 

Crafting. Creating Frozen Studded Leather Armor requires a Rare Workshop and the following Rare Components:
 
 

 Animus from a [monsters]Frost Giant
 

[Dust] Elemental Dust
 

[Fluid] Contaminated Ichor
 

[Fluid] Elemental Fluid
 

Made from tough but flexible leather, studded leather is reinforced with close-set rivets or spikes.

Monsters of Drakkenheim, pg. 386
