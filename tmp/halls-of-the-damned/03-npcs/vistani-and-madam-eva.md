As the laughter dies and the bonfire burns low, a sudden hush falls over the camp.

“Enough wine and wonder. Bring them to me.”
The crowd parts wordlessly. The party is led to Madam Eva’s great tent — crimson, gold, and black,

Read-Aloud:
Inside, smoke swirls in slow spirals beneath low lanterns. The tent feels larger within than
without — the walls shifting and distant, like a dream. A table sits at the center, draped in black
silk embroidered with symbols of moons and eyes.
Behind it, Madam Eva sits — a withered woman wrapped in shawls and shadow. Her eyes gleam
with unnatural light. When she speaks, her voice is low and rough as the rustle of old paper.
“Heroes from beyond the mists… I have waited for you. Sit. Let fate speak.”
The tent is heavy with incense and shadow. Madam Eva shuffles the Tarokka deck with hands that
tremble only with age, not hesitation. Her eyes flash in the firelight as she lays the first card down
upon the table.

“The cards do not lie. They see what was, what is, and what might yet be. Sit, and listen. The
mists part only once.”
Eva shuffles the Tarokka deck with slow, deliberate motions, the cards whispering like dry

She turns it, revealing The Key

lost.”

Overview:
The Vistani are nomadic wanderers, mystics, and fortune-tellers who move freely through the mists that
trap all others within Barovia. Unlike the land’s other inhabitants, the Vistani are uniquely capable of
entering and leaving Barovia at will. They are known for their colorful clothing, decorated wagons, and
enigmatic culture.
Notable Characteristics:
Nomadic Lifestyle: They travel in brightly colored wagons and often set up camp outside Barovian
villages.
- Mysticism & Fortune Telling: Renowned for their accurate, though cryptic, tarot-like card readings
using the Tarokka Deck, they often serve as seers or guides.
- Unique Abilities: They can navigate the deadly Mists of Ravenloft without becoming trapped, a
skill tied to their ancient pact with Strahd himself.
- Relationship with Strahd:
The Vistani maintain a complex relationship with Strahd von Zarovich. Centuries ago, they rescued
Strahd after a great battle and earned his gratitude and protection. He grants them special privileges,
freedom to travel, and protection from harm within his domain.
Notable Figures:
Madam Eva: The most famous and powerful Vistani seer. She uses the Tarokka cards to foresee
events and guide adventurers towards their destinies within Barovia.
- Ezmerelda d’Avenir: A notable Vistani monster-hunter who broke away from traditional Vistani
culture, aligning herself instead with those who fight against Strahd’s darkness.
- The Vistani play an integral role in the narrative of Barovia, acting as guides, mystics, allies, and
occasionally foes, depending upon the choices adventurers make during their time within Strahd’s
cursed realm
