Spawn vs. Vampyr
Vampire Spawn (Servant)
Created when a vampire feeds until near death, but without the ritual they become monsters.	•
They are stripped of willpower: lesser beings, unable to resist orders, shadows of their former selves.	•
Most villagers and adventurers who fall to Strahd become this — enslaved thralls.	•
Vampyr (True Vampire)
Requires a ritual of choice and corruption, not mere feeding.	•
The vampire must:
Feed without killing, binding victim and master through repeated bites.	○
Offer the Dark Gift — a blood ritual performed under the gaze of the Dark Powers (often at a place of unholy significance, like Castle
Ravenloft’s crypts).
  - The victim must drink willingly of the vampire’s blood in return. This acceptance is key — without consent, the transformation fails.	○
The victim dies, then rises at midnight as a Vampyr — a full vampire, independent but forever tied to their creator.	○
Unlike spawn, a Vampyr retains their will, memories, and personality, though twisted by hunger and undeath.	○
