Traits
Duty-Bound. The foresworn is bound to enacting a singular duty it pledged itself to in life. While this duty remains
incomplete, whenever the foresworn is destroyed, it reforms at its previous location after 1d6 days, with all its hit points
restored.
Incorporeal Movement. The foresworn can move through creatures and objects as if they were difficult terrain. It takes 5
(1d10) force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.
Unusual Nature. The foresworn doesn’t require air, food, drink, or sleep.
Actions
Multiattack. The foresworn makes three Spectral Polearm attacks.
Spectral Polearm. Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
Hit: 12 (2d6 + 5) force damage.
Spellcasting. The foresworn casts one of the following spells, requiring no material components and using Wisdom as the
spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15).
- 2/day each: command, detect evil and good
- 1/day: wall of force
Bonus Actions
Wail of the Condemned (Recharge 5–6): The foresworn unleashes a soul-rending scream steeped in centuries of hatred.
Each creature of the foresworn’s choice within 20 feet that can hear it must make a DC 16 Constitution saving throw. On a
failed save, a creature takes 4d8+4 necrotic damage and has Disadvantage on all Attacks and Saves for 1d4 minutes (no
additional saves). On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage and only has disadvantage for 1 turn. Undead
and constructs automatically succeed on this saving throw.
