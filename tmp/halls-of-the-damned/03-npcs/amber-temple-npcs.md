Prince Strahd von Zarovich: The First Vampire, Darklord of
Barovia
Early Life: The Conqueror of Barovia
Strahd von Zarovich was born into the royal family of a mighty kingdom in a distant land—an ancient
empire lost to time. As a prince, Strahd was a brilliant tactician and a ruthless general. He spent most of
his life at war, carving out his domain with blood and steel. His victories were legendary, yet with each
conquest, his heart grew colder, and his obsession with legacy deeper.
In his middle age, weary of endless battles, Strahd claimed a land of misty mountains and dark forests—
Barovia—as his reward. He drove out the native peoples, built the towering Castle Ravenloft atop the
cliffs of Mount Ghakis, and named the land after his mother, Queen Ravenovia.
The Pact and the Curse
Strahd’s decline began not on the battlefield, but in his own halls.
When his younger brother Sergei arrived in Barovia, the boy's charm, warmth, and love for life captured
the heart of a local woman: Tatyana Federovna, a beautiful noblewoman from the valley. Strahd,
already aged and jealous of Sergei’s youth, also fell in love with her. That envy festered into obsession.
Desperate to reclaim his youth and win Tatyana, Strahd sought out dark powers beyond mortal ken. He
discovered the Amber Temple, a place where ancient and forbidden entities known as the Dark Powers
offered forbidden gifts. There, he made a pact—trading his soul and mortality for eternal youth and
dominion over death.
Strahd returned on Sergei’s wedding day and murdered his brother, attempting to claim Tatyana by
force. But Tatyana, horrified by the bloodshed, fled and cast herself from the cliffs of Ravenloft. Her
body was never recovered.
That night, Strahd’s guards turned on him, calling him “kinslayer.” He was slain—but death did not hold
him.
He rose again, reborn as the first vampire, and Barovia was swallowed by the Mists of Ravenloft,
becoming the first Domain of Dread—a prison crafted by the Dark Powers, with Strahd as both warden
and inmate.
The Curse of Immortality
For centuries, Strahd has ruled Barovia from Castle Ravenloft. His curse is not merely undeath—it is
eternal longing. Tatyana’s soul reincarnates across generations, always out of reach. Each time, Strahd
finds her, courts her, and fails. The land itself has decayed into a shadow of its former self: the sun never
shines fully, the people live in fear, and the Mists prevent escape.
Though master of his domain, Strahd is a Darklord—powerful, but eternally tormented. His curse is one
of obsession, control, and endless cycles of failure. He is bound to Barovia. He cannot die by natural
means, nor can he leave.
Powers and Influence
Vampiric Mastery: Strahd possesses powers far beyond a typical vampire—command over
weather, beasts, and the undead.
- Strategist: He is a genius military tactician and manipulator, often playing adventuring parties
against themselves.
- Darklord Abilities: Within Barovia, he has a supernatural awareness, can manipulate events
through the Mists, and is nearly unkillable unless specific conditions are met.

## The Fall of the Stormraven Sisters ##
When Strahd's lust for power drove him to make his fateful pact with the Dark Powers of the Amber
Temple, the sisters followed without hesitation. They stood at his side on the night he seized Castle
Ravenloft, aiding him in slaughtering his enemies and securing his throne.
But they did not anticipate what he had become. That night, when Strahd descended upon his own
soldiers and feasted upon their blood, the sisters hesitated for the first time. Even their loyalty wavered
as they realized their master was no longer a man, but something else—something monstrous.
Strahd, sensing their doubt, gave them a choice:
"Swear yourselves to me once more, body and soul, and I will grant you the gift of eternity. Or resist,
and die as all the others did."
Elandra was the first to kneel, her oath still binding her to the Von Zarovich name. She had given
everything for Strahd’s cause—she would not turn away now.
Seraphine, ever fascinated by the secrets of undeath, saw an opportunity for power beyond anything
she had known. She embraced the darkness willingly, eager to explore the limits of her newfound
immortality.
Kaelin, however, was not so quick to yield. The youngest sister had always been the most
independent, the one who could vanish into the night without a trace. She tried to flee. She did not
make it far. Strahd hunted her down himself, cornering her in the depths of Castle Ravenloft. When he
found her, he did not strike her down.
Instead, he whispered: "You of all should know, little bird—there is no escaping the shadows."
And so, she too was turned, her resistance shattered, her will remade in Strahd’s image. That night,
the Stormraven Sisters were reborn—as dark servants.
