Merrick Grandholm (Changeling Wizard)
Background Notes: Merrick Grandholm was found inexplicably roaming the mountainside near a
monastery. He was raised there and adhered to the same tenets as the monks who called the monastery
home. He was named by his adoptive father Brother Bancroft and given the same surname that the
monks had taken for official identification with their kingdom. The monks residing at the Grandholm
monastery were named Grandholm, and so was he. As Merrick grew and learned all that the monastery
could teach a young boy, Brother Bancroft discovered Merricks great talent for Magic. At the age of
admittance Brother Bancroft enrolled his son in the school at Aarbolest tower, a prestigious school for
young mages. There Merrick eventually joined the Order of Scribes. Having distinguished himself
academically and excelling in the practical exams and interschool tournaments he caught the eye of
Arch magister Gerralt. In time the Gnomish man offered Merrick his apprenticeship. Within the Tower's
chapter of the Order of Scribes it is a right of passage for master to guide apprentice in the making of a
unique artifact of great importance. An enchanted Spell Book meant to accompany the apprentice for
the rest of his days. It was shortly after completing this right of passage that Merrick was swept away in
a ritual gone wrong to unknown lands.
