Narrate Madam Eva using the Tarokka Cards to the players as a whole. Have it call out specific parts of
their past. Such as defeating Kaelin at the Church.. That the allies they search are near Mount Baratok
along the north east banks of Lake Zarovich. Include specific facts about the players backgrounds..
Gregor (Human Ranger/Rogue)
Background Notes: I was found orphaned as a child and was raised by the Talebringers, a group of
ranger-scribes who served and protected the people of Soravia. Talebringers chronicles life in writing
and followed a discipline to train the mind and body equally, and to follow Ilmater, the Broken God.
Years of my impulsive and roguish behavior made a "poor fit" to the cause at home, so once I was of age
they sent me on "scouting" runs to the furthest corners of the land.
Ivan Oronovich (Human Cleric)
Background Notes: Barovian farmer who in hard times turned to religion to supplement his work, keep
crops growing and his family fed. When external threats impose on the village they have taken up arms
and found themselves facing a bigger scarier world.
Korith Glasssteel (Dragonborn Paladin)
Background Notes: He was given his longsword by his mother before his village was destroyed by a
crystal dragon. he fought back against the dragon and managed to stab and permanently damage the
dragons eye and is now searching for the dragon to get revenge for his village.
Krutha Nine-Lives (Orc Barbarian
Background Notes: Was supposed to die in glorious battle, but has consistently failed do do so. His own
preparedness, common sense, nimbleness, & shear toughness has got him through.
He has a set of codes that he follows religiously, things like: "hospitality must be returned in kind", "evil
must be slain", etc. As a young teenager, Krutha's tiny village was attacked by cultists. They captured the
weaker members, & killed the stronger. The catives were beaten, bound, barely fed, and branded with
evil symbols (demonic? devilish? IDK). Over several hours (or days or weeks?), they were steadily
sacrificed in profane rituals. Krutha gained a lot of trauma around losing his freedom, rather than a fear
of pain & suffering. Also a burning hatred for evil & cultists. Krutha only survived because a band of
rightous warriors/paladins/etc, worshipers of Tyr, found and slew the cultists. They took Krutha with
them for a time, long enough for him to pick up Tyr's religion. Krutha is still haunted by the evil brand he
