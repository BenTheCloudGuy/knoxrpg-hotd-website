Amulet of Spell Storing/Capturing (Item Block)
Spell Capture. When a creature casts a spell that targets only you or includes you in its area, you can use your reaction to
attempt to capture it. Make an Arcana check (DC = 10 + the spell’s level). On a success, the spell is absorbed by the
amulet and has no effect on you. The amulet stores the spell for future use.
Stored Spells. The amulet can hold up to 10 spell levels at once. A stored spell can be cast as an action without using a
spell slot or material components. You use your spellcasting ability for attack rolls and save DCs when casting stored
spells. Once released, the spell is removed from the amulet.
Overload. If storing a captured spell would increase the amulet’s stored levels above 10, the amulet shatters. All stored
spells immediately target the wearer in ascending order of spell level. The wearer automatically fails all saving throws
against these spells. The amulet is destroyed afterward.
Arcane Insight. A number of times per long rest equal to your spellcasting ability modifier (minimum of once), you can
gain advantage on an Arcana check as the amulet subtly guides your understanding of magic.
