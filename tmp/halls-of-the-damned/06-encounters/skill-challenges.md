DC 18 Insight
Her fear isn’t of you.
It’s of what might happen if you see something you shouldn’t.
The caution in her movements feels like someone trying to protect a secret, not a home.
DC 20 Insight
She fully expected to be alone tonight.
She’s trying to act hospitable, but her hands are shaking slightly — not from age, but from worry.
You also notice she keeps positioning herself between you and certain parts of the room.
DC 25+ Insight
There’s a moment, just a flicker, where you see something cold in her eyes.
Not fear — calculation.
AREA 01 — Ground Level (Kitchen & Hearth)
The ground floor of the mill is small and crowded, lit by a single candle and the dull red glow coming from a low-burning hearth. The air is warm and heavy with the smell of stew—
venison, rabbit, potatoes—simple food, but comforting after the long road. The space itself is a cramped tangle of household clutter: pans stacked in lopsided piles, chipped bowls,
cutting boards dusted with flour, and bundles of herbs hung from the rafters. A round wooden table sits off to one side with only three chairs, the surface covered in mismatched tin
plates and a battered clay ladle. Offalia ushers everyone in and closes the door quickly behind you. She forces a smile that looks practiced.
Offalia:
“Come, come. Sit. You all look half-frozen. The stew’s hot—you’re lucky you arrived when you did.”
She moves around the room in quick, nervous motions.
“You can stay the night on the ground floor. There’s blankets. I’ll… I’ll keep the fire going.”
She hesitates before adding, “The upstairs is where my family sleeps. It’s a bit cramped up there. Best to leave it be.” She glances toward the staircase as she says it—short,
stiff, and protective. Not rude, just guarded.
When the party steps inside the cramped mill, Offalia’s eyes go immediately to their clothes — the dried blood, torn fabric, cuts in armor, mud smeared up to the knees. She doesn’t
comment at first. She just… stares, her brows pinching tight for a second before she forces herself to look away.
She ladles stew into bowls, but her hands shake more than before.
As the children cling to Ireena, Offalia’s gaze lingers on the wounds they came with — the claw marks, the ripped fabric, the bruising around armor straps. Every glance ramps up her
tension. She tries to hide it, but the more she looks, the more it becomes clear:
These people have been fighting.
Recently.
Violently.
And if they fought something that left those marks…
…then they’re capable of fighting her family.
Insight / Perception Checks (Subtle, Not Announced)
DC 12 — She’s unsettled by how rough the party looks.	•
DC 14 — The blood and torn gear trouble her more than the group seems to think.	•
DC 16 — The fear isn’t for them — it’s for what their presence means.	•
DC 17+ — She’s worried they’re too dangerous for her to handle alone… and that she needs Mograntha now, not in the morning.	•
Once everyone finds a spot on the ground floor, Offalia busies herself at the hearth. The stew pot simmers with the smell of rabbit, deer, onions, and a few wild herbs. It’s warm,
filling, and exactly what a tired group wants after a long day.
Offalia:
“Give me about an hour and it’ll be ready. Fresh meat takes time. Make yourselves comfortable.”
She moves around the kitchen in quick, restless motions — gathering bowls, checking the pot, adjusting the fire even when it doesn’t need it. As she chops a mix of vegetables for
the final batch, she hesitates at the bundle of hanging roots. Her hand lingers a second too long before she plucks a thin, red-skinned one from the back. She keeps her body angled
toward the hearth as she slices it very finely. When she thinks no one is watching, she stirs the pieces into the pot with a practiced, smooth motion. Then she talks to fill the silence.
Offalia:
“Terrible night to be out. Lucky you found us when you did—wolves all over those woods…”
She keeps stirring, glancing at the door and then at the children near Ireena.
After a few more minutes, she wipes her hands on her apron and forces a smile.
Offalia:
“Another little while. Then you’ll all get a hot meal and a good night’s rest.”
The stew smells stronger now, richer… and faintly sweet.
Checks (Concise)
Survival DC 18+: You notice her slipping the red root into the stew.	•
Nature/Knowledge DC 25+: You recognize it as Dreamlatch Root, a sleep-inducing plant.	•
Offalia places bowls on the table, one by one, never letting her eyes drift far from the staircase or the children seated beside Ireena. The stew smells surprisingly good—rich, savory,
filling. She wipes her hands on her apron and forces a light laugh.

## Page 308

#### A Cry for Help ####
As the players and crew manage to fend off the attack by the Night Screamers, they hear a
voice calling out of the woods. From the mist shrouded woods two figures emerge. One leaning
heavily on the other to the of nearly being dragged by the larger man.
GM Narration:
As the last screech of the Night Screamers fades into the night, the chaos aboard the Sea
Spray slowly subsides. The deck is a wreck of broken wood, scattered supplies, and the
blood of the wounded. The fires from the ship’s lanterns cast an eerie glow through the fog
that clings to the coastline, the orange light flickering in and out like the last breaths of the
dying ship. All that remains is the heavy silence of aftermath—the distant crash of waves and
the creaking of the ship’s strained timbers, settling into the rocky shore.
Then, out of the fog that rolls in from the mist-shrouded woods, a voice cuts through the
oppressive quiet—a strong, desperate voice, filled with authority and urgency:
"In the name of King Frostmantle, I call for aid!"
As your eyes search the Darkness, two shadowy figures emerge. One of them, a smaller
man, is leaning heavily on the larger one, his legs barely supporting his own weight. The
larger figure moves with grim determination, dragging the wounded man forward with each
heavy step.
As they come closer, the firelight reveals more. The larger man is clad in battered armor,
streaked with blood and dirt. His face is lined with exhaustion. The man he supports is younger,
his body slumped, and his once-fine clothing torn and drenched in blood. He clutches his side
where a deep wound festers, his face pale, his breathing shallow and labored.
The larger man raises his head and you see the unmistakable symbols of Damaran royalty on
their cloaks, though tattered and weathered.
As they draw nearer, the desperate urgency in his voice returns:
"Please... in the name of the King... help us."
Suggested Player Prompts:
The players will likely rush to help or inquire more. They might want to make Perception or
Medicine checks to discern more about the situation.
-
Perception Check (DC 15): The players notice the intricate sigil of House Frostmantle on the
younger man's cloak, indicating that these are no ordinary travelers.
-
Medicine Check (DC 12): The young man is gravely wounded, with a festering wound in his
side, likely from a blade. His condition is dire and will require immediate treatment.
