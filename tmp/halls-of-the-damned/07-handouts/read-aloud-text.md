Present: PCs, Ismark, Ireena, Father Donavich
Atmosphere: Cold, gray mist chokes the morning. The bell does not toll.
Player-Facing Scene Description:
As the fog clings to the dead grass and the clouds blot out the sun, you gather around a freshly dug grave behind the weathered church. The scent of damp soil and incense
mingles with the faint tang of something... sour.
Ismark stands rigid, hands trembling around a battered shovel. Ireena holds a single white rose, her face stoic, but her eyes shadowed.
Father Donavich rasps a prayer to the Morninglord, but his voice cracks with each line — this is a man whose hope has been bleeding out for some time.
Just as the final rites are spoken, a chill stronger than before crawls across your neck. The wind dies. A whisper rides the fog:
"She is mine."
A shape flickers in the mist. Then another. Then six.
Combat Encounter: Strahd's Message
Name: The Hand of the Devil
Challenge Rating: ~2–3 (adjust for party size)
Creatures:
4x Strahd Zombies (Curse of Strahd, p. 234)
These grotesque undead can keep fighting after dismemberment.
2x Dire Wolves (Monster Manual, p. 321)
Eerie, unnatural howls. Sent to flush and isolate targets (like Ireena).
Optional: One Shadow or Specter whispering to Ireena but visible only to her.
Father Donavich’s Role
Terrified but resolute, steps forward and casts Turn Undead (Channel Divinity).
Offers desperate prayers aloud: “Morninglord, drive back the darkness! Grant these warriors your dawn-blessed fury!”
Struggles to defend Ireena if the players fail.
Ireena & Ismark’s Role
Ireena: Refuses to hide. Tries to draw a blade (unskilled but courageous).
Ismark: Stands to protect her, but is clearly outmatched without PC help.
Strahd's Mark
No physical appearance, but his voice is heard, and the undead carry his will.
"You delay what is inevitable. Every beat of her heart rings louder in my ears. I will have her, soul and scent."
If the players are losing or taking too long:
A raven lands nearby — a symbol of the Keepers of the Feather watching.
Or a burst of sunlight through the clouds stuns the undead briefly (symbolic, not mechanical).
GM Notes
Give the players a chance to shine, but also a taste of helplessness.
Let them win the battle, but leave them shaken.
Consider allowing a Strahd Zombie to grab Ireena and begin dragging her away — force the players to act fast.
Treasure / Aftermath
On one zombie, a black wax seal with the letter “S” (for Strahd), still warm.
Ireena clutches Kolyan’s old silver holy symbol, which glows faintly when held by a PC of good alignment.
After the Battle: Roleplay Prompt
Father Donavich, after battle, breathes hard, wiping ichor from his sleeve.
“You see now… Barovia is not cursed by Strahd. Barovia is Strahd. He knows when we breathe. He sees when we weep. And he will not stop until the world dies or she is his.”
