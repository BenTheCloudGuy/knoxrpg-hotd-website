Halls of the Damned — Inspiration Token House Rules
Core Mechanics
Each player begins the session with 1 Inspiration Token.	•
Additional tokens may be awarded by the DM for:
Exceptional roleplay or dramatic choices	○
Clever problem-solving or teamwork	○
Cinematic or thematic combat moments	○
- Tokens can be pooled with nearby allies (within 30 feet or with line of sight/sound).	•
No more than 5 tokens may be held by a single player at once.	•
Spending Tokens
Tokens Effect
1 Token Reroll any dice roll (yours or the GM’s). You must accept the new result.
2 Tokens Automatically roll a natural 20.
2 Tokens Gain an additional Bonus Action this turn.
X Tokens Recover a spell slot of level X.
2 Tokens Flash of Insight — Ask the DM one question about a person, place, or event
that your character could reasonably infer. The DM must answer truthfully,
though possibly cryptically.
5 Tokens (once per
player per
campaign)
Last Stand — When reduced to 0 HP, you may instead drop to 1 HP and
immediately take a full turn as a reaction. You gain advantage on all rolls during
that turn. Afterward, gain 1 level of exhaustion.

Leap Years
Every 4 years, an extra day called Shieldmeet is added after Midsummer, making that year 366 days
long. This leap year rule keeps the calendar aligned with the solar cycle.
Common Use in Gameplay
Tendays: Instead of weeks, most people in Faerûn use the ten-day cycle (“tendays”). There are 3
tendays per month.

## Page 322

You break through the treeline into a small clearing—and your breath catches.
Before you stands a massive oak, gnarled and ancient, its winter-bare limbs reaching like skeletal arms toward the sky. At
its base, three monstrous hounds—each the size of a bear—circle and snarl, claws tearing into bark and soil. Their fur is coarse
and black, their eyes glowing like embers, and from their open jaws leaks a constant haze of smoke. The stench of burning flesh and
sulfur fills the clearing.
Perched high in the branches, trembling, is a small girl, probably around 12. Her face is streaked with soot and tears. She
clutches the branch so tightly her knuckles are white, and her voice cracks with raw terror as she cries out—
"Help me! Please! They're going to kill me!"
One of the hounds lunges, its claws tearing into the tree, sending a shudder through the trunk. Another paces in a tight
circle, growling in frustration. The third lifts its head and lets out a low, guttural howl—and for a moment, you swear the
shadows in the clearing ripple in response.
"Please! Don't let them get me!"
The creatures turn toward you all… Let's roll for initiative..
