# Session Summary

You were guided through the "Devils Mists" to the gates of Barovia by Velinka d’Avenir. She warned you to be careful around those that call themselves Vistani as many of them serve the Devil Strahd and that you should follow the road and move quickly before the sun sets. 
	"This fog, it remembers… every footstep, every scream, every bloodstain on the road that leads to Barovia. You feel it too, don’t you? Like eyes pressing in from all sides. It is not just weather — it is Barovia itself."
	
	"My sister, Sorina... she was the heart of our caravan. Strong, willful, and more Vistani than she ever admitted aloud. Her daughter, little Ezzie — always listening, always watching. That girl saw more than she should have. Saw the Sisters come for us... the Stormravens in their death-black armor."
	
	"I was pulled from the wreckage by the druids of the Verdant Heart. Nursed back to life in a forest that does not belong on any map. I saw visions there, dreams woven into bark and bone. And in all of them, Barovia burned beneath an unblinking red eye."
	
	"The Vistani… we are not what they think we are. Not all of us serve Strahd. Some of us remember what we were before he shackled our blood to his will. Our music once called down stars, not shadows. But now? Now the old songs go unsung, and our people scatter like leaves before the wind."
	
"Come, The mists part for you now. But do not mistake that for welcome. Barovia wants something. It always does."

After a few hours of making your way down the road you eventually came to the Village of Barovia. The town was eerily silent and empty as you made your way through the Village, eventually coming to the only building with any real signs of life; the Blood of the Vine Inn. 

As you entered you see the inn main room you are introduced to Sarkov and Luna E'Tressa, the owners of the Blood and Vine.  Gregor in attempting to converse with Sarkov, awkwardly learned that Sarkov's tongue was missing and unable to speak. During the conversations you learned they were Vistani that gave up 'The Travel' as they no longer found their views aligned with the rest of the Vistani they travelled with.

You also meet two young Vistani who appear to be crying. This is Dmitru and Sorina Vaduva who tell you that while passing through the Village of Barovia, their young Daughter Liliana (or Lily) had vanished. She was wearing a yellow dress with dark curly hair. They stayed behind to look for her and offer to compensate the players or aid the players if they would help them find her… 

Along with the young couple there was 3 older Vistani women who kept to themselves.  They move around helping around the Inn between talking at a table among themselves. And finally there was a middle aged man who was watching from the back of the room drinking a glass of wine. As you entered you could feel his eyes on you.. 

The man introduced himself as Ismark Kolyanovich, the son of the local Burgomaster (think Mayor) of the Village. He has asked that you join him at the Buromaster estate tomorrow, that he might have some work for the group. Also you met a Priest of the Morninglord named Ivan Oronovich. He talked about having come to the Village to learn from Father Donavich, the local Priest and keeper of the Church of Barovia. He invited you all to join him the following morning. 

During the Night a few of you stayed up to see the nightly march of undead from the Graveyard by the Church to the gates of Castle Ravenloft. As the morning came, you all along with Ivan went to the Church of Barovia where Father Donavich, who clearly had been drinking quickly rushed the players out, much to Ivan's surprise. Dagger started sniffing around and smelled something from under the Church.. After a little B&E Krutha managed to get into the room that Dagger was indicating the smell from and discover a hidden door beneath the single bed in the room. As he went down, the smell was much worse and in the dark a pair of glowing red eyes followed by a sudden movement of a large creature came charging him…
