### [REINFORCEMENTS]

As Players start up the hill or just get up, the second wave of enemies arrive. This should feel overwhelming to players give them a moment to talk about how to address this new development.  This should feel like a desperate last stand for the Players, as they're forced to fall back to the Hill Top (circle the wagons) - this should feel dangerous possible TPK. Don't pull punches on players as they spread themselves out across the battlefield. 

This will include a flesh golem pulling a catapult. He will lock down the arm and load up large leather bags that then get set on fire. The First shot will target an area of 20' (10' radius) | Dex Save for 1/2 8d6+8 Fire Damage. It takes 3 turns to reload and fire (1 turn to lock it down, 1 turn to load and light the ammunition, Fires on 3 turn..  Has enough shots for 4 attacks..

### [WOLF PACK ARRIVES]

The Werewolves lead by Emil Toranescu joins the fray and jumps archers and outliers. Players will hear the howl of wolves, just as they burst out of the high grass attacking the ranged goblins and casters. She'll make her way to the players on the hill.. She will tell the players about the Tser Raven Encampment and that they followed the trail here.. They'll attack the Catapult and suffer casualties while 'Emil Toranescu' pushes past the stitches to let the players know she is here to help.. They players will recognize her as the Werewolf that jumped from the Wizards tower, she has healing magic and will cast healing on any players that might need it.  This will take 2-3 turns before the [END PHASE] begins. 

### [END PHASE]

Skanders open dimensional door to Tser Hill to send Reinforcements at the same time that Barovian Forces arrive under Ismark, Father Donavich, and Doru and 25 members of the Brovian Militia.

## Summary of Session 28

- Dagger and his nemesis from the Abbey, the foul mouthed little MongleKind that he nearly killed the last time they met.. The little creature calls out Dagger from across the battlefield. Just a Dagger locks eyes on the creature - he "misty stepped" vanishing.. Dagger thinking quick jumped back a few feet from where he was standing just as the creature appears right behind where Dagger had just been standing! Before the creature can figure out what happened - Dagger unleashes a crossbow bolt into its back - the creature screamed in pain and surpirse - then misty stepped again to a neaby roof top taking cover. 
- Brother Ivan casts fireball on the catapult and hits the ammunition, blowing the device apart before it ever gets a shot off. The Giant decides to move toward the fight carrying his massive ram rod as a weapon.
- Vasilka reappears and drops a flaming sphere, a tornado of fire that engulfs Thorian just after he tossed one of the patchwork goblins into the burning hut. Thorian is forced to pull back out of the flames, badly burned. He and Merrick counter-attack now that Vasilka has shown herself, landing a devastating dual hit on her as she tries to flee through a portal. The party can see the Abbott on the other side of the portal, his eye full of anger and hatred as his prize is blasted through and lands wounded and unconscious on the stone floor in front of him. He starts to say something just as the portal snaps out of existence.
- Just as the catapult and Vasilka are dealt with, the werewolves led by Emil Toranescu come running out of the forest and tear into the remaining patchwork goblins. Emil, the one everyone thought was dead from the fall at the tower, tells the players about the attack on the other Vistani camp. Her people made sure the survivors made it to Krezk, then she followed this bunch here. Seeing Thorian badly burned, she offers some quick healing.
- Krutha and Korith continue to engage the enemies, Krutha still very much naked. Korith makes a mad dash for the catapult and the Giant about to fire it, darting through a gap in the enemy line like a linebacker who suddenly found himself with the ball, headed straight at the approaching Giant. As he is running through, Ivan fires off another fireball at the horde of mongrelfolk Korith is running through, and Korith barely stays ahead of the blast that wipes out most of the mongrelfolk on that flank. Just as he readies for a fight, the Giant takes two mighty swings at Korith and nearly drops him.
- Merrick and Thorian then join their magic again to bury the Giant up to his face, trapping the creature and giving Korith a chance at some much needed payback. Krutha and Dagger rush up to help. Dagger, with a mischievous grin, uncorks the large bottle of oil he has been carrying, pours it down the Giant's face and throat, and lights him up. The three stand back and admire their handiwork as the Giant, trapped in dirt and burning to death, is finished off.
- When the Giant is defeated the remaining mongrelfolk decide to retreat, with Emil's werewolves hot on their heels. They run straight into the forces from Vallaki arriving via large portals opened by the Skanders. Alongside them is the detachment from the Village of Barovia. Ireena rides up on her grey gelding: "This is the second time I've come rushing to your aid only to find you didn't need it." Coming up beside her is Ismark Kolyanovich: "You kept your word and saw my sister to safety. Thank you. I'm here for whatever you need." The players also see Father Donavich and his now healthy son Doru with the new arrivals.
- The party decides to take the survivors and move everyone back to the safety of Vallaki and hold a conference at the Inn. Bray and Brom, Urwin and Danika's two eldest sons, agree to fly out together to warn the remaining Vistani of Strahd's betrayal and tell them to get to Vallaki.
- While at the Inn, they get the message from Van Richten letting them know they managed to take the "old fool" (what he calls Mordenkainen) and that he is in the back of the wagon recovering with Ezmerelda tending to him. The Scroll of Restoration did the trick and restored his mind, but Mordenkainen is still weak from his ordeal. The players ask the Skanders to open another large portal so Van Richten, Moddy, and Ezzie can join the council at the Inn. This is where we left off, with the players and their growing list of allies sitting around the tables at the Blue Water Inn getting ready to decide how best to deal with Strahd and Lady Williams, and unsure who is actually the greater threat here.
