# Session Summary

The session started at the Blue Water Inn with a rare quiet moment before everything went wrong. The party had breakfast while Vallaki prepared for the Festival of Light. During that scene, Van Richten contacted the group through the speaking stone. He said he and Ezmerelda had found the Mad Mage, but he was too dangerous to deal with while awake. He also warned the party to split up the speaking stones and not to trust easy answers, miracles, or smiling nobles telling them everything was fine. 

By late morning, the festival was underway. Vallaki looked more cheerful than usual on the surface, with lanterns, streamers, musicians, food, and crowds in the square. A number of important NPCs were there, including the Martikovs, Father Lucian, Lady Fiona and her children, Luvash and Arabelle, and the Krezkovs. Even so, the whole event still felt tense underneath. 

When Baron Vargas brought the honored guests forward, things turned fast. Lady Williams appeared and publicly delivered Strahd’s message. She mocked the festival, made it clear Strahd was watching, and completely took control of the moment. Right after that, the mist rolled into the square and visibility dropped hard. Panic spread almost immediately. 

From there, the session split into two major threads. In the square, the main objective was not killing everything but getting civilians to safety inside the church. Father Lucian tried to direct the crowd while undead and other horrors came out of the fog. Korith and Krutha held the line in melee while Merrick used fireballs to keep larger groups of enemies from overrunning the area. Brother Ivan used the Holy Symbol of Ravenkind against the undead. Several NPC moments stood out during the chaos, including Brom saving Arabelle, Karl being killed in the fog, Nikolai rushing the thing that killed his brother, Anna Krezkova forcing Fiona to move, and Tomas Vek saving one of the party at the last moment. 
At the same time, Greggor and Dagger carried out the infiltration of the Burgomaster’s estate. With most of the household distracted by the festival, they got inside, made their way down through the house, and found the hidden altar room. There they discovered the real source of the attack: cultists, a corpse on an altar, wraiths, and a tall ritual creature leading the whole thing. Greggor kept pressure on the enemies while Dagger got in close and landed the key hit with the wolfsbane dagger, stabbing the creature in the eye. The creature turned to mist and escaped, but the ritual was broken. 

Once that happened, the fog in town began to clear. The square fight started to turn, and the immediate danger passed. The party regrouped with the survivors as Father Lucian and Brother Ivan began helping the wounded. Afterward, Urwin and Danika approached the party and pointed them toward the Wizard of Wines as the next major lead. Danika then revealed her raven form and flew south to send word ahead.
