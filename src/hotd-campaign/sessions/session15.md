# Session Summary

At Tser Pool, the party confronted Seraphine Stormraven, one of Strahd’s dreaded Stormraven Sisters and the same dark sorceress responsible for turning Duro and unleashing horrors upon the land. With no intention of allowing her to escape, the group chose to stand and fight.

The battle was swift but brutal. Seraphine wielded powerful dark magic, attempting to overwhelm the party with shadow and sorcery, but the heroes pressed their attack relentlessly. Through coordinated strikes and sheer determination, they brought her down, marking the second Stormraven Sister the party has defeated in their campaign.

With Seraphine slain, the party secured the area around Tser Pool, searching the battlefield for anything of value or importance. They gathered the fallen and burned the dead, ensuring the corrupted would not rise again in Barovia’s cursed lands.

Exhausted from the conflict, the group took a short rest, tending to wounds and collecting themselves after the fight. With their enemy defeated and the road ahead still long, they began preparing for the next leg of their journey toward Vallaki.

The session ended with the party regrouping at the edge of the Vistani encampment, knowing that their actions had struck another blow against Strahd’s servants—but that far greater dangers still awaited them deeper in Barovia.
