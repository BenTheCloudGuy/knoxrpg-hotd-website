## Current Events

### Portal to Krezk

The Skanders open a large portal in Vallaki and the party, with Dmitri and Anna Krezkov and a contingent of Krezk soldiers, steps through onto the snow-packed road below the walls of Krezk. The town gates are barred. Smoke trails from the Abbey on the mountainside above. The mongrelfolk that survived Tser Hill have fallen back here, and the Abbot is waiting for them.

Dmitri leads his soldiers to retake the village and hold the gate, which keeps the bulk of the mongrelfolk horde busy. The party's job is the Abbey itself: cut through whatever the Abbot throws at them and reach him. Vasilka is somewhere inside, still wounded, and the Abbot will not let her die.

## Krezk Assault — The Abbot and the Mongrelfolk

The fight is built so the players cannot simply alpha-strike the Abbot down. The mongrelfolk are mooks: cheap, expendable bodies that screen the Abbot, eat opportunity attacks, body-block lanes, and force the party to spend actions clearing them before they can focus the boss. Run roughly 8 to 12 mongrelfolk that refresh as more shamble out of the Abbey wings each round. The Abbot fights from behind them, healing himself, smiting from range, and reviving any mongrelfolk that drop near him.

### The Abbot (Fallen Deva)

A radiant, beautiful, and increasingly unhinged angel. He speaks gently while he kills, certain that everything he does is mercy. Reduce or raise hit points to taste for a Level 9 party of your size.

- **Armor Class** 17 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points** 187 (25d8 + 75)
- **Speed** 30 ft., fly 90 ft.
- **STR** 18 (+4) **DEX** 18 (+4) **CON** 18 (+4) **INT** 17 (+3) **WIS** 20 (+5) **CHA** 20 (+5)
- **Saving Throws** Wis +9, Cha +9
- **Skills** Insight +9, Perception +9
- **Damage Resistances** radiant; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks
- **Condition Immunities** charmed, exhaustion, frightened
- **Senses** darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 19
- **Languages** all, telepathy 120 ft.
- **Challenge** 10 (5,900 XP)

**Angelic Weapons.** The Abbot's melee weapon attacks are magical. When he hits with any weapon, the weapon deals an extra 18 (4d8) radiant damage (included below).

**Innate Spellcasting.** Spellcasting ability is Charisma (save DC 17). He can innately cast the following, requiring only verbal components:
- At will: *detect evil and good*
- 1/day each: *commune*, *raise dead*

**Cleric Spellcasting (12th level).** The Abbot is a 12th-level cleric. Spellcasting ability is Wisdom (save DC 17, +9 to hit). Lean on the attack and debuff spells to grind the party down while the mongrelfolk screen him.

**Cantrips (at will):**
- *Sacred flame* — DC 17 Dex save, 3d8 radiant; ignores cover. Default snipe at a screened-off target.
- *Toll the dead* — DC 17 Wis save, 3d12 necrotic vs. a wounded target (3d8 if at full HP).
- *Guidance* — buff a mongrelfolk's next ability check; flavor for keeping the horde organized.

**1st level (4 slots):**
- *Bane* — three targets, DC 17 Cha save, each subtracts 1d4 from attacks and saves. Open with this to soften the party.
- *Guiding bolt* — ranged spell attack, 4d6 radiant, next attacker has advantage. Sets up the mongrelfolk pile-on.
- *Command* — DC 17 Wis save, one-word order ("flee," "grovel," "drop") to pull a striker out of position.

**2nd level (3 slots):**
- *Hold person* — DC 17 Wis save, paralyzes a humanoid; auto-crits while held. His best lockdown on the martials.
- *Silence* — 20-ft. sphere, no verbal spells; drop it on the party casters.
- *Spiritual weapon* — bonus action, 2d8 + 5 force, floats and follows. Free chip damage every turn.

**3rd level (3 slots):**
- *Spirit guardians* — radiant 4d8, DC 17 Wis half, halves speed. Core engine; keep it up while he hides behind mooks.
- *Bestow curse* — DC 17 Wis save, disadvantage on attacks/saves vs. a key target or extra damage.
- *Dispel magic* — strip a buff or summon off the party.

**4th level (3 slots):**
- *Banishment* — DC 17 Cha save, removes the biggest threat for up to a minute. Concentration.
- *Guardian of faith* — 20 radiant to anyone entering; zones a doorway or the altar.

**5th level (2 slots):**
- *Flame strike* — 10-ft. radius, DC 17 Dex, 4d6 fire + 4d6 radiant. Punish a clustered party.
- *Insect plague* — 20-ft. cube, DC 17 Con, 4d10 piercing, difficult terrain. Wall the party off and grind.

**6th level (1 slot):**
- *Harm* — DC 17 Con, 14d6 necrotic and caps max HP. Reserve for the front-liner.

> Opening play: *bane* to soften, *spirit guardians* up, then *flame strike* / *insect plague* to zone, *hold person* and *banishment* to peel strikers, and *harm* to gut the tank. Keep the mongrelfolk between him and the casters.

**Magic Resistance.** Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

#### Actions

- **Multiattack.** The Abbot makes two melee attacks.
- **Mace.** Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 7 (1d6 + 4) bludgeoning plus 18 (4d8) radiant.
- **Healing Touch (4/Day).** The Abbot touches another creature, which regains 20 (4d8 + 2) hit points and is freed from any curse, disease, poison, blindness, or deafness. He uses this on Vasilka and on wounded mongrelfolk to keep his screen alive.

#### Special Attacks

- **Radiant Smite (Recharge 5–6).** As an action, the Abbot looses a blinding flare in a 30-foot cone. Each creature in the area takes 27 (6d8) radiant damage and must make a DC 17 Constitution save or be blinded until the end of its next turn (half damage and no blind on a success).

#### Reaction

- **Shield of Faith Aura.** When hit by an attack, he can add +2 AC against that attack as celestial light wards him.
- **Sanctified Redirection (Recharge 5–6).** When the Abbot is targeted by a spell, he can use his reaction to cast *Spell Redirect* (below), turning the incoming spell back on its caster.

#### Custom Spell — Spell Redirect

*4th-level abjuration · Casting Time: 1 reaction (when targeted by a spell) · Range: 60 ft. · Components: V · Duration: Instantaneous*

Celestial light snaps the incoming spell around and hurls it back. The triggering spell takes effect on its original caster as though they had cast it on themselves; for area effects, the burst recenters on the caster. The caster must make a saving throw using their own spellcasting ability modifier (their attack stat) against the Abbot's spell save DC 17. On a failure, the redirected spell resolves in full against them. On a success, the spell fizzles and is wasted. Only spells of 4th level or lower can be redirected at base.

**At Higher Levels.** When cast with a 5th-level slot, the redirect DC increases by 1 (DC 18) and can catch spells up to 5th level. With a 6th-level slot, DC increases by 2 (DC 19), catches spells up to 6th level, and the redirected spell deals an extra die of its damage type.

#### Lair Actions

The Abbey grants the Abbot **3 lair action points per round** (refresh on initiative count 20). He spends them on the lair actions below, on his **Sanctified Redirection** reaction, or to fuel his **Sanctified Upcasting**. Any points spent on upcasting are gone for that round, so a heavy upcast leaves him without healing or redirection.

- **Sanctified Upcasting.** As a free part of casting any leveled spell, the Abbot may spend lair action points to upcast it. Each point raises the spell's effective slot by one level, no slot required. He may spend up to all 3 points on a single spell (a 3rd-level *spirit guardians* becomes 6th, *flame strike* scales to 7d6/7d6, etc.). Points spent here cannot be used for the lair actions or reaction below this round.
- **Sanctified Renewal.** The Abbey's holy light gathers around the Abbot. He regains 22 (4d8 + 4) hit points. He cannot use this lair action two rounds in a row.
- (Alternate, if he is at or near full HP) **Cleansing Flare.** Radiant light bursts from the altar; he ends one condition on himself and one mongrelfolk within 30 ft. heals 11 hit points.

### Mongrelfolk (Mooks)

Stitched-together patchwork people. Use them in numbers; they live to die for the Abbot.

- **AC** 11 · **HP** 11 (2d8 + 2) · **Speed** 30 ft.
- **STR** 13 (+1) **DEX** 11 (0) **CON** 12 (+1) **INT** 9 (−1) **WIS** 10 (0) **CHA** 7 (−2)
- **Mimicry.** Imitates sounds and voices to distract or unnerve the party (Insight DC 14 to see through).
- **Multiattack.** Two attacks. Claw +3, 4 (1d6 + 1) slashing, or improvised weapon +3, 5 (1d8 + 1) bludgeoning.
- **Random Limb (1/day).** One mongrelfolk has a single useful trait: spit acid (1d6, 10 ft.), a breath stink (Con save or poisoned), or a misty step. Use the misty-step "Mongle" Dagger fought before for flavor.

**Mook rules:** drop at any damage that meets HP, no death saves, screen the Abbot, soak opportunity attacks, and refresh as new ones spill from the Abbey wings until Dmitri's soldiers break the horde at the gate.

# Session Summary

The party woke after a good night’s rest at the Blue Water Inn and spent the morning making plans with the others. Brother Ivan offered blessings to the group, Emil joined in to everyone’s surprise, and Korith did not. Dagger also emptied the kitchen of bacon before the party, along with Dimitri and his fifty men at arms, set out through the portal to Krezk. The other Skanders opened a separate portal for Ezmerelda, Van Richten, Emil, and a few werewolves in human form, sending them toward Tsolenka Pass and the Amber Temple. Krutha stayed behind at the Inn as he was dealing with an outbreak of Patchwork Goblin Herpes from running around the battlefield slaughtering the little bastards with his junk hanging out.. *Turns out that isn't the most hygenic way to do battle!*

At the Abbey, Dagger and Gregor moved to the ramparts while Korith kicked in the front door with Ivan right behind him. The casters hung back to snipe from safety. Ivan called out the Abbot for his lack of true faith, and the Abbot answered with twisted blood magic that made Ivan’s blood boil. Korith charged in, only to trigger a blood trap that dropped him to his knees. Merrick and Thorian used Merrick’s invisible spying eye to attack the Abbot while he sat on his throne, but he barely seemed bothered and kept Vasilka hidden.

The Abbot then dropped the shield on the door and forced Merrick’s eye out of existence, inviting the casters into the cathedral. Gregor moved to the bell tower, and Dagger slipped into the room unseen. As the fight tightened, Ivan healed Korith and pulled him back from the brink. Gregor slid down the bell ropes, the bells rang out, and he stepped in behind the Abbot with his arcane pistol. The shot hit hard and staggered him. Korith followed with a heavy strike, and when the Abbot raised spiritual shield, Merrick and Thorian counterspelled it, leaving him nearly cleaved in two.

Vasilka then stepped in and hit Korith, dropping him back into the blood pool. Blood Blisters rose from the pools and clawed at the party, including the downed paladin. Gregor got to Korith in time and brought him back up, and Ivan followed with healing word. Gregor and Dagger pressed Vasilka again, and Merrick finally landed another fire spell that hit both Dagger and Vasilka, killing her.

After the fight, Lord Dmitri came in covered in gore and smiled as the party recovered. He offered Brother Ivan the Abbey, saying Krezk needed a new spiritual leader. While looking around, Korith opened a container on the table and set off another trap, only to find six bottles of 8hr Energy, a potion that gave the effect of a long rest in about five minutes. The party was left licking their wounds and deciding what came next.
