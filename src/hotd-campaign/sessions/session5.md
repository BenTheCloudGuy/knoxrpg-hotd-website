# Session Summary

The party pressed deeper into the shattered city of Valls, moving through broken streets and burning ruins while the dead howled in the darkness. The sun had not yet risen, and the night still belonged to the undead. Ghouls raced along the rooftops while the once-living citizens of the city—now twisted into shambling zombies—closed in from every direction.

Seeking temporary refuge, the party barricaded themselves inside an abandoned building. From the shattered windows and doorways they held the line. Gregor and his loyal companion Dagger covered the streets with ranged attacks, cutting down undead as they advanced. Meanwhile Sage summoned a radiant Moonbeam, its pale light burning through the approaching hordes and destroying many of the creatures before they could break into the building.

For a brief moment the assault slowed, giving the party time to catch their breath. Using the lull in the fighting, the group took a short rest, gathering their strength while the distant sounds of the undead still echoed through the ruins.

But the reprieve would not last long. The Citadel of Valls still lay ahead, and whatever darkness had consumed the city awaited them there.
