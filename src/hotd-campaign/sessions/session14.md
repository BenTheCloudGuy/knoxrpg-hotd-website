# Session Summary

As the party woke up from sleep at the Vistani Encampment. The morning bliss was shattered as arrows came raining down on the camp. As the players ready themselves and the Vistani in the camp begin to fall, Ireena grabs the two children and takes cover under some wagons as a Death Knight leads a large host of undead warriors from the East. As the players move in to engage, another Death Knight leads additional undead forces from the West.. Krutha and Dagger move to engage the second group. As the battle rages, Goliath Skeleton creatures emerge from the Tser pool.. The players manage to beat undead back.. Just then a carriage drawn by Nightmares (undead horses) - and a woman steps out and walks toward the wagon where Ireena is hiding. She identifies herself as Seraphine Stormraven and tells Ireena that Lord Strahd demands that Ireena join him at Castle Ravenloft. The players are given a choice, fight or let Ireena go..  That is where this session wraps up.
