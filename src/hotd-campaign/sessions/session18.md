# Session Summary

After their long journey through Barovia, the party finally arrived at the gates of Vallaki, where thick fog rolled down from Lake Zarovich and the town’s looming wooden palisades emerged from the mist. The gates were guarded by two watchmen—Marek Halvorsen, a hardened veteran, and Tomas Vek, a nervous younger guard—who questioned the party before allowing them entry. The guards warned them of Vallaki’s strict expectations and reminded them of the Baron’s mantra: “All will be well.”

Just inside the gates, Ireena Kolyana parted ways with the party. She took the rescued children with her to the Burgomaster’s Mansion, promising to ensure they were safely returned to their families and to inform her brother Ismark that she had reached Vallaki safely. Before leaving, she paid the party for escorting her and suggested they stay at the Blue Water Inn, one of the safer places in town.

At the inn, the party met Urwin Martikov, the innkeeper, and noticed a lone traveler at the bar—Ezmerelda d’Avenir. By overhearing their conversation, the party learned that a mysterious “storyteller” who recently stayed at the inn was actually Rudolph Van Richten, the legendary monster hunter traveling under an alias. Ezmerelda revealed she had been searching for him and feared he had gone alone toward the Old Wizard Tower near Lake Baratok to investigate the Mad Mage.

After settling in at the inn, the party explored Vallaki. Some members visited St. Andral’s Church, where Father Lucian mentioned the same mysterious traveler heading toward the wizard’s tower and agreed to perform a ceremony after the upcoming festival. Others went shopping in town, visiting a strange Arcane Emporium run by the eccentric Iskander and Askander, hedge wizards who traded magical items and lore. The party sold old Netherese coins for gold and jewels, purchased magical gear, and learned that the shopkeepers knew of Mordenkainen and might aid them in the future fight against Strahd. Thorian chose to remain behind temporarily to study with them.

Meanwhile, other party members visited the blacksmith, ordering silver ammunition and arrows to prepare for future battles.

Later that evening, back at the Blue Water Inn, the party spoke with Ezmerelda more openly after revealing their intentions to oppose Strahd. Convinced they shared the same goal, she agreed to help them locate Van Richten. The group decided their next move would be to travel to the Wizard Tower at first light to find the monster hunter.
