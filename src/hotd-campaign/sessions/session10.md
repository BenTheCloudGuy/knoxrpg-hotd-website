# Session Summary

Krutha and party make their way down below the church where they encounter Duro (Vampire Spawn). The players are shocked by the strength and speed of the creature.. When a quick "Hold Person" spell - the players are able to finally get the drop on Duro and just before the final blow comes - Father Danovich comes running down the stairs and begs you to not kill his son.. 

He explains that Duro followed a stranger, a powerful Wizard that came to Barovia to end Strahd. The Wizard Mordenkainen, came about a year ago with promised that he and his friends would end Strahd forever.  However while waiting for his friend Rudolph van Richten and other allies, Strahd learned of his presence. He was forced to take the initiative before his allies had arrived and several of the villagers, including Duro charged Castle Ravenloft during the morning hours - hoping that Strahd and his spawn would be weaker.. The battle was short and bloody as Strahd was lying in wait.. Strahd was gravely wounded by the Wizard, but not before Strahd's Allies were able to defeat the Wizard and those that followed him. Only a 3 villagers managed to make it back, 2 of them dragged Duro back near death. Father Danovich healed the 3 and kept them secreted below the Church for fear Strahd would come for them. He never did, in fact it was almost a year before Strahd made an appearance again, the first time being just over a month ago.. The players take time to learn that Father Donavich using his powers and guidance from another stranger that came about a month after the battle - a Flamboyant half-elf dressed in bright, almost theatrical clothes. His name was Rictavio. He claimed to be a "Ringmaster" of a circus looking for new performers and creatures for his carnival and was bought here by the Vistani. He helped Father Donavich with the ritual to help hold back the effects that took Duro, he also gave him the collar that holds his powers at bay. Father Donavich explains that the only way to heal Vampyrism is to kill or "cure" the creature that turned you. This left Krutha is a difficult place, as he was bitten by Duro and Duro was bitten by Seraphine Stormraven, the Dread Sorceress of Ravenloft. The only way to save Duro is to kill Seraphine. The players shared that they've encountered Kaylen Stormraven and defeated her, Father Danovich promises his aid if you can help end Seraphine and save his son, and thus save Krutha. Krutha has time, a month maybe more, maybe less before the change takes in full. 

The players decide to next go talk to the Burgomaster and see what information he can share.. 

About a month after the battle, another stranger came to town - called himself “Rictavio”, a flamboyant half-elf carnival ringmaster. He dressed in bright, theatrical clothes, claims to be seeking performers for his traveling troupe. He was following the Vistani when they led him through the mists. But he asked specifically about another friend of his that he heard rumored was coming this way, and described his friend, it was clear he was talking about the Wizard Mordenkainen.
