# Session Summary

After the battle at the Wizard’s Tower, the party set out with Ezmerelda and Van Richten to recover the stolen Tome of Aurelian Thone, which had been carried off by Strahd’s servants. The journey north along the shores of Lake Baratok was quiet but ominous, with numerous werewolf tracks heading deeper into the wilderness, far more entering the region than leaving it.

Their search led them to a jagged cave entrance carved into the mountainside—the Werewolves’ Den. Inside, the party was met not with immediate violence but with wary guardians and the pack’s elders. Kiara, the den’s druid matriarch, approached peacefully alongside her mate Duesius. They explained the pack’s tragic history: werewolves had once been hunted nearly to extinction by humans, and Strahd had originally protected them, allowing them to survive. Over the centuries, however, Strahd had grown cruel, demanding their obedience and turning some of the younger wolves into predators who hunted humans openly.

The tension deepened when Jiro, a young boy whose father and brothers had been killed by human hunters—and whose mother had died in the recent tower battle—lashed out at the party in grief and anger. If spared, Kiara revealed the truth behind the boy’s pain and the pack’s divided loyalties. Some among them now question their allegiance to Strahd and might abandon him if convinced a different future is possible.

Deeper within the den, the party finally confronted Elandra Stormraven, the last surviving member of the Stormraven sisters. A deadly bladesinger vampire, Elandra had taken the tome for Strahd and awaited the party in the den’s central chamber, surrounded by Vampyre Knights. With cold fury, she swore vengeance for the deaths of her sisters Seraphine and Kaelin, declaring that the party had taken everything from her—and that she would do the same in return.

The confrontation erupted into battle as Elandra attempted to bend the party to Strahd’s will with powerful enchantments before unleashing her speed, magic, and summoned allies against them.

The session closes with the party facing Elandra and her knights in the heart of the Werewolf Den, where the fate of the stolen tome—and the pack’s future—will be decided. The werewolves after watching you defeat so powerful an enemy agrees to allow you rest the night under their protection and propose an alliance.
