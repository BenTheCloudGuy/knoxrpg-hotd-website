# Session Summary

Team B at the Abbey of St. Markovia

Team B — Gregor, Dagger, and Ivan traveled to the Abbey of St. Markovia in Krezk in search of the Holy Symbol of Ravenkind. When they arrived, they found the abbey in the aftermath of a brutal assault. Strahd and his forces had recently attacked, enraged by the Abbot’s belief that he could restore Strahd’s lost humanity through Vasilka, the flesh-crafted bride meant to redeem him. The grounds were littered with the butchered bodies of mongrelfolk, their deaths recent and violent, the slaughter clearly one-sided.

Those mongrelfolk who survived had been driven into a frenzy by the Abbot’s grief and rage, attacking the party without hesitation. After fighting their way through the maddened creatures, the party confronted the Abbot, who had become unstable after seeing Vasilka gravely wounded during the attack. Still convinced that his plan could save Strahd’s soul, the Abbot demanded the party prove their worth before he would surrender the artifact they sought.

To test them, the Abbot unleashed a massive Flesh Golem against the group. The battle was brutal, nearly killing Ivan and Dagger, but in the end the party prevailed, with Dagger delivering the final blow.

Though furious, the Abbot honored his word. He removed the Holy Symbol of Ravenkind from around Vasilka’s neck—where he had hidden and magically masked it—and handed it to Ivan. Realizing they were no longer welcome and that the Abbot’s madness could turn on them at any moment, the party quickly departed the abbey.

They rode hard through the night, returning to Vallaki just before midnight, now in possession of one of the sacred relics capable of opposing Strahd.
