# Session Summary

After escaping the werewolf ambush on the Old Svalich Road, the party rode hard through the mist until they reached the lantern-lit wagons of the Vistani camp at Tser Pool. The travelers welcomed them cautiously but warmly, impressed that the group had survived an encounter with the wolves of Barovia.

The Vistani leader Stanimir greeted the party with laughter, wine, and an invitation to sit by the great bonfire at the center of the encampment. As the tension of the road faded, the camp slowly returned to life—music from a battered fiddle drifted through the smoke while Vistani families gathered around the fire to celebrate the heroes who had ridden from the dark forest alive.

Throughout the evening the party mingled with the camp’s inhabitants.

Stanimir shared stories of Barovia and warned that Strahd’s influence reaches everywhere, claiming the Devil sees through the eyes of wolves and bats.

Danyela, a sly gambler, challenged the party to games of dice and hinted that the Vistani alone can move safely through the mysterious mists.

Old Piotr, the fiddler, played an ancient hymn tied to forgotten holy orders.

Nicolai, the horse-master, warned that the wolves were only the beginning of the horrors that stalk the night.

A curious young girl named Arabelle eagerly questioned the party about their travels and the outside world beyond Barovia.

As the night deepened, rumors and half-truths circulated around the fire: tales of Strahd’s rule over the land, the mad wizard near Lake Baratok, the supposed safety of Vallaki, the secrets of the Abbey of Saint Markovia, and whispers of relics capable of challenging the Devil himself.

Later that night the camp fell suddenly silent when a summons came from Madam Eva, the ancient Vistani seer. The party was led into her incense-filled tent where she performed a Tarokka reading, revealing visions of their fate. Her prophecy spoke of hidden relics, ancient ruins, powerful allies, and the looming threat of a chained god whose awakening could bring ruin to the world. She also foretold the party’s connection to a mad but powerful ally near Lake Zarovich, hinting that their path would soon lead them there.

Eva’s reading singled out each member of the party, revealing fragments of their destinies, the trials they would face, and the choices that would define them. Her final revelation centered on Ireena Kolyana, whom she called “The Heart”—a soul bound to the land itself and central to the fate of Barovia and Strahd.

After the heavy revelations of prophecy, the party was allowed to rest safely within the circle of the Vistani campfires. That night each hero experienced strange and symbolic dreams, visions hinting at their personal struggles and the greater forces shaping their journey.

By morning, the mist had thinned and the Vistani wagons were already preparing to move on. With a long rest behind them, new knowledge from Eva’s prophecy, and the road to Vallaki still ahead, the party prepared to continue their journey deeper into the cursed lands of Barovia.
