# Session Summary

The session began with the party leaving Vallaki and riding west toward the Old Wizard Tower near Lake Baratok, urged onward by Ezmerelda d’Avenir, who feared for the safety of her mentor, Rudolph Van Richten. As dusk settled over the lake, the party emerged from the woods to a chaotic scene: dozens of werewolves swarming the tower, climbing its stone walls and forcing their way through shattered windows while the howls of an enormous pack echoed through the night.

Inside the tower, Van Richten fought to hold the fourth floor, unleashing powerful arcane defenses. A sudden lightning strike from a clear sky crashed into the tower, followed by a violent magical explosion that blasted werewolves from the structure and briefly silenced the battlefield. The momentary calm was broken when a wounded female werewolf rushed to the fallen pack leader, desperately casting healing magic on the survivors before the wolves regrouped for another assault.

The party charged into the fight, cutting through packs of werewolves while pushing toward the tower. Amid the chaos, two werebats swooped in through the tower’s upper levels and tore a massive tome from Van Richten’s hands, escaping into the night. Gregor managed to mark one of the creatures before they vanished toward the north.

With the wolves finally driven off and Ezmerelda reunited with Van Richten, the hunters shared what they had learned from the tower’s archives. The tower had once belonged to Aurelian Kael Thorne, an ancient artificer who attempted to repair the legendary Sun Sword, the only weapon capable of truly destroying Strahd von Zarovich. When Strahd discovered his work, Thorne hid the blade’s fragments with the Order of the Silver Dragon at Argynvostholt, whose knights died defending it.

Van Richten explained that defeating Strahd would require more than the sword. The party must recover several key relics and uncover the truth behind Strahd’s Necromantic Anchor, the dark pact that allows the vampire to return from death again and again.

Key objectives revealed:

The Sun Sword lies hidden in Argynvostholt.

The Amulet of Ravenkind may be found at the Abbey of St. Markovia in Krezk.

The Tome of Aurelian Thorne, stolen during the battle, is needed to repair the Sun Sword and is now being carried toward the Werewolf Den.

The legendary Mad Mage of Mount Baratok (Mordenkainen) could become a powerful ally and may be able to help restore the blade.

Van Richten and Ezmerelda plan to search for the Mad Mage, leaving the party with sending stones so they can remain in contact across Barovia.

As the night ends, the party rests in the tower and makes plans for their next move. Their immediate goal is to recover the stolen tome before Gregor’s Hunter’s Mark fades, after which the group intends to split forces—one heading toward Krezk for the Amulet of Ravenkind while the other travels to Argynvostholt to locate the Sun Sword.
