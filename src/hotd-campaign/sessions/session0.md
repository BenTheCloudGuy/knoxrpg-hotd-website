# Session Summary

The adventure began in the frozen frontier town of Frosthaven, within the warm but rough confines of the Frozen Anchor Tavern. There, a group of strangers—each with their own past and purpose—found themselves gathered together: Gregor, Korith, Merrick, Sage, Krutha Nine-Lives, and Nina. What began as an ordinary evening of food, drink, and conversation soon turned into the beginning of something far greater.

During the night, the party met Captain Reaven “Emerald Eyes,” the bold Tabaxi captain of the river vessel Sea Spray. She was seeking capable fighters and adventurers willing to accompany her crew on a dangerous journey south along the river into Damara, a land troubled by war, strange rumors, and tightening laws against magic. After some discussion—and perhaps a bit of curiosity about what awaited beyond the frozen north—the group agreed to join her expedition.

The following morning, the newly formed fellowship met Captain Reaven at the docks where the Sea Spray waited. With the crew assembled and preparations complete, the ship cast off from Frosthaven and began its voyage downriver toward Damara.
