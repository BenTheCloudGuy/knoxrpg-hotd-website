# Session Summary

After leaving the Werewolves’ Den, the party split their forces. Team A—without Korith, Merrick, and Krutha—set out toward the ancient ruins of Argynvostholt, following the Old Svalich Road to the Luna River, where they took a small boat south. They arrived in the early evening to find the ruined fortress looming over a broken landing, its shattered towers and blackened stone hinting at the ancient battle fought there centuries ago.

Near the ruins they encountered an old orc woman known as Granny, who offered to help guide them through the dangerous grounds. Signs around the fortress suggested something unnatural: no wildlife, massive claw marks in the stone, and worn carvings depicting the silver dragon Argynvost and the knights who once served him. The carvings and ancient records revealed the history of the fortress—Argynvost and his Order once stood against Strahd, nearly defeating him before the Stormraven sisters struck from the flanks. Argynvost was slain and his head taken as a trophy, while Seraphine later cursed the fallen knights, leaving them bound as revenants to the ruined keep.

As the party approached the courtyard, four revenants emerged from the stone itself, mistaking the intruders for enemies of the Order. After a brief but brutal fight, the party entered the fortress and descended into the web-choked lower halls.

Inside, the ruins were filled with traps, undead horrors, and monstrous necro-spiders that had infested the abandoned chambers. The party navigated deadly hazards including trapped doors, poison dart corridors, and a flooding bathhouse while exploring the remains of the Order’s quarters, library, armory, and great hall. In the armory they recovered several relics of the fallen knights, including enchanted weapons, potions, and items bearing the dragon’s crest.

In the Dining Hall, the party encountered Sir Godfrey, a revenant knight of the Order. Recognizing the resemblance of Korith to the long-dead dragon Argynvost, he halted the fighting and shared the truth of the fortress. Long ago a wizard ally brought the Sun Sword here for safekeeping after Strahd’s forces hunted it down. Strahd destroyed the Order searching for the weapon but never found it. The blade remains sealed within the Meditation Room, though a terrible creature has since claimed the chamber.

Godfrey gave the party the key to the Meditation Room, asking only one thing in return: if they could recover Argynvost’s lost head and return it for burial, the spirits of the Order would finally find peace and aid the party in their war against Strahd.

The party entered the Meditation Room and faced the creature lurking within—a massive purple Spider Queen that had claimed the chamber as its nest. After a fierce battle lasting several rounds, the party defeated the monster. In the center of the chamber they discovered a sealed stone chest containing the broken fragments of the legendary Sun Sword, wrapped carefully in silver cloth.

The session ended with the party returning to the courtyard to make camp. As night fell, the spirits of the fallen knights emerged from the ruins—a dozen revenants standing silent watch over the party. Each bowed in respect, their attention lingering on Korith, whose likeness to Argynvost stirred hope among the cursed remnants of the Order.
