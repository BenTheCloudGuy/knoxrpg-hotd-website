# Session Summary

After completing their separate missions, the party reunited in Vallaki, taking time to regroup, resupply, and share what they had learned from their journeys. The session focused largely on role-play and preparation as the group began uncovering troubling signs that something darker was stirring within the city.

At the Blue Water Inn, the party reconnected with Urwin Martikov and the Keepers of the Feather, who continue quietly opposing Strahd’s influence in Barovia. The group spent time organizing supplies, selling equipment, and upgrading gear. Through a series of transactions with local merchants, including Iskander, they sold magical items and armor, leaving them with roughly 2,000 gold after expenses.

During this time, Thorian devoted himself to research at Wachterhaus, studying ancient texts connected to the fall of the Netherese Empire. His work uncovered references to an ancient puzzle written in Netherese cuneiform, tied to verses about Tharizdun and the construction of a mysterious Great Machine. The puzzle appears to require placing ancient verses in the correct order, suggesting deeper arcane secrets may yet be hidden in Barovia.

Later that evening, the party received a letter from Ireena Kolyana, who had troubling news from the Burgomaster’s Estate. The Vistani children had been safely returned to their people, but the Vistani demanded answers and asked the party to visit their seer soon. Ireena also shared growing concerns about the strange behavior within the Burgomaster’s household.

She described unsettling events that had occurred during her stay:

One night she awoke to find her door mysteriously open despite locking it earlier.

She has heard strange chanting from the basement, laced with dark arcane energy.

A maid who angered Lady Lydia Vallakovich during dinner disappeared the following morning, and no one in the household will speak of her.

Concerned for Ireena’s safety, the party went to check on her at the Burgomaster’s Estate. What they found only deepened their suspicions. Baron Vargas Vallakovich appeared unstable and oblivious to the darker activities around him, while Lady Lydia seemed tied to unsettling cult-like practices meant to keep Strahd from harming the city.

While visiting the estate, the party noticed another disturbing sign: the Holy Symbol of Ravenkind began vibrating when they crossed the estate threshold, suggesting powerful or corrupt magic may be present within the manor.

Recognizing that Vallaki may be hiding something dangerous, the group began forming a plan. With the Festival of the Blazing Sun scheduled for the following day, the party intends to use the chaos of the celebration as cover to investigate the estate more closely.

Before leaving, they ensured Ireena received one of their Sending Stones, allowing her to contact them if anything went wrong.

The session ended with the party preparing for their next move in Vallaki—gathering information, watching the Burgomaster’s household carefully, and planning a possible infiltration of the estate during the coming festival.
