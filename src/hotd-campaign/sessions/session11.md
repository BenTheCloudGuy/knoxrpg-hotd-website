# Session Summary

	You laid Father Donavich’s grief bare when you discovered his son, Duro, bound in the church’s undercroft. Once a brave youth who fought beside Mordenkainen in the doomed assault on Castle Ravenloft, he now bore the curse of vampirism, turned by Seraphine Stormraven. Donavich revealed that only by destroying Seraphine could both Duro and Krutha be saved from their curse. With heavy hearts, you agreed to take on this grim task.
	
	From there, you traveled to the Burgomaster’s estate, where you met Ismark and his sister, Ireena. They told you of the failed assault on Ravenloft—the wizard’s near-victory, Strahd’s momentary hesitation upon seeing Ireena, and the slaughter that followed. Both siblings carry deep scars from that day: Ismark wracked with guilt for Duro’s fate, Ireena haunted by Strahd’s obsession and the echo of a name whispered on his lips—Tatyana.
	
	Kolyan Indirovich, their father and Burgomaster, had recently succumbed to grief and illness. You bore his coffin through the streets in a somber procession, joined by villagers who whispered of Strahd’s shadow over them all. At the churchyard, Father Donavich delivered final rites, committing Kolyan to the earth with words of faith and defiance.
	
	Afterward, Ismark pressed you to escort Ireena to Vallaki—a walled town to the west where she might find safety beyond Strahd’s immediate reach. With night falling, you agreed to rest at the Burgomaster’s estate before beginning the journey. Along the way, you crossed paths with familiar faces—the eerie old woman selling pies, Vistani women from the inn, and villagers burdened with fear.
	
	The next morning, you set out. The road was quiet until dusk, when the lights of a Vistani camp came into view. Relief was short-lived: the long, mournful howls of wolves echoed from the forest behind you, closing in fast…
	
	And that is where we ended—your party racing toward the safety of the camp as the pack descends.
