# Session Summary

After leaving the Village of Barovia with Ismark and Ireena, the party began their journey west toward Vallaki along the Old Svalich Road. Their goal was to reach the nearby Vistani encampment at Tser Pool, where they planned to rest before continuing deeper into the valley.

As dusk settled and the road wound through the dark forests, the party could see the distant lanterns and hear faint music drifting from the Vistani camp ahead. The moment of relief was short-lived. From the woods behind them came the unmistakable sound of howling wolves.

Within moments, shapes burst from the treeline—werewolves, nearly a dozen of them, racing through the mist in pursuit.

What followed was a desperate chase through the forest road. The riders urged their mounts forward while the pack closed in from all sides, snapping at horses and riders alike. Branches whipped through the darkness, fallen logs blocked the road, and the thick fog threatened to swallow the path entirely. Several times the wolves nearly pulled riders from their saddles, but the party fought back where they could, striking at the beasts and buying precious seconds.

The chase became a race for survival as the lights of the Vistani camp grew closer.

In the final stretch, the party burst from the treeline onto the hill overlooking Tser Pool. The Vistani, alerted by the noise and howls, rushed to defend their camp. Muskets cracked and arrows flew into the darkness, the sudden blaze of firelight forcing the werewolves to recoil.

The pack circled briefly at the edge of the light before retreating back into the mist-shrouded forest.

Exhausted but alive, the party finally reached the safety of the Vistani wagons, where they could rest after the brutal ride through the night. The road to Vallaki still lay ahead, but for now they had found temporary refuge among the travelers of Tser Pool.
