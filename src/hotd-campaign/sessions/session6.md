# Session Summary

As the players approach the Citadel they hear the screams of Mistress Allyson Clarissa.  
In the ruined chamber beneath the Citadel of Valls, Mistress Clarissa Allyson—leader of the Order of the Yellow Rose—is forced to her knees, iron chains binding her wrists and collar. Despite her battered condition, her posture remains defiant. Behind her is a creature with black tendrils of energy crackling from his fingers to the mind of Mistress Allyson and LeJean, the local Constable standing next to Elysra
Lady Elysra Williams — leans over her.
The succubus appears almost compassionate at first glance: pale fingers brushing the blood from Clarissa’s hair, her expression soft, sympathetic even. But her eyes burn with fanatic intensity. A faint violet glow curls around her hand like smoke as abyssal power gathers beneath the surface.
Behind them stands a dark, half-seen figure—an executioner or tormentor—waiting for her command.
Clarissa, chained and kneeling on the cold stone floor, struggles to breathe but still manages to whisper:
	“To free the Chained God would be the end of all things…”
Elysra composure cracks. Her eyes flare with madness. The chamber fills with her voice:
	“YES! To shatter the wheel of suffering… to burn this wretched tapestry to ash!”
Then she softens again, kneeling close to Clarissa, almost pleading.
	“Just tell me where the Key is.”
She flicks her wrist. The shadow behind them moves forward continuing his work.. Clarissa screams. And the party moves in.. 

As they run in, Elysra releases a wall of Arcane energy at the party throwing them back.. Dagger made is way around to free the imprisoned priestesses.. As the players are picking themselves up, The creature behind Mistress Allyson finally looks up.. "We have it Mistress, we should go…" Then Elysra waves her hand and opens a portal, in which the players can see ruins and woods… Elysra turns and draws a dark dagger and plunges it into the LeJeans stomach.. "That is for your betrayal! Fool".. Then she vanishes through the portal and it flashes out of existence. The party watches as the dagger melts into LeJean and he begins to grow, morph, turn into something out of nightmares.. As 40' tall monstrosity with a 20' long whip that looks to be made of a large spine.  His rib cage forms an iron jail with souls screaming. 

The party eventually manage to bring the creature down. And rush to free the survivors of the Order of the Yellow Rose.
