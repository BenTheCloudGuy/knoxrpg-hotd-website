# Session Summary

The journey south aboard the Sea Spray began quietly as the crew settled into life on the river. For several days the voyage passed without incident while the party grew familiar with Captain Reaven’s crew and the rhythms of travel through the misty northern waterways. As the ship entered Bloodstone Pass, however, strange shapes were occasionally seen moving through the fog along the riverbanks—an unsettling sign that the lands of Damara were not as peaceful as they once were.

Late one night the calm was shattered when two terrifying creatures known as Night Screamers burst from the mist and attacked the ship. Their sonic shrieks paralyzed Sage while the rest of the party fought desperately to keep the creatures from destroying the vessel. Amid the chaos, water began flooding the lower decks as the ship was driven toward the rocky shore. Steel, magic, and desperate courage eventually brought the monsters down, but the Sea Spray was left badly damaged and the crew shaken by the sudden assault.

As the survivors struggled to gather themselves in the eerie silence that followed, two battered figures emerged from the fog along the riverbank. One man supported the other, who was gravely wounded and barely conscious. Their cloaks bore the sigil of Damaran royalty, and the elder of the two urgently begged for aid. The injured man was revealed to be Prince Ian Frostmantle, heir of the ruling house.

When Sage attempted to heal the prince, she was overwhelmed by strange and disturbing visions—glimpses of ruined lands, dead giants, and an alien sea filled with glowing orbs pressing against her mind. Though she resisted whatever force tried to reach her, the experience left her shaken.

Before collapsing back into unconsciousness, the fevered prince and his companion revealed that they had been ambushed near Valls and that something terrible had happened there. The party was given a map and instructions to travel to Valls and seek out Mistress Clarissa Allyson, a leader of the Order of the Yellow Rose, in hopes of discovering what had unleashed the darkness now creeping across the land.
