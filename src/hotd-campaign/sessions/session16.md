# Session Summary

Following their victory over Seraphine Stormraven, the party resumed their journey west along the Old Svalich Road, escorting Ireena Kolyana toward Vallaki. Two Vistani children rescued earlier—Toma and his younger sister Riya—traveled with them, clinging to Ireena for safety after the horrors they had endured.

During the journey, Gregor experienced a strange encounter when a lone raven perched above the road reached out to him with a strange, almost telepathic presence. Through symbolic images and impressions, the raven delivered warnings about Vallaki’s ruler Baron Vallakovich, the dangerous noble Fiona Wachter, and the importance of protecting Ireena—the “Heart.” The message also hinted at allies waiting at the Blue Water Inn and pointed toward a mysterious tower near Lake Zarovich, where a powerful but broken mind might aid them.

As night fell, the party approached the crooked windmill known as Old Bonegrinder. There they were greeted by a nervous woman named Offalia, who reluctantly allowed the weary travelers inside for warmth and food. Though she attempted to appear hospitable, her uneasy demeanor and protective stance around parts of the windmill quickly raised suspicion. Careful observation revealed signs that something was wrong: children’s footprints around the mill, clothing hidden in grain sacks, and strange evidence of violence around the grinding machinery.

While exploring the upper levels of the windmill, the party discovered the truth—a frightened Vistani girl named Arabelle, who had been abducted several days earlier. She revealed that one of the women of the mill had lured her away from camp with toys before locking her inside. The discovery confirmed the party’s fears that the windmill’s occupants were involved in something sinister.

Reuniting Arabelle with the other rescued children created a powerful emotional moment, reinforcing the urgency of getting them all to safety. With the truth uncovered and danger clearly looming, the party prepared to confront the dark forces lurking within the windmill.

The session culminated with the battle against the Bonegrinder hag, marking the beginning of the party’s confrontation with the sinister witches who prey upon Barovia’s children.
