# Session Summary

Krutha and Korith hacked their way through patchwork goblins, pushing toward Vasilka on the southern flank. Korith sacrificed his summoned steed and used his flight to launch himself over Vasilka, landing a midair cannonball on her head. She tried to dodge but wasn't quick enough, taking a grievous wound from the impact. As soon as Korith hit the ground, patchwork goblins swarmed him to defend their master. Krutha saw his brother in trouble and rushed forward, stomping through goblins as he tried to tackle Vasilka. He rolled a natural 1. Twice. Vasilka saw him coming, grabbed the big orc by the loincloth, and hurled him (minus the loincloth) straight at Korith. Korith dodged the orc-shaped missile, but as he moved to counterattack, Vasilka shoved Krutha's loincloth into his mouth. Korith fell back retching. Krutha, meanwhile, was more than happy to keep fighting in the buff.

To the north, Gregor, Brother Ivan, and Dagger were dealing with more patchwork goblins pushing in. Dagger took to a nearby rooftop while Ivan and Gregor held the line, with Gregor putting his horde buster skills to work. Mid-fight, Gregor received a telepathic warning from Brom Martikov about more creatures approaching from the woods. Brom dropped from the sky, shifted to human form, and opened fire on the goblins attacking Ivan. Gregor eventually pushed forward to support Ivan while Dagger fell back into the defensive ring around the wagons. Ivan cast Wall of Fire between the larger horde and the hilltop defenders, keeping the line from being overrun.

Thorian and Merrick kept hurling spells at Vasilka and the goblin hordes. A fireball wiped out a large cluster of goblins. Merrick's Firestorm was dispelled by Vasilka. Both casters were running on fumes, but the combined damage from the spellcasters and the two warriors on the southern flank forced Vasilka to retreat under invisibility. As she disappeared, the defenders finally started gaining ground against the patchwork goblins as their numbers thinned out.

That's when a large group of mongrelfolk and a massive hulking beast carrying a catapult emerged from the tree line. The catapult was loaded and ready to fire as the mongrelfolk charged up the hill. That's where the session ended. Vasilka invisible and somewhere on the battlefield. Krutha, nude from the waist down, still swinging beside Korith. Merrick and Thorian catching their breath. Dagger, Ivan, and Gregor holding the north side.
