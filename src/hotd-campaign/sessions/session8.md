# Session Summary

After recovering from the battle at the Citadel of Valls, the party is aided by Mistress Clarissa Allyson, who uses what strength she has left to teleport them near an ancient druid grove. Her hope is that they might reach Brother Caelis before Lady Elysra Williams and her allies can ambush him at the ruins of the Abbey of the Yellow Rose.

As the party approaches the grove, they quickly realize they are already too late to prevent bloodshed. The grove had been attacked shortly after Brother Caelis arrived. Undead creatures had descended upon the druids, accompanied by a mysterious woman who called herself Seraphine Stormraven. Through dark and unnatural magic, Seraphine corrupted several of the druids, twisting them into monstrous servants who turned against their own kin as well as Brother Caelis.

The party fights through the aftermath of the attack and manages to defeat the remaining twisted druids, bringing an end to the immediate threat. With the grove secured, the group takes shelter for the night.

During their rest, they meet Velinka d’Avenir, a Vistani woman who now lives among the druids. Around the campfire she recounts the night her caravan was slaughtered by the Stormraven Sisters, the same enemies now moving in the shadows of Damara. Her story deepens the party’s understanding of the enemy they face and the long trail of suffering left in the sisters’ wake.

An agreement is made that night. The party will help Velinka confront the growing corruption among the druids, while she in turn will help guide them through the Devil’s Mist. As a member of the Vistani, Velinka possesses the rare ability to move through the cursed mists that surround Barovia.

At dawn the following morning, Velinka performs a final ritual of travel magic. In a swirl of ancient druidic power, the party is transported to the very edge of the Devil’s Mist, where the dark valley beyond waits. Before them lies Barovia. The path forward leads into the land of Strahd.
