# Session Summary

After the attack on the Festival of Light, Thorian finally rejoined the party and caught everyone up on what he found in Wachterhaus. What he uncovered was pointed to something being hidden in the mountains to the South. Tensions were still high in town. At one point Gregor tried to punch the Burgomaster, only to be stopped by Imp before he could actually land the blow. The 3 noble women's little "Book Club/Occult Play" had managed to summon and trap probably a 1000 demons in Barovia due to the restrictions of the mist. 

The Burgomasters made it clear they had bigger problems in front of them at that moment. They told the party they would meet them later that evening at the Blue Water Inn, after they had done what they could to calm the citizens and get Vallaki under control. From there, the party made its way back to the Blue Water Inn, talking through what Thorian had learned and what it meant for the town, for Fiona, and for whatever had really been going on behind closed doors in Vallaki all these years.

When they arrived, they found Lady Williams already waiting for them.

She made her offer plainly. She said she would help the party defeat Strahd so that she could finally be free of Barovia herself. She only wants the Key that Mistress Clarrissa had sent out before the city fell. She confirmed she had tricked Fiona into summoning her into Barovia, she did it to find the key.. What Lady Williams had not understood at the time was that the Mists would trap her here too.

That offer split the party.

Korith and Gregor wanted no part of the deal. The others were more willing to at least hear her out. Then Merrick raised the real problem: if Fiona was the one who summoned Lyzara into Barovia in the first place, then Fiona may still hold some measure of control over her. That turned the whole conversation. Both sides agreed that Lady Williams could NOT be trusted, but better to have her think they were allies. 

By the end of the session, the party had more answers than before, but none of them made things simpler. The town itself was still trying to recover from open violence, and to top it off - Ireena appears to have vanished with Lady Williams..
