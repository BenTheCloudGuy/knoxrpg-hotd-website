# Werewolf den


Kiara Tormanescu — Den Mother (Druid/Healer), Jiro's grandmother. Approaches the party peacefully. 

Duesius Tormanescu — Kiara's husband, Jiro's grandfather. Very protective of family.

Jiro Toranescu — A young werewolf boy who lost his father Emil and older brothers to human hunters, and his mother Emil now leads the Den. She was believed killed by the Players at the Fight at Wizard Tower after she jumped from the tower window to escape them. 

Kiril Stoyanovich - Alpha Werewolf that looks to lead the Den after the death of Zuleika Torancscu. He is brutal and full of hatred toward the people of Vallaki who killed his pack when he was young. The Toranscu pack took him into their den after that and raised and provided for him. But he believes that Zuleika is weak and that Kiril should lead the Den and all the packs that reside in the den. Knowing he could not defeat Zelueika if he challenged him for leadership, he instead plotted with Strahd aid Elandra Stormraven to her people ambush Zuleika while he in some of his men were out hunting. They made it appear as if Human Hunters from Vallaki had done it. This allowed Stoyanvich to take over the Den and the 12 packs (tribes) in the Den. He had the wolf packs attacking without mercy anyone they could, throwing themselves against the towns and farms. This led to the deaths of many Werewolves, and eventually the Den rose up and expelled Kiril in favor of Emil Toranescu (Zuleikka's Mate) who was a respected warrior and shaman(Druid) in her own Pack. This enraged Kiril and many of the warriors followed Kiril out into the wilds. Kiril and his newly formed pack call themselves "Blood Hunters" and server Lord Strahd without question. Currently there are close to 30 members of the Blood Hunters from various packs who left the den - most are warriors who have lost their loved ones to human hunters and want revenge. It was the Blood Hunters that attacked the players outside of the Tser Pool Vistani under orders from Elandra Stormraven, to test their capabilities. 

Emil Toranescu - The leader of the Den. She like her husbands parents (Kiara and Duesius) due not want to continue the war with the humans. They feel they have lost so many loved ones and too much of their humanity already. She feared to betray Strahd and his emmisarry Elandra Stormraven, so when Elandra came to the Den and told her to retrieve some artifacts from the Wizards tower and to kill the people there. She didn't want to do it, but knew to resist Strahd was certain death for all her people, includeing her small pup Jiro. Unknown to the players and the Den - she survived the fight, and her jump. After the players had left the Den the morning after the battle with Elandra Stormraven, she in about 8 other wounded from the battle came back to learn of the deal that Kiara and Duesius made with the players and the defeat of three Stormraven Sisters.. She plans to honor the agreement as she hopes the players will be able to defeat Strahd and give the packs a chance as peace. 

Zuleika Toranescu was the Pack leader of the Toranescu pack and Den Leader (Leader of all the packs in Barovia). He was a capable warrior and just leader. But he did not hate the humans as his mate was once a human who was turned. He would do what was best for the Den and his Pack, but he tried to reign in the elements in the Den that wanted to kill for the sake of killing. He was killed along with his hunters by Elandra Stormraven and Strahds personal guard, dressed as Humans from Vallaki.. Along side Zuleika that day was his Eldest Son Ekrol who died in next to his father. 

Currently there are 10 packs in Barovia
- Toransescu - At the Den under leaderhip of Emil, her pack is among the smallest with just 6 survivors. 
- Blood Hunters - Under the leaderhip of Kiril Stoyanovich and Roming Barovia in Service of Strahd
- Volkov - The eldest and first Pack. At the den under the leaderhip of Chekov Volokov a grey haired grizzled werewolf who believe honor and his word are the only things his kind have left. There are about a dozen in his pack. 
- Petrescu - The Petrescu under the guidance of their shaman <female name> do not like hunting humans and instead act as smiths, farmers, butchers, watch the Den for the other packs. While they are able to fight, and will to defend the Den and the other packs, especially the young - they don't ever start the fight and would rather withdraw if possible. 
- Baranov - Part of the Den, but prefer to keep to themselves in the Mountains to the South away from the Human Settlements. This small pack is led by Grogu. They're stubborn and Territorial and do not share easily with the rest of the Packs. They've had a few skirmishes with the Dusk Elves of Tser Hill.
- Grozenscu - Not part of the Den or the Blood Hunters. They believe in Honor and a warriors Death. They only turn those that they feel are worthy and ask them for that honor. So while their pack under the leaderhip of Baba Yaga (a huge beast of a man and wolf) has never served Strahd, but also does not trust Humans as they have proven time and time again to be without honor. They see Zuleika and his Blood Hunters as honorless for the cowardly way the murder without cause and will kill them on sight. They currently have a small camp in the Northern Reaches and hunt game there.
- Lazarescu - This Reclusive pack with it's ties to death and rebirth; possibly spiritually connected to Mother Night. This is closes to the religious order that Werewolves have. They do not fight and instead teach the ways of the Night Mother to the Shaman of other packs. They do not have an Alpha, and instead allow any who wish to learn of the Night Mother to come and if they choose to stay, may join the pack and have a voice. Many of the other packs provide some of their warriros to protect the Lazarescu in exchange for their gifts of healing and other blessings of the Night Mother.
- Cernikov - This pack is known only as rumor. Their said to be part Demon, part Wolf. That the Alpha of the pack made a pact with a demon they had found wondering nn the wildes of Barovia.. it is said they gave up all their humanity in exchange for powers.. None of the this Cernikov (now called the Shaodow Pack) has been seen in almost a century. The truth is the handful that do live are twisted vistages of their formerselves and hunt anything that is not them. This includes, Animals, Humans, Werewolves, or Vampires. There madness makes them dangerous and uncontrollable. Strahd sent Seraphine and Rhahdin to deal them nearly a century ago and believes them to be wiped out. Truth is there is still a handful that remain. 
- Radescu - This pack prefers to live as Humans and only turn when the Night Mothers call comes on the full moon. They have a small village along the Luna River to the South and keep to themselves, trading through ordinary human fronts when they need supplies. They never reveal themselves to outsiders. They do follow the Den Leader and are good and honorable creatures. They're one of the largest packs in the Den.
