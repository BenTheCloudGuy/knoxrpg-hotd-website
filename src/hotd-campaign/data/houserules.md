# House Rules

## Table Rules

### 1. Be Kind to Everyone and Have Fun
This is rule number one for a reason! Everything else is secondary to having fun. 

### 2. The Solution is NOT on Your Character Sheet
Think creatively. Your abilities are tools, not the only answer.

### 3. Dice Can Fail
A good plan doesn't always require a roll!

### 4. Good for the Goose good for the Gander
Anything you all can do, I can do.. So keep that in mind when getting too creative with the rules.. I may very well allow it, but that mean it now applies to baddies as well. 

### 5. Inspiration Tokens
At the start of each session for any player at the table - you will get an Inspiration Token. These can be used to do the following: 
- 1 Token forces a re-roll. You may spend 1 token to reroll dice.
    - This can Attack Rolls, Savings Throws, Skill Checks, or Damage Dice
    - Can force DM to re-roll as well! Handy way to stop a crit in it's tracks. 
    > You must take the new roll regardless of results
- 2 Tokan allows the player to turn any D20 Roll to Max on the dice. If this results in a critical success, so be it! 
- Players can spend tokens on other players as well. 
- 2 Token is equal to 1 HD. So you can additional HD back into the HD pool for healing or other effects that use HD. 
- 2 Tokens will allow you to take an additional Action that turn.. This does NOT include taking an additional **Casting Action**.

> No more stacking 3+ tokens together to create massive crits - learned that leason after Dagger 1 shot Elandra Stormraven.. that is simply too powerful.. 

## Mechanic Rules

### 1. Critical Success / Critical Fail
Critical Success and Fail happen on a +10/-10 over the Target Number.

- **Crit Success:** MAX damage + Roll as normal.
- **Crit Fail:** Advantage on next attack(s) against you.

> This stacks. So if you get a +20 over creatures AC, then you would do 2x MAX Damage + Role as normal. On a fail You would have disadvantage and they would have advantage. 

### 2. Death / Dropped
When you fall below 0 HP, you gain 1 level of Exhaustion (each time). Dying is hard on the body, and should not be shrugged off. Remember people it is okay to run away or retreat when in over your head.. 

Exhaustion Effects:

| Level | Effect |
|:-----:|:-------|
| 1 | Disadvantage on ability checks |
| 2 | Speed halved |
| 3 | Disadvantage on attack rolls and saving throws |
| 4 | Hit point maximum halved |
| 5 | Speed reduced to 0 |
| 6 | Death |

> Remember these are cumulative.. 

### 3. Flanking
You are considered flanking if you or another ally are opposite the enemy. Your attacks have Advantage. If there are two or more allies, then they all have Advantage and the enemy has disadvantage on attacks 

### 4. Healing 

#### Magic Works Differently Than Potions

- **Potions** do FULL healing to show the stability of Alchemy over healing magic.
- **Healing Magic** uses Exploding Dice and represents the chaotic nature of Magic.
- Simple **Medicine checks** can be used to bring players to 0 HP and stop dying.

#### Recovery

Hit Dice are recovered 1 HD per 4hrs of rest (that means not doing anything strenuous), and any HD Spent is unavailable to be used for healing during short/long rests.  
- A player may only use a single HD during a Short Rest (1hr), if they have any left. 
- They may use upto 1/2 their available HD on a long rest (8hrs). 
- And may use all their HD for 24hrs of continous rest. A player may recover 1 HP + CON Bonus per short rest if they don't burn any HD. 

Exhaustion is recovered 1 level per 24hrs of rest with food and water, or via Lesser/Greater Restoration spells.

### 5. Magic

#### Overcasting

When a spellcaster has no spell slots remaining, or needs to cast at a higher level than their available slots, they can draw on their own life force to fuel the magic. This is called overcasting. It is dangerous, painful, and not something any sane mage does lightly.

- **Pay Hit Dice** equal to the spell level (or the difference if you consume a lower slot to offset the cost). Roll the expended HD; the total becomes necrotic damage you take. This damage cannot be reduced or prevented.
- **Constitution Save** at DC = 10 + (2 x spell level). On a success, you take half the rolled necrotic damage. On a failure, you take the full amount and gain 1 level of exhaustion.
- Each level of exhaustion you already have increases the DC by 2.
- You cannot overcast above 9th level, with 0 HD remaining, or at exhaustion level 5+.
- HD spent on overcasting are unavailable for healing. HD recover at 1 per 4hrs of rest.

> See the full [Overcasting](/overcasting) rules for details, examples, and Circle integration.

#### Casting Circles

Multiple spellcasters working together can pool their power to cast spells beyond the limit of any individual. One caster is designated "The Lead" and controls the spell. Circle members (anyone with Spellcasting or Pact Magic) contribute by expending spell slots within 30 feet of The Lead.

- Circle members can substitute slots for The Lead's own, letting them cast even with no slots remaining. Two slots of a given level combine to equal one slot of the next level up.
- Each contributing member grants one **Circle Enhancement** chosen by The Lead: Empower (bonus damage dice), Heighten (cast at a higher level), Widen (increase area), Extend (increase duration), Reach (increase range), or Fortify (increase save DC).
- Circle spells get +1 save DC per 2 contributing members (max +3).
- Once committed, members cannot voluntarily withdraw. Breaking free requires a Wisdom save (DC 12 + circle size) and collapses the spell.
- If The Lead loses Concentration, the circle collapses and all spent slots are lost.

> See the full [Circle Magic](/circle-magic) rules for enhancement details, slot conversion tables, and examples.

### 6. Initiative 

We don't roll of initiative and instead decide when we will go in a turn by declariing you have initiative and flipping your initiative tracker. As the GM, I can choose to take inititative from any player except those that have bonuses to their initiative from things like the Alert Feat. Bosses lair actions can still steal initiative regardless of feats. 

#### Group Initiative

Bad guys will work on grouped initiative allowing them to go in smaller groups vs individually - this will help speed up combat and reduce the overhead of me trying to remember which baddies went. 

> Bosses always act on their own Initiatives! 

#### Be Ready!

When take initiative you must be ready to declare your actions. if you are not - you will be considered hesitating and will lose your turn. After you declare initiative - you have 60sec to declare your actions and roll the dice. This is to keep combat fast since we have such a large table.
