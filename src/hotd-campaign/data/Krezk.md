# Krezk

A remote walled village in western Barovia, nestled at the base of the Abbey of Saint Markovia. Krezk is isolated, cautious, and self-sufficient — its gates remain closed to outsiders unless they can prove themselves useful. The village is small but well-defended, with a tight-knit community shaped by fear and pragmatism.

**Key Locations:** Abbey of Saint Markovia, Belview family experiments, Holy Pool of Krezk

## NPCs

### Baron Dmitri Krezkov (npcid 24)
- **Race:** Human
- **Class:** Noble / Warrior
- **Location:** Krezk
- **Status:** Alive
- **Alignment:** Ally

Baron Dmitri Krezkov is the burgomaster of Krezk. He is a cautious, protective leader who keeps Krezk's gates closed to outsiders, grudgingly allowing strangers in only if they can prove themselves useful to the village's survival. Despite his guarded nature, Dmitri is a good man who cares deeply for his people and maintains close friendships with the other burgomasters of Barovia.

Dmitri is close friends with Baron Vargas Vallakovich — they share counsel on protecting their people and have attended each other's festivals. Dmitri and Anna were honoured guests at the Festival of Light in Vallaki, where they watched the proceedings and reacted to events as they unfolded — including coming to the aid of the players when Ireena came looking for help after Lady Williams was waiting for them at the Blue Water Inn. He was also a friend of the late Kolyan Indirovich, whose death hit Dmitri hard.

DM: Dmitri's greatest sorrow is the recent death of his young daughter Ilya, which has left both him and Anna devastated. This grief may make him susceptible to desperate bargains — particularly any offer that promises to restore his duaghter. He does not know that the Abbot may have used Ilya's body or essence in the creation of Vasilka. He does not trust the Abbot, but also knows that there is little he can do about him. His creatures the locals deride as "stitches" or "mongle-folk" are foced to stay on the Abbey grounds. If he sees Vasilka - he would recognize his daughters face/essence. 


The party visited Krezk in Sessions 22–23 while recovering the Amulet of Ravenkind from the Abbey.

### Anna Krezkova (npcid 87)
- **Race:** Human
- **Class:** Noble
- **Location:** Krezk
- **Status:** Alive
- **Alignment:** Ally

Dmitri's wife and the steady hand behind the Krezkov household. She is grieving the death of their child Ilya but remains practical and steady — the kind of person who acts when others freeze. 

At the Festival of Light in Vallaki, Anna and Dmitri were honoured guests watching the proceedings with the other burgomasters and their families. When Strahd attacked, Anna was the one who physically got Lady Fiona Wachter moving after Fiona broke down following Stella's death. While others froze, Anna did not hesitate and continues to help Fiona Grieve having lost her child recently. 

Anna leads the Krezk contingent alongside Dmitri and is a respectable sorceress having studied with Fiona. She is a practical ally — calm, observant, and steady under pressure.

**Associations:** Baron Dmitri Krezkov (24), Lady Fiona Wachter (11), Baron Vallakovich (10)

### The Abbot (npcid 25)
- **Race:** Human
- **Class:** Cleric
- **Location:** Abbey of Saint Markovia, Krezk
- **Status:** Evil
- **Alignment:** Neutral

The Abbot is the enigmatic master of the Abbey of Saint Markovia above Krezk. He appears as a serene, handsome man who heals the sick and tends to the abbey's inhabitants — a collection of strange, misshapen creatures called mongrelfolk by him, but the townsfolk call them stitches. He radiates an otherworldly calm and speaks of saving Strahds soul with uneasing certainty. 

DM Notes: 
**True Nature:** The Abbot is a deva — an angelic being who descended to Barovia centuries ago to bring hope to its people. He restored the Abbey and healed the sick, but centuries of exposure to Barovia's corruption and the Dark Powers twisted his mind. He now creates grotesque mongrelfolk from body parts and is building a "perfect bride" for Strahd — a flesh golem named Vasilka — believing this will end the curse. The Abbot's fall from grace is one of Barovia's greatest tragedies. He used the body and Essence of Ilya to create the creature he calls Vasilka. 

**Post-Session Events:** Strahd attacked the Abbey, offended by the very notion he would ever love Vasilka. With Vasilka gravely wounded, the Abbot went mad with rage. The Belview mongrelfolk feed off the Abbot's rage, becoming feral and dangerous. Using the power of the Holy Symbol of Ravenkind - he was able to restore Vasilka, who wore it as a present from the one she thinks of as her Faither/Creator. During Sessions 22–23, the Abbot forced the players to prove themselves by summoning a massive Flesh Golem. After a brutal battle that dropped Ivan and Dagger, the Abbot kept his word, ripped the amulet from Vasilka's neck, and handed it to Ivan. This act caused Vasilka to drop to the ground.. And when she woke she was angered that these people would force her Father to give up such a powerful relic. After that Lady Williams came to the Abbot and told them that Strahd was betrayed by the Vistani and wishes them all to pay. That they're to take his followers and attack the Vistani at Tser Raven Vistani, then move on to the Tser Hill Vistani. They overwhelmed the Vistani at Tser Raven in the middle of the night, killing any they could and burning their wagons. The surviving members fled into the woods or to shelter in Krezk. After that Vasilka took the horde to attack the Tser Hill Encampment. (This is the current battle the players are in right now).  

**Tser Hill Attack (Sessions 27–28):** The Abbot later sent Vasilka and her Patchwork Goblins to attack the Vistani encampment at Tser Hill. His plan was to wipe out the Tser Hill Vistani and frame the players and their allies. He would then offer an alliance to Strahd to deal with the players, if Strahd agreed to take Vasilka as his bride. The Abbot will eventually show up in person — he is by far the biggest threat and will come to extract Vasilka when she begins taking damage.

### Vasilka
- **Race:** Undead/Construct
- **Class:** Sorceress
- **Location:** Abbey of Saint Markovia
- **Status:** Active
- **Alignment:** Forces of Dark

A flesh golem bride being created by the Abbot for Strahd. Also referred to as "the Resurrected Ilya Krezkov" — suggesting she was created using Ilya's body or essence. The Abbot thinks of Vasilka as his daughter, an innocent child made to appease Strahd.

**Combat (Sessions 27–28):** Vasilka led an assault on the Tser Hill Vistani camp with Patchwork Goblins and Direwolves. She demonstrated terrifying power — taking control of Thorian's water spell and hurling it back over the defenders. She has what feels like an unlimited supply of Mongrelfolk/Patchwork Goblins at her disposal.

DM Notes:
A Powerful Undead/Construct Sorceress — Lair Actions and special combat actions. She is feircly loyal to the Abbot and Strahd as she created to serve him.
