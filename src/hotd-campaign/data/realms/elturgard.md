# Elturgard

![Map: Sword Coast](../../images/realms/map-02.010-sword-coast.jpg)

> **At a Glance:** This cosmopolitan region is famous for its large cities and the Realms-shattering menaces that threaten them.
> **Region:** Sword Coast
> **Languages:** Chondathan

The realm of Elturgard encompasses Elturel, Triel, Scornubel, Soubar, and Berdusk. It also includes much of the surrounding land, including the Fields of the Dead. Once a shining kingdom, Elturgard fell into disarray when its capital Elturel was dragged into the Nine Hells and back again. Even after the capital’s return, Elturgard struggles to pick up the pieces. Some of Elturgard’s outer cities have attempted to secede from the increasingly theocratic realm. Elturel keeps them close by threatening to withdraw the protection of the Hellriders, a cavalry force that keeps Elturgard’s loyal cities safe from danger.

Elturgard is governed by the High Observer, a position currently held by Thavius Kreeg, who was instrumental in the city's initial descent into Avernus. The government is heavily influenced by religious leaders, particularly those devoted to the Triad (Torm, Tyr, and Ilmater). The region is known for its strict adherence to law and order, a necessity after the chaos of its recent history. The geography of Elturgard is diverse, with fertile lands near the River Chionthar and the ominous Fields of the Dead to the north.

## Notable Locations

### Elturel

Elturel is the capital of Elturgard and its largest city. After its return from Avernus, the city has been struggling with the remnants of its infernal ordeal. The people have become more pious, desperate to ensure their afterlives send them to the Upper Planes. This has created new problems, as the paranoid populace now distrusts and harasses tieflings, blaming them for the city’s fall. Fearing for their safety, Elturel’s tieflings have fled in droves for Baldur’s Gate. The city is also home to the Hellriders, an elite cavalry force that is both revered and feared.

### Triel

Triel is a small town located along the River Chionthar. It serves as a crucial trade stop between Elturel and the western cities. The town is known for its strong community spirit and its dedication to the Triad. However, it has been struggling with increased bandit activity since the fall of Elturel, as the Hellriders are stretched thin.

### Scornubel

Known as the "Caravan City," Scornubel is a bustling trade hub where merchants from across Faerun gather. It is a city of contrasts, with wealthy traders living alongside impoverished laborers. The city has a reputation for being a place where anything can be bought or sold, which has attracted a fair share of unsavory characters. Despite this, Scornubel remains a vital part of Elturgard's economy.

### Soubar

Soubar is a rough-and-tumble town located near the Fields of the Dead. It is known for its rowdy taverns and as a gathering place for adventurers seeking fortune in the dangerous lands to the north. The town has a reputation for lawlessness, which has only increased as Elturgard's central authority has weakened.

### Berdusk

Berdusk, often called the "Jewel of the Vale," is a city of culture and learning. It is home to several renowned bardic colleges and libraries. The city is a center for the arts and is known for its beautiful architecture and vibrant festivals. Berdusk has remained relatively stable, thanks to its strong local leadership and a focus on cultural preservation.

### Fields of the Dead

This vast, rolling plain is littered with ancient battlefields and the remnants of long-forgotten conflicts. It is a place of both historical significance and danger, as undead creatures are known to rise from the ground. The Fields of the Dead serve as a grim reminder of the region's turbulent past and a challenge for those who dare to explore its haunted expanses.

### The Companion

The Companion is the radiant sphere that once hovered over Elturel, now a darkened reminder of the city's infernal past. Though no longer active, it remains a symbol of the city's trials and is a focal point for religious ceremonies. The Companion's presence continues to influence the spiritual and political landscape of Elturgard.

### The High Hall

The High Hall is the seat of government in Elturel, where the High Observer and the council of priests convene. It is a fortress-like structure that also serves as a place of worship for the Triad. The High Hall is a symbol of the city's resilience and its commitment to law and order.

### River Chionthar

This river is a vital trade route that connects Elturgard to the Sword Coast. It is heavily patrolled by the Hellriders to protect against piracy and banditry. The River Chionthar is also a source of life for the region, providing water and fertile lands for agriculture.

### The Hellrider Barracks

Located in Elturel, these barracks are the training ground for the Hellriders. The barracks are a testament to the martial prowess and discipline of Elturgard's elite cavalry. They are also a place of recruitment and training for new members, ensuring the continued protection of the realm.
