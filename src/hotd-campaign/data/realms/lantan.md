# Lantan

![Map: Trackless Sea](../../images/realms/map-02.011-lantan.jpg)

> **At a Glance:** Each island realm is its own small, self-contained pocket of culture, from the ancient traditions of the Moonshaes to innovative Lantan.
> **Region:** Trackless Sea
> **Languages:** Lantanese, Illuskan, Chondathan

Lantan is a secluded archipelago in the Trackless Sea, known for its technological advancements and devotion to Gond, the god of invention. The islands are governed by the Ayrorch, a theocratic council composed of twelve high-ranking members of the Church of Gond, many of whom are gnomes. The gnomish population plays a significant role in Lantan's culture and technological progress. Historically, Lantan has maintained a policy of isolation, which has allowed it to develop unique technologies such as smokepowder, firearms, and even submarines. Despite their isolation, Lantanese traders occasionally venture to mainland port cities like Baldur’s Gate and Waterdeep, exchanging their inventions for precious metals and resources scarce on the islands.

## Notable Locations

### Lantanese Inventions

Lantan is renowned for its cutting-edge technological innovations. The islanders have developed explosive smokepowder, parachutes, firearms, and submarines. These inventions are closely guarded secrets, as the Lantanese fear that widespread knowledge could weaken their trading power. The island's exports are carefully controlled, with only small shipments reaching the mainland to ensure that their technological edge remains unchallenged.

### Northern Lantan

The northern island of Lantan is the political and spiritual heart of the archipelago. Sambar, the capital city, hosts the Ayrorch and serves as the administrative center. Nearby, the city of Illul is home to the High Holy Crafthouse of Inspiration, a grand temple dedicated to Gond. This temple is a hub of religious and technological activity, led by a high artificer who oversees the spiritual and inventive pursuits of the island.

### Orlil

Orlil is a seemingly barren island that conceals a network of Underdark tunnels inhabited by Lantanese deep gnomes, or svirfneblin. These gnomes have lived in isolation from the drow and other Underdark threats, allowing their communities to thrive in peace. The deep gnomes of Orlil contribute to Lantan's technological prowess, often working on innovations in secret.

### Southern Lantan

Southern Lantan is the gateway for outsiders wishing to engage in trade with the islanders. The port city of Anchoril serves as the primary point of contact for foreign merchants. While the people of Southern Lantan are as inventive as their northern counterparts, they intentionally downplay their technological advancements to protect the secrets of Lantan. This strategic deception helps maintain the island's advantage in trade and innovation.

### Sambar

Sambar is the bustling capital of Lantan, where the Ayrorch convenes to govern the realm. The city is a center of invention and religious devotion, with many workshops dedicated to Gond's teachings. Sambar's architecture reflects the island's technological advancements, featuring intricate mechanisms and devices integrated into everyday life.

### Illul

Illul is a vibrant city known for the High Holy Crafthouse of Inspiration. This temple is not only a place of worship but also a center for learning and experimentation. Artificers, engineers, and priests collaborate here to push the boundaries of invention, ensuring that Lantan remains at the forefront of technological progress.

### Anchoril

Anchoril, located on Southern Lantan, is the primary trading hub for the archipelago. The city's docks are busy with ships from the mainland, and its markets are filled with Lantanese goods. Despite its openness to trade, Anchoril maintains a careful balance, revealing only enough of its technological prowess to entice traders without compromising Lantan's secrets.

### The Underdark Tunnels of Orlil

Beneath the surface of Orlil lies a complex network of tunnels inhabited by svirfneblin. These tunnels are a marvel of engineering, with hidden workshops and living quarters designed to support the deep gnome communities. The svirfneblin here are known for their expertise in mining and gem cutting, skills that complement Lantan's technological focus.

Lantan's unique blend of isolation, innovation, and devotion to Gond has allowed it to develop a culture unlike any other in Faerun. The islanders' commitment to maintaining their technological edge ensures that Lantan remains a mysterious and intriguing realm to outsiders.
