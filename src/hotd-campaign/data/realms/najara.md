# Najara

![Map: Sword Coast](../../images/realms/map-02.010-sword-coast.jpg)

> **At a Glance:** This cosmopolitan region is famous for its large cities and the Realms-shattering menaces that threaten them.
> **Region:** Sword Coast
> **Languages:** Chondathan

The ancient realm of Najara stretches across the Serpent Hills, the Marsh of Chelimber, and the Forest of Wyrms. It is a land dominated by the yuan-ti, nagas, and lizardfolk. The society of Najara is characterized by its cold, calculating nature, with a rigid hierarchy led by powerful serpentine beings. The realm remained hidden from the wider world for centuries, until its current ruler, the spirit naga Jarant, decided to reveal its existence to neighboring regions. Under Jarant's rule, Najara has become a growing threat, with its ambitions largely unknown to outsiders. The Lords’ Alliance and Elturgard have been preoccupied with other concerns, allowing Najara to expand its influence quietly. This serpent kingdom is poised to strike when the time is right, leveraging its strategic position and the power of its reptilian inhabitants.

## Notable Locations

### Ss’thar’tiss’ssun

Ancient and evil, the spirit naga Jarant rules the realm of Najara from its capital city Ss’thar’tiss’ssun with the aid of a silver circlet called the *Marlspire of Najara.* This artifact grants Jarant powerful abjuration magic and control over reptilian creatures, including the first ruler of the kingdom, Terpenzi, now a powerful bone naga called the Guardian of Najara. The city is a dark and foreboding place, filled with serpentine architecture and guarded by loyal yuan-ti and lizardfolk.

### Marsh of Chelimber

The Marsh of Chelimber is a vast swamp that serves as a natural barrier and a resource-rich area for Najara. It is home to numerous dangerous creatures, including hydras and will-o’-wisps, making it a difficult place for intruders to navigate. The marsh is also rumored to contain ancient ruins and treasures from past civilizations, drawing the interest of adventurers and scholars alike.

### Serpent Hills

The Serpent Hills are a rugged and inhospitable region, dotted with ancient ruins and hidden lairs. It is a place of great significance to the yuan-ti, who consider it sacred. The hills are also home to various tribes of lizardfolk and other reptilian creatures who serve the naga rulers of Najara. Adventurers often explore the hills in search of lost artifacts and forgotten magic.

### Forest of Wyrms

The Forest of Wyrms is a dense and dangerous woodland that marks the southern border of Najara. It is named for the many dragon-like creatures that are said to inhabit its depths. The forest is a place of mystery and danger, with many hidden paths and ancient secrets waiting to be discovered. It serves as a natural defense for Najara, deterring most would-be invaders.

### The Tomb of Diderius

Located within the Serpent Hills, the Tomb of Diderius is an ancient crypt that once belonged to a powerful diviner. It is a place of great historical and magical significance, rumored to contain powerful artifacts and knowledge. The tomb is heavily guarded by traps and undead, making it a perilous destination for those seeking its secrets.

### The High Moor

The High Moor is a vast, desolate expanse of moorland that lies to the west of Najara. It is a place of bleak beauty, with rolling hills and mist-covered valleys. The moor is home to many dangerous creatures, including trolls and ogres, and is often shrouded in mystery. It serves as a buffer zone between Najara and the more civilized lands to the west.

### Darkhold

Though not directly under Najara's control, Darkhold is a significant fortress located near the borders of Najara. It is controlled by the Zhentarim, a powerful mercenary organization with its own ambitions. The presence of Darkhold adds a layer of complexity to the region's politics, as both Najara and the Zhentarim vie for influence and control over the surrounding territories.
