# Kingdom of Many-Arrows

![Map: The North](../../images/realms/map-02.008-the-north.jpg)

> **At a Glance:** In this harsh and beautiful wilderness, nothing lasts and survival is a day-to-day concern.
> **Region:** The North
> **Languages:** Bothii, Chondathan, Illuskan, Reghedjic

Self-crowned King Orrusk Homebringer is working to restore an orc domain in the far north, beginning with the shattered fortress of Dark Arrow Keep. The Kingdom of Many-Arrows united the disparate orc tribes of the Spine of the World for over a hundred years until it was razed in the War of the Silver Marches. Orrusk would see it come into being again, and more orcs arrive every day, drawn by his vision of a new, independent orc realm.

The Kingdom of Many-Arrows was originally founded by Obould Many-Arrows, an orc leader who sought to create a lasting orc kingdom. Under his leadership, the kingdom was recognized by the neighboring human and dwarf realms, marking a significant shift in the region's politics. The kingdom's history is marked by periods of peace and conflict with its neighbors, particularly the dwarves of Mithral Hall and the humans of Silverymoon. The War of the Silver Marches was a devastating conflict that led to the kingdom's downfall, but the orc spirit remains resilient, as evidenced by Orrusk's efforts to rebuild.

Geographically, the Kingdom of Many-Arrows is located in the Spine of the World, a mountain range known for its harsh climate and rugged terrain. The orcs who inhabit this region are well adapted to the cold and often inhospitable environment. The culture of Many-Arrows is heavily influenced by orc traditions, with a strong emphasis on martial prowess and clan loyalty. Despite their reputation as fierce warriors, the orcs of Many-Arrows have a rich cultural heritage, including storytelling, music, and craftsmanship.

## Notable Locations

### Dark Arrow Keep
Dark Arrow Keep serves as the capital of the Kingdom of Many-Arrows. Once a formidable fortress, it was heavily damaged during the War of the Silver Marches. King Orrusk Homebringer has made its restoration a priority, seeing it as a symbol of orc unity and strength. The keep is strategically located, providing a vantage point over the surrounding lands and serving as a rallying point for orc forces.

### Spine of the World
The Spine of the World is a vast mountain range that forms the backbone of the kingdom. It is home to numerous orc tribes, as well as other creatures adapted to the harsh environment. The mountains are rich in resources, including precious metals and minerals, which the orcs mine to support their burgeoning kingdom. The range also serves as a natural barrier, protecting the kingdom from potential invaders.

### Mithral Hall
Though not part of the Kingdom of Many-Arrows, Mithral Hall is a significant location due to its proximity and historical interactions with the orcs. It is a dwarven stronghold known for its wealth and military strength. Relations between Mithral Hall and Many-Arrows have been tense, but there have been periods of peace and cooperation, particularly during Obould's reign.

### Silverymoon
Silverymoon is a major city to the south of Many-Arrows, known for its magical prowess and cultural significance. It has historically been a center of opposition to orc expansion, but also a place of diplomacy and negotiation. The city's leaders are wary of the orc resurgence under King Orrusk, but some see potential for renewed dialogue and understanding.

### Icewind Dale
Located to the west of the Kingdom of Many-Arrows, Icewind Dale is a harsh, frozen land that has seen its share of conflict with the orcs. The region is sparsely populated, with small settlements that must often defend against orc raids. Despite this, there are individuals in Icewind Dale who have formed alliances with the orcs, seeking mutual benefit in trade and defense.

### The Evermoors
The Evermoors, a vast swampy region to the south, is a place of mystery and danger. While not directly controlled by the Kingdom of Many-Arrows, it is a region that the orcs occasionally venture into for resources and exploration. The moors are home to various creatures and are rumored to hold ancient ruins and treasures.

### The High Forest
The High Forest lies to the southeast and is a vast, ancient woodland inhabited by elves and other fey creatures. The orcs of Many-Arrows have traditionally avoided conflict with the forest's inhabitants, respecting their strength and the forest's mystical nature. However, skirmishes have occurred, particularly when orc expansion encroaches on the forest's borders.

## Organizations

### The Orc Clans
The Kingdom of Many-Arrows is composed of various orc clans, each with its own traditions and leaders. These clans are united under the banner of King Orrusk, but they maintain a degree of autonomy. Clan loyalty is paramount, and disputes are often settled through combat or negotiation. The clans are known for their fierce warriors and skilled hunters.

### The War Council
The War Council is an advisory body to King Orrusk, composed of the leaders of the major orc clans. It is responsible for planning military campaigns and ensuring the defense of the kingdom. The council meets regularly at Dark Arrow Keep to discuss strategy and address issues facing the kingdom.

### The Iron Guard
The Iron Guard is the elite military force of the Kingdom of Many-Arrows. Comprised of the best warriors from each clan, the Iron Guard is tasked with protecting the king and carrying out special missions. They are highly trained and equipped with the finest weapons and armor available to the orcs.

### The Shaman Circle
The Shaman Circle is a group of spiritual leaders who guide the orc community in matters of faith and tradition. They are respected advisors to the king and play a crucial role in maintaining the cultural heritage of Many-Arrows. The shamans are also skilled in the use of magic, often calling upon the spirits of the land to aid the kingdom.

## DM Notes

### Campaign Hooks
- A diplomatic mission from Silverymoon seeks to negotiate peace with King Orrusk, but not all orc clans are in favor of diplomacy.
- Rumors of a powerful artifact hidden in the Spine of the World draw adventurers to the region, threatening the fragile stability Orrusk is trying to build.
- An ancient enemy of the orcs stirs in the Evermoors, and King Orrusk seeks allies to combat this new threat.
