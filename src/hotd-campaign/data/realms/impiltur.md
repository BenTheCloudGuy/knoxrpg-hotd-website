# Impiltur

![Map: Forgotten Lands](../../images/realms/map-02.005-forgotten-lands.jpg)

> **At a Glance:** Booming trade cities dot rugged wilds in a diverse region that bursts with untapped wealth: gemstone-studded mountains, powerful primal magic, and relics of buried kingdoms.
> **Region:** Forgotten Lands
> **Languages:** Damaran

Impiltur, once a beacon of wealth and power in Faerun, has faced significant challenges over the past century. The realm's geography was altered by magic, and it suffered from incursions by demons and their worshipers. Despite these setbacks, the rising waters of the Sea of Fallen Stars have heralded a resurgence in Impiltur's fortunes. The realm is now on a path to reclaim its former glory, with whispers among the populace of a lost monarch from the old ruling family who might return to restore the empire. Impiltur is known for its rich deposits of gemstones and a history steeped in both primal magic and ancient relics, making it a land of great interest to adventurers and scholars alike.

The government of Impiltur operates as a monarchy, although it has been without a true king for many years. The Lords of Imphras II, a council of regents, currently oversee the realm's administration. This council is tasked with maintaining order and defending against the persistent threat of demonic forces. The culture of Impiltur is heavily influenced by its Damaran roots, with a strong emphasis on trade, maritime prowess, and a rich tradition of magic.

## Notable Locations

### Lyrabar

The capital city of Impiltur, Lyrabar, is a bustling hub of commerce and naval activity. It is renowned for its numerous sailing academies, which are fierce rivals and host regattas and other contests of skill year-round. Waywater’s Nautical University, one of the most prestigious academies, specializes in teaching abjuration magic to seafaring mages. The city is also home to the headquarters of the Warswords, chainmail-clad soldiers responsible for patrolling Impiltur’s trade roads and keeping merchants safe from brigands and monsters. The Warswords sometimes deputize adventurers as “swordpoints”, temporary agents of the law granted special privileges within Impiltur’s borders.

### Hlammach

Hlammach is a key port city located on the northern coast of Impiltur. Known for its shipyards, Hlammach is a center of maritime trade and shipbuilding. The city is governed by a council of merchant lords who oversee its bustling markets and ensure the prosperity of its docks. Hlammach is also a strategic military location, with a fortified harbor that serves as a base for Impiltur’s navy.

### Dilpur

Dilpur is a smaller city renowned for its gem-cutting industry. Nestled near the Earthspur Mountains, the city is a vital part of Impiltur’s economy, exporting finely crafted gemstones throughout Faerun. The city is watched over by a local lord, who ensures the safety of the mines and the prosperity of its people. Dilpur is also known for its vibrant festivals, celebrating the wealth and beauty of its natural resources.

### The Earthspur Mountains

The Earthspur Mountains form a natural barrier along Impiltur’s western border. Rich in mineral resources, these mountains are dotted with mines and small settlements. The mountains are also home to various dangers, including tribes of orcs and other creatures that threaten the miners and travelers. Adventurers often venture into the Earthspurs in search of treasure and ancient relics.

### The Gray Forest

The Gray Forest is a dense woodland located to the west of Lyrabar. It is known for its eerie beauty and the presence of ancient ruins hidden within its depths. The forest is a place of mystery and magic, with many legends surrounding the creatures and spirits that inhabit it. The Gray Forest is a popular destination for those seeking adventure or knowledge of the ancient past.

### The Vast Swamp

The Vast Swamp lies to the south of Impiltur, a sprawling wetland teeming with life and danger. It is a place where few dare to tread, home to dangerous creatures and rumored to be a hiding place for cultists and outlaws. The swamp is also believed to hold secrets from ancient times, drawing the interest of adventurers and scholars alike.

## DM Notes

Impiltur's history of grappling with demon incursions mirrors Damara's current crisis. The realm's proximity to the Forgotten Lands makes it a potential ally or refuge as events in Damara escalate. The Warswords' practice of deputizing adventurers as "swordpoints" could provide the party with legal authority when operating in Impiltur's territory.
