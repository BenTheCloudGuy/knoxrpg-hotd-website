# Chondath

![Map: Vilhon Reach](../../images/realms/map-02.014-vilhon-reach.jpg)

> **At a Glance:** The Emerald Enclave helps to maintain balance between people and nature in this region.
> **Region:** Vilhon Reach
> **Languages:** Chondathan, Shaaran, Turmic

Chondath was once a large and powerful realm comprising all of the southern Vilhon Reach, with distant colonies in the land now known as Sembia. It was a center of trade and military might, known for its skilled mercenaries and strategic location along the Sea of Fallen Stars. However, over the centuries, Chondath faced numerous challenges, including military defeats and devastating plagues, which led to its decline. The Spellplague further decimated nearly all of Chondath’s coastal settlements, sealing the dying realm’s fate and reducing it to a shadow of its former self.

Today, Chondath is but a shell of its former glory. Its capital city, Arrabar, still stands, desperately demanding increasingly exorbitant taxes from the tiny towns of Samra, Timindar, and Orbech, all three of which are considering secession. The region struggles with internal strife and external threats, as neighboring powers and opportunistic adventurers eye its resources. Adventurers flock to Chondath to see what abandoned treasures lie amid the ruined cities of Mussum, Iljak, Shamph, and Hlath.

## Notable Locations

### Arrabar

Arrabar, the capital of Chondath, is a city determined to hold on to the last vestiges of its realm’s old splendor. Lady Udriana Wianar rules Arrabar and the realm from a grand palace called the Generon. Her family, along with Arrabar’s many nobles, lead decadent lifestyles funded by the rest of Chondath. Despite its decline, Arrabar remains a bustling hub of trade and intrigue, attracting merchants, diplomats, and adventurers seeking fortune or favor.

### Chondalwood

The Chondalwood is a vast and mysterious forest that has long been a source of both wonder and danger. Rumor has it that a city-state of reclusive wood elves called Rucien-Xan lies hidden somewhere within its depths. Those who’ve searched the woods have found only monsters, raucous satyrs, territorial druids, and Ghostwise halflings, a distrustful and uncharacteristically ferocious clan of halflings with telepathic gifts. The forest is also home to ancient ruins and magical phenomena, making it a place of interest for explorers and scholars.

### Mussum

Once a thriving port city, Mussum now lies in ruins, a victim of the Spellplague and subsequent neglect. Its crumbling docks and abandoned warehouses are a testament to its former prosperity. However, the city is not entirely deserted. Bands of scavengers and treasure hunters pick through the wreckage, seeking artifacts and relics from Chondath's golden age. Some say that Mussum's depths hide secrets that could restore Chondath's power, drawing adventurers from across Faerun.

### Iljak

Iljak was once a key trading post along the Vilhon Reach, known for its bustling markets and diverse population. Today, it is a ghost town, its streets overgrown with weeds and its buildings slowly crumbling. Despite its desolation, Iljak is occasionally visited by traders and adventurers who use it as a waypoint on their journeys. The city's ruins are rumored to be haunted, with tales of ghostly merchants and spectral sailors circulating among the local populace.

### Shamph

Shamph, like many of Chondath's coastal settlements, suffered greatly during the Spellplague. Its once-busy shipyards now lie silent, and its harbors are choked with debris. Despite this, Shamph remains a place of interest due to the presence of several ancient temples and shrines dedicated to forgotten deities. These sites are often explored by clerics and historians seeking to uncover lost religious knowledge.

### Hlath

Hlath was once a vibrant city known for its skilled artisans and craftsmen. Today, it is a shadow of its former self, with many of its workshops and forges abandoned. However, some artisans remain, producing goods of exceptional quality that are highly sought after by collectors and connoisseurs. The city's remaining inhabitants are fiercely proud of their heritage and work tirelessly to preserve Hlath's legacy.

### Samra, Timindar, and Orbech

These three towns, though small, are significant in Chondath's current political landscape. Each is considering secession due to the heavy tax burdens imposed by Arrabar. Samra is known for its fertile farmlands, Timindar for its skilled hunters, and Orbech for its small but prosperous fishing industry. The towns' leaders are in constant negotiation with Arrabar, seeking a more equitable arrangement or, failing that, independence.

## Organizations

### The Emerald Enclave

The Emerald Enclave plays a crucial role in maintaining the balance between civilization and nature in the Vilhon Reach. This organization of druids and rangers is dedicated to preserving the natural order and protecting the region's wilderness from exploitation. They are often at odds with Chondath's rulers, who see the Enclave's activities as a threat to their authority. Despite this, the Enclave is respected by many for their wisdom and commitment to the land.
