# Vaasa

![Map: Forgotten Lands](../../images/realms/map-02.005-forgotten-lands.jpg)

Vaasa is a desolate, frozen wasteland in the Cold Lands of northeastern Faerun. Separated from Damara by the Galena Mountains and Bloodstone Pass, it is a harsh and dangerous land of tundra, swamps, and rocky barrens. Despite its inhospitable terrain, Vaasa has been the staging ground for some of the most devastating military campaigns in the region's history, including the rise of the Witch-King Zhengyi and the Warlock Knights.

## Geography

Vaasa sits northwest of Damara, bordered by the Galena Mountains to the east and southeast, and by vast stretches of frozen wilderness to the north and west. The land is predominantly flat tundra and boggy marshland, broken by rocky outcroppings and sparse vegetation. Bloodstone and other minerals lie beneath the surface in significant quantities, making the land valuable despite its brutality.

Bloodstone Pass, cutting through the Galena Mountains, is the primary passage between Vaasa and Damara. It has been the site of numerous battles and military fortifications throughout history. The Damaran Gate at the southern end of the pass has been garrisoned and fortified by multiple Damaran regimes.

The terrain is even more hostile than Damara's. Monsters, goblinoid tribes, giants, and undead roam the wilderness freely. Permanent settlements are rare and heavily fortified.

## History

### Early History

The Von Zarovich dynasty counted the lands of Vaasa among their territories during the height of their rule over the northeastern frontier. Viktor Von Zarovich's conquest extended across the region that would later be divided into Damara, Vaasa, Narfell, and Impiltur.

After Strahd's curse shattered the Von Zarovich dynasty and Damara fell into the period known as The Fractured Realms, Vaasa drifted further into lawlessness. The land became a refuge for monsters, raiders, and those who could not survive in more civilized territories.

### The Witch-King (1347-1359 DR)

In 1347 DR, the lich Zhengyi raised the fortress of Castle Perilous on a crag in northern Vaasa in a single night. The Witch-King gathered an army of goblins, giants, orcs, undead, and extraplanar creatures, along with the support of the Grandfather of Assassins. From this base, Zhengyi launched the Vaasan War, occupying Bloodstone Pass and sweeping into Damara.

After a decade of war, Gareth Dragonsbane and his companions defeated Zhengyi in 1359 DR. Dragonsbane subsequently annexed Vaasa, forming the Kingdom of Bloodstone with its capital at Bloodstone City.

### The Warlock Knights

Following the Spellplague in 1385 DR, a new power arose in Vaasa: the Warlock Knights. This organization of dark spellcasters accumulated power through the chaos following the magical catastrophe. In 1460 DR, they rebelled against the Kingdom of Bloodstone, and by 1469 DR they had overrun and razed Bloodstone City.

The Warlock Knights now control Vaasa, maintaining forces in Bloodstone Pass and posing a constant threat to Damara. Their origins and ultimate goals remain shrouded in secrecy.

## Government and Politics

Vaasa has no stable traditional government. Throughout most of its history, the land has been controlled by whoever held the most military power. Currently, the Warlock Knights exercise authority over the region through a combination of arcane might and conscripted forces.

## Notable Locations

- **Castle Perilous** - The fortress raised by Zhengyi in a single night, built on a crag in northern Vaasa. Even after the Witch-King's defeat, the castle remained standing, and dark energies still emanate from within.
- **Bloodstone Pass** - The mountain passage connecting Vaasa to Damara, site of countless battles.
- **Bloodstone City (ruins)** - The former capital of the Kingdom of Bloodstone, razed by the Warlock Knights in 1469 DR.
- **Frosthaven** - A settlement in Vaasa where the campaign's players began their journey. Located near the Pelavir River.

## Culture and Society

Vaasa has little in the way of organized culture. Its human inhabitants are survivors, living in scattered fortified settlements and eking out an existence through mining, hunting, and trade with Damara. The land attracts adventurers, mercenaries, and those seeking mineral wealth, particularly bloodstone.

The population includes significant numbers of goblinoids, giants, and other dangerous creatures. Humanoid tribes and monstrous inhabitants outnumber human settlers in many areas, making travel outside established routes extremely hazardous.

## DM Notes

Frosthaven in Vaasa is where the campaign began. The players were hired by Captain "Emerald Eyes" Reaven to protect the Sea Spray as it moved cargo down the Pelavir River. The ship wrecked past Bloodstone Pass due to fighting along the river, connected to King Frostmantle's wars with Narfell.

The Warlock Knights' rise in Vaasa may be connected to fragments of Netherese magic buried beneath the region. Their arcane abilities and the source of their power have not been fully revealed, but parallels to the Netherese research at Thul Avenar are worth exploring.
