# Luskan

![Map: The North](../../images/realms/map-02.008-the-north.jpg)

> **At a Glance:** In this harsh and beautiful wilderness, nothing lasts and survival is a day-to-day concern.
> **Region:** The North
> **Languages:** Bothii, Chondathan, Illuskan, Reghedjic

Luskan, known as the City of Sails, stands as a testament to the ebb and flow of fortune. Once a thriving port city, it has been marred by years of crime and misfortune, transforming it into a place where survival is uncertain and the streets are filled with filth. The city is dominated by the Hosttower of the Arcane, a twisted spire that serves as the headquarters for the Arcane Brotherhood, a powerful and secretive organization of wizards. Governance in Luskan is in the hands of five pirate lords known as the High Captains. These leaders are responsible for the city's defense and trade, both legitimate and illicit, often engaging in piracy against the Northlander islands.

Luskan's history is marked by conflict and change. Originally a settlement of the Northmen, it was later conquered by Illuskans, who established it as a center of trade and exploration. Over time, the city became a haven for pirates and mercenaries, its fortunes rising and falling with the tides of power in the region. Despite its decline, Luskan remains an important, if dangerous, hub of activity in the North. The city is known for its harsh climate, with cold winds blowing in from the Sea of Swords, adding to the challenges faced by its inhabitants.

## Notable Locations

### Hosttower of the Arcane
The Hosttower of the Arcane is a massive, twisted structure that looms over Luskan. It serves as the headquarters of the Arcane Brotherhood, a group of powerful wizards who wield significant influence over the city. The tower is both a place of arcane study and a fortress, housing numerous magical defenses. It is a place of mystery and danger, with many secrets hidden within its walls. The Brotherhood's influence extends beyond Luskan, as they have interests throughout the Sword Coast.

### Mirabar Shield
The Mirabar Shield is a fortified district within Luskan, home to the city's wealthier inhabitants and a number of important trading houses. It is heavily guarded, with its own militia to protect against the rampant crime and piracy that plague the rest of the city. The district is a stark contrast to the rest of Luskan, with clean streets and well-maintained buildings. Merchants here conduct business with a level of security rarely seen elsewhere in the city.

### The Cutlass
The Cutlass is one of Luskan's most infamous taverns, known for its rowdy clientele and frequent brawls. It is a popular gathering place for pirates, mercenaries, and adventurers, offering cheap ale and a place to find work or information. The tavern is run by a retired pirate who maintains a semblance of order through a combination of charm and intimidation. Many a plot has been hatched within its walls, making it a hub of intrigue and opportunity.

### Ruins of Illusk
Located beneath the city, the Ruins of Illusk are the remnants of an ancient settlement that predates Luskan. These ruins are a labyrinth of tunnels and chambers, filled with traps and the remnants of ancient magic. They are rumored to be haunted and are a popular destination for adventurers seeking treasure and forgotten knowledge. The ruins are also believed to be connected to the Underdark, adding an additional layer of danger.

### North Bank
The North Bank is the area of Luskan that lies north of the River Mirar. It is a rough and tumble district, home to many of the city's poorer residents. The North Bank is known for its bustling markets and street vendors, as well as its high crime rate. Despite its reputation, it is a vital part of the city's economy, providing goods and services to those who cannot afford the prices in the more affluent areas. The district is a melting pot of cultures and languages, reflecting the diverse population of Luskan.

### The Docks
The Docks of Luskan are the heart of the city's maritime activities. Despite their dilapidated state, they are a hive of activity, with ships constantly coming and going. The docks are controlled by the High Captains, who use them as a base for their piracy and smuggling operations. The area is notorious for its lawlessness, and it is not uncommon for disputes to be settled with violence. The docks are also a key point for trade, both legal and illicit, with goods flowing in and out of the city.

### Pirate Isles
The Pirate Isles are a group of small islands off the coast of Luskan, used by the High Captains as bases for their raiding activities. These islands are heavily fortified and serve as safe havens for the pirates who operate out of Luskan. They are a constant source of tension between Luskan and the nearby Northlander communities. The isles are strategically located, allowing the pirates to strike quickly and retreat to safety.

### Blackrock Company
The Blackrock Company is a mercenary group based in Luskan. Known for their ruthlessness and efficiency, they are often hired by the High Captains to carry out raids and other unsavory tasks. The company operates out of a fortified compound in the city, and their presence is both feared and respected by the inhabitants of Luskan. Their services are in high demand, making them a powerful force in the city's power dynamics.

### Tower of the High Captains
The Tower of the High Captains is the seat of power for the five pirate lords who rule Luskan. It is a heavily fortified structure, serving as both a meeting place and a stronghold. The tower is a symbol of the High Captains' dominance over the city, and it is here that they plot their raids and manage their various enterprises. The tower's defenses are formidable, reflecting the constant threat of internal and external challenges.

### The Sea Tower of Luskan
The Sea Tower of Luskan is a prominent lighthouse and fortification that guards the entrance to the city's harbor. It is manned by a contingent of soldiers loyal to the High Captains, serving as both a navigational aid and a defensive structure. The tower's beacon can be seen for miles, guiding ships safely to port while also serving as a deterrent to would-be attackers.
