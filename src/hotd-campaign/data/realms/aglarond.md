# Aglarond

![Map: Arcane Empires](../../images/realms/map-02.003-aglarond-and-thay.jpg)

> **At a Glance:** Arcane magic profoundly influences these cultures: sorcery in Aglarond, wizardry in Thay, and warlock pacts in Rashemen.
> **Region:** Arcane Empires
> **Languages:** Aglarondan, Rashemi, Thayan

The realm of Aglarond was founded five centuries ago, uniting human settlers and elves of the Yuirwood in a treaty of mutual defense and friendship. This alliance was crucial in countering the persistent threat posed by the Red Wizards of Thay. Historically, Aglarondans were wary of arcane magic due to the aggressive nature of Thayan wizards. However, this perception shifted under the leadership of the Simbul, a sorceress of legendary power known for her unpredictable yet benevolent nature. Her reign marked a new era where individuals with innate magical abilities were trained as sorcerers, a tradition that continues under the current governance of the Simbarch Council, predominantly composed of arcane spellcasters.

Aglarond is renowned as a realm where elves and humans coexist harmoniously, often through intermarriage, creating a unique cultural blend. The geography of Aglarond is diverse, featuring the dense Yuirwood, coastal marshes, and the strategic Umber Marshes. The cultural tapestry is enriched by the integration of elven and human traditions, with a strong emphasis on community and mutual protection.

## Notable Locations

### Altumbel

The small human kingdom of Altumbel, while often associated with Aglarond, maintains its independence and distinct identity. The inhabitants primarily depend on the sea for their livelihood, with the capital, Spandeliyon, earning the moniker "City of Pirates" due to its history of maritime raiding and trade.

### Fang Light

Located on the South Fang peninsula north of Velprintalar, Fang Light is a magical lighthouse operated by a resident mage. It serves as a crucial navigational aid for ships traversing the Sea of Dlurg. The area is considered a backwater, and its residents, known as "Fangers," are often the subject of derision by other Aglarondans. The lighthouse is a frequent target for pirates seeking to disrupt maritime traffic.

### Sunglade

Situated at the southern edge of the Yuirwood, the Sunglade is a double circle of ancient standing stones inscribed with elven runes. These stones were erected by the ancient Yuir and are dedicated to the Seldarine, the elven pantheon, and deities of the Yuirwood's early human inhabitants. The site retains a mystical aura, and many believe it holds secrets waiting to be uncovered.

### Umber Marshes

The Umber Marshes form the natural border between Aglarond and Thay, characterized by its iron-rich, rust-colored waters. This swampy region has been the site of numerous conflicts and is notorious for its swarms of bloodsucking insects and dangerous wildlife. It serves as both a natural barrier and a battleground against Thayan incursions.

### Velprintalar

Velprintalar, the capital of Aglarond, is celebrated as one of Faerun's most beautiful cities. The Simbarch Council governs from a grand palace constructed of pale-green Mulhorandi stone. The city is a hub for arcane learning, attracting sorcerers from across Faerun seeking to hone their skills.

### Watchwall

Stretching fifteen miles between the fortress city of Glarondar and Mount Umbergoth, the Watchwall is a formidable stone barrier constructed by the galeb duhr for the Aglarondan crown. Manned by vigilant guards known as Watchers, it stands as a sentinel against potential Thayan aggression.

### Wizards’ Reach

The southern coast of Aglarond, known as Wizards’ Reach, is a series of cities under nominal Aglarondan control. However, the influence of the Red Wizards grows stronger the further east one travels. The coastal marshes are challenging to traverse, and the area is plagued by monsters emerging from the Yuirwood.

### Yuirwood

The ancient forest of Yuirwood was originally settled by the Yuir elves. Over time, humans integrated into the forest, adopting elven customs and living among the remnants of Yuir civilization. The forest is protected by potent magic that obscures it from scrying and detection spells, and it is riddled with fey crossings and inhabited by dangerous creatures.
