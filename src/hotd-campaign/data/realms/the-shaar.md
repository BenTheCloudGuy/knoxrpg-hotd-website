# The Shaar

![Map: Beyond the Map](../../images/realms/map-faerun.jpg)

> **At a Glance:** Distant lands and island realms that lie at the edges of Faerunian knowledge and exploration.
> **Region:** Beyond the Map
> **Languages:** Various

The Shaar is a vast, rolling grassland stretching from the Shining Sea in the west to the eastern lands beyond Faerun. This expansive region is characterized by its sweeping plains and a climate that alternates between scorching days and freezing nights. The Shaar is home to the Shaaryan nomads, a collection of tribes that share a common culture and lifestyle centered around hunting and herding. These tribes, along with centaurs and gnolls, roam the grasslands, living off the land which is rich in native grasses but unsuitable for traditional agriculture. Despite their shared culture, the Shaaryan tribes have never formed a unified nation, preferring their independent, nomadic existence. The horses of the Shaaryan are well adapted to the plains but struggle outside their native environment, which discourages large-scale migration.

## Notable Locations

### Lhesper

Lhesper is a desolate ruin that was once a thriving city. It is now controlled by a powerful clan of yuan-ti, who use it as a base for their nefarious activities. The yuan-ti dominate the area around Lake Lhespen, preying on unwary travelers. Those captured often face grim fates, either sacrificed to the yuan-ti deities or transformed into monstrous servants. The Shaaryan nomads avoid this area, aware of the dangers that lurk within the ruined city.

### Shaarmid

Shaarmid is a bustling free trading city located in the heart of the Shaar. Unlike the nomadic Shaaryan tribes, the inhabitants of Shaarmid are settled and have established a reputation as skilled traders. The city serves as a vital hub for commerce, where merchants from across Faerun come to trade goods in safety. The Shaaryan tribes view Shaarmid as an ally, thanks to the city's history of facilitating beneficial trade deals. This relationship has fostered a sense of mutual respect between the settled and nomadic peoples of the Shaar.

### Hardcastle

Hardcastle is a fortified settlement located on the northern edge of the Shaar. It serves as a defensive outpost against gnoll raids and other threats from the north. The settlement is governed by a council of warriors who maintain order and protect the surrounding lands. Hardcastle also acts as a waypoint for travelers heading into the deeper regions of the Shaar, offering supplies and rest.

### The Landrise

The Landrise is a dramatic escarpment that marks the transition from the flat grasslands of the Shaar to the more rugged terrain of the eastern lands. This natural barrier is a strategic location for the Shaaryan tribes, who use it as a vantage point for spotting potential threats. The Landrise is also home to several ancient ruins, remnants of long-lost civilizations that once inhabited the region.

### The Shaarwood

The Shaarwood is a dense forest located near the eastern edge of the Shaar. It is a rare break from the endless grasslands and serves as a refuge for various wildlife. The forest is considered sacred by some Shaaryan tribes, who believe it to be the dwelling place of powerful nature spirits. The Shaarwood is also rumored to contain hidden groves and ancient sites of druidic magic, attracting adventurers and scholars alike.

### The Great Rift

The Great Rift is a massive canyon that cuts across the southern part of the Shaar. It is the homeland of the gold dwarves, who have established a thriving underground kingdom within its depths. The dwarves of the Great Rift are known for their exceptional craftsmanship and have a strong trade relationship with the surface dwellers of the Shaar. The canyon itself is a natural wonder, with steep cliffs and a network of tunnels and caves that offer both danger and opportunity for exploration.
