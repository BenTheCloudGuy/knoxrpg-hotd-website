# Northern Dwarfholds

![Map: The North](../../images/realms/map-02.008-the-north.jpg)

> **At a Glance:** In this harsh and beautiful wilderness, nothing lasts and survival is a day-to-day concern.
> **Region:** The North
> **Languages:** Bothii, Chondathan, Illuskan, Reghedjic

Throughout the North, many dwarves dwell in independent fortresses called dwarfholds. These bastions of dwarven culture and craftsmanship are scattered across the rugged terrain, often nestled within the protective embrace of mountain ranges. Many such dwarfholds are lost, abandoned, or infested by monsters, but some still stand firm as symbols of dwarven resilience and tenacity. The dwarves in Mithral Hall, Citadel Adbar, Citadel Felbarr, Gauntlgrym, Stoneshaft Hold, and Ironmaster trace their ancestry back to Delzoun, the ancient dwarf kingdom of the North. This lineage is a source of pride and identity, influencing their governance and societal structure.

Mirabar and Sundabar are also notable dwarfholds. Mirabar has embraced a more cosmopolitan identity by welcoming human immigrants, fostering a unique blend of cultures and expertise. Conversely, Sundabar was devastated by the Kingdom of Many-Arrows during the War of the Silver Marches. This conflict led to a significant shift in the political landscape; before the war, most dwarfholds were part of the Lords’ Alliance. However, the failure of Silverymoon to protect Sundabar prompted the dwarfhold leaders to declare their intention to rely solely on one another, reinforcing their independence and mutual aid.

## Notable Locations

### Citadel Adbar

Citadel Adbar is a formidable fortress located in the Ice Mountains, originally established by the dwarves of ancient Delzoun. It has withstood numerous sieges over eighteen centuries, thanks to its strategic design and the unwavering determination of its inhabitants. The War of the Silver Marches significantly reduced the ranks of the Knights of the Mithral Shield, but the Iron Guard, Citadel Adbar's primary military force, remains robust and vigilant, ensuring the citadel's continued security.

### Gauntlgrym

Gauntlgrym, once a thriving center of Delzoun, lay dormant beneath the mountains for centuries. It was reclaimed by King Bruenor Battlehammer, who led an army of shield dwarves to liberate it from the clutches of Lolth's followers. The heart of Gauntlgrym is its legendary forge, powered by Maegera the Dawn Titan, a fire elemental of immense power. This forge is central to the city's economy and defense, producing unmatched dwarven weapons and armor.

### Mithral Hall

Mithral Hall is the ancestral home of the Battlehammer clan, situated beneath the Frost Hills. Known for its near-impenetrable defenses, the hall serves more as a stronghold than a traditional city. Its extensive network of tunnels connects it to other dwarfholds, facilitating trade and mutual defense. Queen Dagnabbet Waybeard, a staunch supporter of the Lords’ Alliance, governs Mithral Hall with a focus on maintaining its security and prosperity.

### Sundabar

Sundabar, once a dual-layered city with both surface and subterranean components, was ravaged by the Many-Arrows orcs during the War of the Silver Marches. The surface city was left in ruins, forcing many refugees to flee to Silverymoon and other nearby settlements. The dwarves of the lower holds have since isolated themselves, banning outsiders and focusing on maintaining their underground forges, which continue to produce goods amidst the city's ruins.

### Citadel Felbarr

Citadel Felbarr is another ancient stronghold of Delzoun, reclaimed from orcish occupation by the dwarves. It stands as a testament to dwarven perseverance, with its massive gates and formidable defenses. The citadel is ruled by King Emerus Warcrown, who has strengthened ties with neighboring dwarfholds to ensure mutual protection and prosperity.

### Ironmaster

Ironmaster is a secluded dwarfhold located on the icy shores of the Sea of Moving Ice. Known for its isolationist policies, Ironmaster strictly controls access to its lands, allowing only dwarves to enter. The hold is renowned for its skilled blacksmiths, who produce some of the finest weapons and armor in the North, often sought after by adventurers and mercenaries alike.

### Stoneshaft Hold

Stoneshaft Hold is a lesser-known but strategically important dwarfhold, serving as a key trading post between the northern dwarfholds and the human settlements of the North. Its location makes it a vital hub for the exchange of goods and information, fostering alliances and trade agreements that benefit the wider dwarven community.

These locations collectively embody the strength and resilience of the dwarves in the North, each contributing uniquely to the cultural and economic tapestry of the region.
