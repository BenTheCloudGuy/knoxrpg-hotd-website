# Shining Plains

![Map: Vilhon Reach](../../images/realms/map-02.014-vilhon-reach.jpg)

> **At a Glance:** The Emerald Enclave helps to maintain balance between people and nature in this region.
> **Region:** Vilhon Reach
> **Languages:** Chondathan, Shaaran, Turmic

The Shining Plains, a vast expanse of dry grassland, is characterized by its shimmering heat waves that rise most of the year, giving the region its name. This area is home to centaurs and thri-kreen, who maintain civil, if distant, relationships with the handful of towns scattered across the plains. The region lacks a unifying political force, and its governance is influenced by various powers from the surrounding Vilhon Reach. The geography of the Shining Plains is diverse, featuring rolling grasslands, the imposing Deepwing Mountains, and the mysterious Wetwoods. The culture here is deeply tied to nature, with the Emerald Enclave playing a significant role in maintaining the balance between civilization and the natural world. Historically, the Shining Plains has been a crossroads for various nomadic tribes and wandering adventurers, each leaving their mark on the land.

## Notable Locations

### Deepwing Mountains

The Deepwing Mountains are named for the many winged monsters that inhabit its heights, including manticores, griffons, hippogriffs, dragons, and wyverns. These formidable creatures make the mountains a place of danger and mystery. The peaks serve as a natural barrier and a source of untapped resources, attracting adventurers seeking glory and treasure. Despite the dangers, the mountains are occasionally traversed by brave traders and explorers.

### Nathlekh

Nathlekh, known as the City of Cats, is an independent city-state where cats are revered and found in abundance, lounging on rooftops and sipping milk from public troughs. The citizens of Nathlekh predominantly worship Nobanion, the lion-god, who is rumored to roam the nearby Gulthmere Forest. The city is a hub of trade and culture, with its unique feline-centric society drawing visitors from across Faerun.

### Wetwoods

The Wetwoods is a misty forest inhabited by lizardfolk, bullywugs, and other bog-dwelling creatures. The dense, swampy terrain makes it a challenging area to navigate, but it is home to several small halfling hamlets. The most notable settlement is Urml, a reclusive village of bullywugs. The Wetwoods' mysterious atmosphere and hidden dangers make it a place of intrigue and caution for travelers.

### Shaar

The Shaar is a vast, open savanna that stretches beyond the Shining Plains. It is a land of rolling grasslands and scattered hills, inhabited by nomadic tribes and wild herds. The Shaar serves as a natural extension of the Shining Plains, offering a sense of continuity to the landscape. It is a region of stark beauty and harsh conditions, where survival depends on adaptability and resilience.

### Gulthmere Forest

Located near Nathlekh, the Gulthmere Forest is a dense woodland known for its ancient trees and diverse wildlife. It is said to be the roaming ground of Nobanion, the lion-god, and holds a mystical allure for those who seek to connect with nature. The forest is a sanctuary for druids and nature worshippers, who protect its secrets and maintain its balance.

### Emerald Enclave

The Emerald Enclave is a powerful organization dedicated to preserving the natural order and protecting the balance between civilization and nature. They have a strong presence in the Shining Plains, working to ensure that the region's diverse ecosystems remain intact. The Enclave's druids and rangers are respected and sometimes feared for their ability to wield nature's power.

### The Vilhon Reach

The Vilhon Reach is a significant geographic and cultural region that influences the Shining Plains. It is a hub of trade and interaction, with its waters serving as a conduit for goods and ideas. The Reach's cities and ports are vital to the economy of the Shining Plains, providing a link to the broader world of Faerun.
