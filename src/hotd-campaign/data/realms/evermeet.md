# Evermeet

![Map: Beyond the Map](../../images/realms/map-faerun.jpg)

> **At a Glance:** Distant lands and island realms that lie at the edges of Faerunian knowledge and exploration.
> **Region:** Beyond the Map
> **Languages:** Various

Almost twenty thousand years ago, elves used high magic to create a homeland for themselves. In the process, they devastated Toril in a cataclysmic disaster eventually known as the Sundering. But the island they created, Evermeet, remains. Elves from across Faerun eventually travel to the island. Evermeet is often described as a paradise, a place of breathtaking beauty and tranquility, where the natural world and elven artistry blend seamlessly. The island is a haven for all elvenkind, serving as a cultural and spiritual center for elves worldwide.

Evermeet is protected by many layers of defenses. First, it is magically warded to prevent navigators from finding the island. Winds and subtle currents turn ships aside, stars move in the sky, and powerful illusions hide the island itself. Only navigators entrusted with secret knowledge can find the way. These magical protections are maintained by the island's most powerful spellcasters, who ensure that Evermeet remains hidden and safe from those who would seek to harm it.

Second, a great navy shields Evermeet from enemies and allows the island nation to project its power into Faerun when necessary. Elf merchants bring goods from Evermeet to markets in Faerun and import anything the elves can’t make themselves. The navy is renowned for its swift ships and skilled sailors, who are ever vigilant against threats from both sea and sky.

Finally, the capital city of Leuthilspar is protected by magically constructed walls of glass. Evermeet’s Royal Council meets here. The city serves as the political and cultural heart of Evermeet, where decisions affecting the entire island are made. The architecture of Leuthilspar is a marvel, with structures that seem to grow naturally from the landscape, adorned with intricate carvings and vibrant mosaics.

Evermeet lies at a conjunction of three planes of existence: the Material Plane, the Feywild, and Arvandor. Non-elves aren’t permitted to visit, but on very rare occasions, exceptions have been made, and at least one gnome has managed to reach the island by stowing away with an elf traveler. This unique planar position grants Evermeet a mystical quality, with flora and fauna that are as much fey as they are material.

## Notable Locations

### Leuthilspar
Leuthilspar is the capital city of Evermeet and the seat of its Royal Council. It is a city of unparalleled beauty, with its glass walls and elegant spires. The city is a hub of elven culture and politics, where leaders gather to discuss matters of state and where artisans and scholars thrive.

### Tower of the Sun
The Tower of the Sun is a significant magical and religious site located near Leuthilspar. It serves as a temple to Corellon Larethian, the chief deity of the elves, and a place of study for those practicing high magic. The tower is a beacon of light, visible from miles away, symbolizing hope and enlightenment.

### The Green Glade
The Green Glade is a vast forest that covers much of Evermeet, home to a wide variety of flora and fauna, some of which are not found anywhere else in the world. It is a place of serenity and natural beauty, where elves can connect with nature and find peace.

### The Feywild Crossing
Located deep within the Green Glade, the Feywild Crossing is a portal that links Evermeet to the Feywild. It is a closely guarded secret, used by the elves to travel between the two realms. The crossing is surrounded by ancient trees and guarded by powerful fey creatures.

### The Crystal Reef
The Crystal Reef surrounds much of Evermeet's coastline, a natural barrier composed of shimmering coral and inhabited by a myriad of sea creatures. It is both a natural wonder and a strategic defense, making it difficult for enemy ships to approach the island undetected.

### The Silver Palace
The Silver Palace is the residence of the Queen of Evermeet and the heart of its royal court. It is a magnificent structure, built from silver and white marble, with gardens that bloom year-round. The palace is a symbol of the island's enduring legacy and the strength of its people.

## DM Notes

Evermeet's creation during the Sundering is tied to the same ancient elven high magic that created the mythals and other powerful magical constructs encountered throughout the campaign. The island's position at a conjunction of three planes (Material, Feywild, and Arvandor) makes it relevant to any planar-focused storylines.
