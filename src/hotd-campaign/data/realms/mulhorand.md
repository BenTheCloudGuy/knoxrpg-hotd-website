# Mulhorand

![Map: Old Empires](../../images/realms/map-02.009-old-empires.jpg)

> **At a Glance:** Living deities walk among mortals, leading their followers into cycles of terrible wars followed by golden ages.
> **Region:** Old Empires
> **Languages:** Chessentan, Mulhorandi, Untheric

Mulhorand is the oldest of the Old Empires and was literally shaped by gods. Deities from a distant world, gods with names like Anhur, Horus-Re, and Set, took on physical form, leading their people to settle in rich, densely populated metropolises and build massive stone pyramids. To this day, these incarnated deities continue to walk the land and directly influence the realm.

The government of Mulhorand is a theocratic monarchy, where pharaohs with divine lineage hold ultimate authority. These rulers are supported by a class of powerful theocrats, including high priests, sages, and holy warriors who serve the incarnated gods. The society is deeply hierarchical, with all Mulhorandi, from nobles to commoners, adhering to the god-kings’ edicts in every aspect of life. The influence of the gods is pervasive; for instance, Nephthys ensures the safe arrival of merchant shipments, Hathor oversees childbirth, and Anhur is invoked for military victories.

Mulhorand's geography is dominated by fertile plains, expansive deserts, and the mighty River of Swords, which serves as a natural boundary with the neighboring empire of Unther. The culture is steeped in tradition, with a rich tapestry of art, music, and architecture that reflects the divine heritage of its people. The history of Mulhorand is marked by cycles of conquest and prosperity, driven by the ambitions of its god-kings and their mortal followers.

## Notable Locations

### The Great Vale

The Great Vale is the agricultural heart of Mulhorand, producing vast quantities of barley, lentils, and onions, essential for feeding the empire's populace. It is also the primary source of papyrus, a critical material for record-keeping and communication. The advanced technology of large bronze pumps is used to draw water from the River of Spears, ensuring the valley's farms remain fertile. The eastern edge of the vale is bordered by the Dragonsword Mountains, where the pharaohs and high priests are interred in the Land of the Dead, a region dotted with ancient step pyramids and towering obelisks.

### River of Swords

The River of Swords serves as the contested border between Mulhorand and Unther, frequently witnessing skirmishes and battles. Its waters are mostly saline, making them unsuitable for drinking or irrigation. The city of Sekras, located along its banks, houses a unique machine operated by priests of Thoth that desalinates the river water. The city's control frequently shifts between Mulhorand and Unther, leaving its residents uncertain of their allegiance.

### Skuld

Skuld, known as the City of Shadows, is the oldest continuously inhabited city in Faerun. It is the cultural and political center of Mulhorand, where the incarnations of the god-kings reside in the City of the Gods district. This area is characterized by grand temples, lush gardens, and courtyards adorned with gilded statues. Skuld is a hub of activity, where the divine and mortal realms intersect, and visitors can witness the full expression of Mulhorandi culture.

### Sultim

Sultim is a bustling port city on the shores of the Alamber Sea. It serves as a critical trade hub, facilitating commerce between Mulhorand and other regions of Faerun. The city is known for its vibrant markets, where exotic goods from distant lands can be found. Sultim is also home to a significant naval presence, tasked with protecting Mulhorand's maritime interests and ensuring the safety of its trade routes.

### The Raurin Desert

The Raurin Desert, also known as the Desert of Desolation, is a vast and inhospitable region that lies to the east of Mulhorand. It is a place of ancient ruins and lost cities, remnants of a bygone era when the desert was fertile and populated. Adventurers and treasure seekers are drawn to the Raurin Desert in search of forgotten relics and untold riches, though the harsh environment and lurking dangers make it a perilous destination.

### The Dragonsword Mountains

The Dragonsword Mountains form a natural barrier to the east of the Great Vale. These rugged peaks are home to numerous tombs and burial sites of Mulhorand's most revered leaders. The mountains are also rumored to harbor hidden enclaves and ancient secrets, guarded by creatures and spirits loyal to the Mulhorandi gods. Explorers who venture into the mountains do so at their own risk, as the terrain is treacherous and the guardians vigilant.

### The City of Mishtan

Mishtan is a city located near the border with Unther, known for its strategic importance and military fortifications. It serves as a key defensive position against potential incursions from Untherite forces. The city is heavily garrisoned, with a strong presence of Mulhorandi soldiers and war priests dedicated to Anhur. Mishtan's residents are accustomed to the constant threat of conflict, living in a state of perpetual readiness.

### The Plains of Purple Dust

Located to the south of Mulhorand, the Plains of Purple Dust are a mysterious and eerie landscape. The region is characterized by its unusual purple-hued sand, which gives the area its name. The plains are largely uninhabited, with only a few nomadic tribes daring to traverse its expanse. Legends speak of ancient curses and magical phenomena that permeate the area, deterring most from exploring its secrets.
