# Lords' Alliance

![Map: The North](../../images/realms/map-02.008-the-north.jpg)

> **At a Glance:** In this harsh and beautiful wilderness, nothing lasts and survival is a day-to-day concern.
> **Region:** The North
> **Languages:** Bothii, Chondathan, Illuskan, Reghedjic

The Lords’ Alliance is a coalition of powerful city-states and settlements across the North and beyond, formed to ensure mutual prosperity and security. Its members include major cities like Waterdeep, Neverwinter, Silverymoon, and Mithral Hall. The Alliance operates on a principle of mutual aid, though each member prioritizes its own interests. This coalition is known for employing adventurers to tackle threats and is not averse to using force or subterfuge when necessary. The Alliance plays a crucial role in maintaining stability in the region, especially in the face of external threats such as orc hordes and other monstrous incursions.

The political landscape of the Alliance is complex, as each member holds significant autonomy. Decisions are made through a council where representatives from each member city discuss and vote on matters affecting the region. The Alliance's influence extends beyond its member cities, impacting trade routes, diplomatic relations, and regional security. Its existence has been pivotal in keeping the North relatively stable compared to other parts of Faerun.

## Notable Locations

### High Road

A vital trade artery, the High Road connects the bustling ports of Luskan and Waterdeep, passing through Neverwinter. This route is crucial for commerce and the movement of goods along the Sword Coast. In recent years, efforts led by Dagult Neverember and the High Road Charter Company have restored the road, making it safer for travelers and merchants. Patrols and fortified waystations have been established to deter banditry and monster attacks.

### Mirabar

Mirabar is a wealthy city known for its mining operations and strategic location on the Mirar River. The city is a hub of commerce, with its riches stemming from the vast mineral wealth extracted from the surrounding mountains. Governed by a hereditary marchion and the influential Council of Sparkling Stones, Mirabar's society is deeply intertwined with its dwarven roots. The Axe of Mirabar, a formidable military force, ensures the city's defenses remain strong against any threats.

### Neverwinter

Once devastated by the eruption of Mount Hotenow, Neverwinter is now a city reborn under the leadership of Lord Dagult Neverember. Known for its skilled craftsmen and enchanting gardens, Neverwinter has regained its status as a center of culture and trade. The city's strategic location and natural resources make it a key player in the Lords' Alliance, contributing significantly to regional stability and prosperity.

### Silverymoon

Silverymoon, often referred to as the Gem of the North, is a city renowned for its beauty, culture, and magical prowess. It serves as a beacon of learning and enlightenment, with institutions dedicated to the arts and arcane studies. The Knights in Silver, along with the city's magical defenses, protect its serene streets and towering spires. Despite the dissolution of the Silver Marches alliance, Silverymoon remains a respected and influential member of the Lords' Alliance.

### Waterdeep

Known as the City of Splendors, Waterdeep is the most influential city within the Lords' Alliance. Its strategic location and bustling port make it a center of trade and politics. Governed by the mysterious Masked Lords, Waterdeep is a melting pot of cultures and races. The city's influence extends far beyond its borders, playing a pivotal role in the Alliance's operations and diplomatic endeavors.

### Mithral Hall

Mithral Hall is a dwarven stronghold located beneath the Spine of the World. Known for its rich deposits of mithral and other precious metals, the hall is a center of dwarven culture and craftsmanship. It is ruled by the Battlehammer clan, with Bruenor Battlehammer being one of its most famous leaders. Mithral Hall's contributions to the Alliance are invaluable, providing both military support and economic resources.

### Luskan

Once a city of pirates and cutthroats, Luskan has slowly transformed into a more stable and prosperous settlement. Its location at the mouth of the River Mirar makes it an important port for trade with the northern regions. While still rough around the edges, Luskan's inclusion in the Alliance has brought a degree of order and opportunity for growth.

### Baldur's Gate

Though not a northern city, Baldur's Gate is a key member of the Lords' Alliance due to its strategic location along the Trade Way. It serves as a gateway between the North and the southern regions of Faerun. The city's wealth and influence make it a crucial ally in the Alliance's efforts to maintain stability across the continent.

### Icewind Dale

A remote and rugged region, Icewind Dale is known for its harsh climate and hardy inhabitants. While not a formal member of the Lords' Alliance, its settlements often benefit from the Alliance's protection and trade networks. The region's unique resources and strategic location make it an area of interest for the Alliance's long-term plans.
