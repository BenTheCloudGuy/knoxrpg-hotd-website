# Cormyr

![Map: Heartlands](../../images/realms/map-02.006-heartlands.jpg)

> **At a Glance:** These are lands of chivalrous knights, dastardly despots, rich and scheming merchants, and backwoods farmers just trying to make a living.
> **Region:** Heartlands
> **Languages:** Chondathan, Damaran

Cormyr, often referred to as the Forest Kingdom, is a land steeped in tradition and valor. Governed by the Obarskyr dynasty for over a thousand years, the kingdom is a bastion of stability and order amidst the often chaotic Heartlands of Faerun. The Purple Dragon Knights, Cormyr's elite military force, are renowned throughout the continent for their discipline and prowess. The kingdom's government is a constitutional monarchy, with Queen Raedra Obarskyr at the helm, supported by a council of nobles and advisors. The Suzail Writ ensures that even the monarch is subject to the laws of the land, promoting a sense of justice and equality.

Geographically, Cormyr is bordered by the Storm Horns to the west and the Thunder Peaks to the east, with vast stretches of fertile farmland and dense forests in between. The kingdom's economy thrives on agriculture, trade, and the skilled craftsmanship of its artisans. Culturally, Cormyr is a melting pot, with influences from neighboring regions and a rich history of both human and non-human inhabitants. The kingdom has faced numerous threats over the centuries, from orc invasions to internal strife, yet it has always emerged resilient and united.

## Notable Locations

### Farsea Marshes and Marshes of Tun

The Farsea Marshes and the Marshes of Tun are treacherous wetlands located in northern Cormyr. These areas are notorious for their dangerous terrain and the remnants of ancient civilizations that predate even the elves. The marshes are filled with hostile creatures and magical diseases, remnants of a bygone era when rival societies unleashed devastating magical plagues upon each other. Today, adventurers and scholars alike are drawn to these marshes, seeking lost knowledge and treasures hidden within the murky depths.

### Huthduth

Situated in a secluded valley south of the Farsea Marshes, Huthduth is a monastery dedicated to Chauntea, the goddess of agriculture. The monastery is fortified with strong walls and defended by militant priests who protect its secrets. The spirit of Huthduth's founder is said to linger within the monastery, occasionally manifesting to guide and protect its inhabitants. The monks of Huthduth are known for their fine wines and wool, products of the fertile lands they tend. Rumors persist of hidden treasures or powerful magic safeguarded within its walls.

### Shargrailar’s Lair

Nestled within the Thunder Peaks, Shargrailar’s Lair is a cavernous complex that once served as the home of the first dracolich, Shargrailar. Although Shargrailar was destroyed long ago, the lair remains a site of interest for the Cult of the Dragon. The cavern is a breeding ground for red dragon wyrmlings, and the cultists who visit hope to uncover lost secrets of dracolich transformation. The lair's proximity to the headwaters of the Immerflow adds to its strategic importance.

### Sunset Mountains

The Sunset Mountains form a natural barrier along Cormyr's western border. These rugged peaks are home to the Darkhold, a formidable fortress controlled by the Zhentarim, a shadowy organization with ambitions across Faerun. The mountains are also known for the Well of Dragons, an extinct volcanic caldera where dragons go to die. Although the Cult of the Dragon's base beneath the Well has been dismantled, the area remains a focal point for dragon-related activities and intrigue.

### Suzail

Suzail, the capital city of Cormyr, is a bustling hub of commerce and governance. The city is renowned for its beautiful architecture, vibrant marketplaces, and the imposing Royal Palace. Queen Raedra Obarskyr rules from the Dragon Throne, balancing the interests of the nobility with those of her subjects. Suzail is a center of culture and learning, attracting scholars, artisans, and adventurers from across Faerun. The city's port facilitates trade with distant lands, enhancing Cormyr's wealth and influence.

### Thunder Peaks

The Thunder Peaks are a formidable mountain range that marks Cormyr's eastern frontier. These peaks are infamous for their fierce storms and the ancient ruins hidden within their crags. Aurgloroasa, a dracolich known as the Sibilant Shade, resides in a forgotten dwarven city within the mountains. She leads the Sembian sect of the Cult of the Dragon, using the peaks as a base for her dark machinations. The Thunder Peaks are a place of danger and opportunity for those brave enough to explore their depths.

### Tilverton

Once a thriving town, Tilverton now stands as a haunting reminder of the Shadowfell's destructive power. A cataclysmic event left the town in ruins, replaced by a shadowy crater from which nightmarish creatures occasionally emerge. The area is avoided by most, but some adventurers are drawn to the mystery and potential rewards hidden within the shadows. The ruins of Tilverton serve as a stark warning of the dangers posed by planar rifts and the unpredictable nature of magic.
