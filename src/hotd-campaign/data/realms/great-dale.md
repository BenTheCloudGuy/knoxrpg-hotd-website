# Great Dale

![Map: Forgotten Lands](../../images/realms/map-02.005-forgotten-lands.jpg)

> **At a Glance:** Booming trade cities dot rugged wilds in a diverse region that bursts with untapped wealth: gemstone-studded mountains, powerful primal magic, and relics of buried kingdoms.
> **Region:** Forgotten Lands
> **Languages:** Damaran

The Great Dale is a narrow strip of land nestled between the formidable forests of Dunwood to the north and the Forest of Lethyr to the south. This region, part of the larger Unapproachable East, is a land of contrasts where civilization and wilderness collide. The Great Dale is governed by a loose confederation of druids and rangers who uphold the natural order and maintain a delicate balance between the encroaching civilizations and the wildlands. The region’s history is steeped in the ancient conflicts of Narfell and Raumathar, leaving behind a legacy of ruins and magical artifacts that continue to intrigue adventurers and scholars alike.

The culture of the Great Dale is deeply influenced by the druids and fey who inhabit the forests. These inhabitants are known for their secretive nature and their reluctance to engage with outsiders. The people of the Great Dale are primarily Damaran, but the influence of the fey and the ancient Nar is evident in their customs and beliefs. The region is also known for its primal magic, which is often harnessed by the druids to protect their lands from those who would seek to exploit them.

## Notable Locations

### Dunwood

Dunwood is a dense and foreboding forest characterized by its thick canopy of firs and oaks. The forest is notorious for its treacherous terrain and the dark creatures that dwell within. The soil, rich in peat, has preserved the ruins of ancient elven and Nar cities, making it a site of great archaeological interest. However, the forest is also home to dangerous creatures such as demonic zombies and ancient living trees, which deter all but the most determined adventurers.

### Dun-Tharos

Once the capital of the ancient Nar empire, Dun-Tharos is a sprawling ruin located in the heart of Dunwood. The city was dedicated to demonic powers, and its ruins are filled with the remnants of dark rituals and powerful magic. Despite its ruinous state, Dun-Tharos remains a place of interest for those seeking the lost treasures of the Nar. Adventurers are drawn to the city by tales of hidden shrines and flooded chambers filled with valuable artifacts.

### Forest of Lethyr

The Forest of Lethyr is a vast and mysterious woodland that serves as a sanctuary for the druids who protect its sacred groves. The forest is rich in natural resources, but the druids are fiercely protective of their land and will resort to violence to defend it. Within the forest lies Yeshelmaar, a city that showcases the architectural prowess of the elves. Yeshelmaar is known for its floating herb gardens, tiered stone courts, and intricately carved grottoes, making it a marvel of elven engineering.

### Yeshelmaar

Yeshelmaar is the cultural heart of the Great Dale and a testament to the ingenuity of its elven inhabitants. The city is built into the natural landscape, with structures that blend seamlessly with the surrounding forest. Its floating herb gardens are a source of both beauty and sustenance, while the tiered stone courts serve as gathering places for the city’s inhabitants. Yeshelmaar is also known for its grottoes, which are filled with pools of crystal-clear spring water and are said to have healing properties.

### Rawlinswood

Located to the east of the Great Dale, Rawlinswood is a dense forest that shares many characteristics with Dunwood. It is a place of ancient magic and hidden dangers, with creatures such as dire wolves and owlbears roaming its depths. The forest is also home to several tribes of fey, who are known for their trickery and capricious nature. Rawlinswood is a place where the boundaries between the material world and the feywild are thin, making it a site of interest for those who study planar magic.

### The Beast Country

To the north of the Great Dale lies the Beast Country, a rugged and untamed land inhabited by tribes of gnolls and other savage creatures. The region is known for its harsh climate and unforgiving terrain, making it a challenging environment for even the most seasoned adventurers. Despite its dangers, the Beast Country is rich in natural resources, including rare herbs and minerals that are highly prized by alchemists and traders.

### The Great Barrow

The Great Barrow is an ancient burial mound located near the center of the Great Dale. It is believed to be the final resting place of a powerful Nar chieftain and is rumored to contain vast treasures. However, the barrow is also said to be cursed, and those who seek to disturb its contents often meet with misfortune. The Great Barrow is a site of great historical and cultural significance, attracting scholars and adventurers alike who seek to uncover its secrets.
