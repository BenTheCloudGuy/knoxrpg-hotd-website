# Narfell

![Map: Forgotten Lands](../../images/realms/map-02.005-forgotten-lands.jpg)

> **At a Glance:** Booming trade cities dot rugged wilds in a diverse region that bursts with untapped wealth: gemstone-studded mountains, powerful primal magic, and relics of buried kingdoms.
> **Region:** Forgotten Lands
> **Languages:** Damaran

Narfell is a land steeped in ancient history, marked by the ruins of two great empires: Narfell, known for its demon worshipers, and Raumathar, renowned for its battle mages. These ancient civilizations left behind a legacy of powerful magic and cursed ruins, now scattered across the plains where nomadic horse riders roam. The Nars, the current inhabitants, are a pragmatic people who value deeds over lineage. This practical mindset leads them to generally avoid the demon-cursed remnants of their ancestors. However, a faction among them seeks to reclaim the dark magic of ancient Narfell, aspiring to revive the sinister practices of Nar demonology. The region is also characterized by its rugged geography, including the formidable Mount Nar and the expansive plains that provide both a home and a challenge to its nomadic people.

## Notable Locations

### Bildoobaris

In a vast, empty plain beneath the shadow of Mount Nar, tens of thousands of nomads gather for one tenday every summer for an annual trade meet, forming a vast tent city called Bildoobaris. Here, Nars barter in horses, furs, and jewelry; share news and oral histories; and celebrate the realm’s all-too-brief time of plenty. In recent years, Nar demonologists have come to Bildoobaris bearing demonic relics and weapons from the buried cities of ancient Narfell in the hope of recruiting more followers.

### Dun-Tharos

Located in the nearby Dunwood, Dun-Tharos was once the capital of ancient Narfell. Now a ruin, it is shrouded in dark magic and haunted by the remnants of its demonic past. The ruins are a focal point for those seeking to understand the powerful conjurations that once emanated from the city. Adventurers and scholars alike are drawn to Dun-Tharos, despite the dangers posed by lingering demonic entities and unstable magical energies.

### Mount Nar

This imposing mountain dominates the landscape of Narfell, serving as both a geographical landmark and a cultural symbol for the Nars. It is said that the mountain holds veins of precious gemstones, though few have dared to mine them due to the treacherous terrain and the mountain's association with ancient curses. Mount Nar is also a place of spiritual significance, with many Nars believing it to be a sacred site connected to the spirits of their ancestors.

### The Great Dale

Bordering Narfell to the west, the Great Dale is a vast forested region that provides a natural barrier between Narfell and the rest of Faerun. The dense woods are home to various creatures and are known for their untamed beauty. The Great Dale is also the site of the Order of the Emerald Enclave, a group dedicated to maintaining the balance between civilization and nature. This order keeps a watchful eye on Narfell, wary of the dark forces that might spill over from the demon-cursed lands.

### The Rawlinswood

Once part of the great forest that covered much of the region, the Rawlinswood is a dark and foreboding place. It is rumored to be inhabited by malevolent spirits and creatures that serve the lingering demonic powers of ancient Narfell. The forest is a place of mystery and danger, attracting those who seek forbidden knowledge or wish to test their mettle against its perils.

## DM Notes

Ancient Narfell's demon-worshiping past is directly relevant to current campaign events. The demonic relics and weapons being recovered from buried Nar cities, and the rise of new Nar demonologists at Bildoobaris, represent a growing threat that could compound the supernatural dangers already unleashed by the Fall of Valls.

The ruins of Dun-Tharos in the nearby Dunwood (see Great Dale) were the former capital of ancient Narfell. The demonic powers once controlled by Nar conjurers are connected to the same planar instabilities the Order of the Yellow Rose was established to guard against.
