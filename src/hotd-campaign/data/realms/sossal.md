# Sossal

![Map: Beyond the Map](../../images/realms/map-02.005-forgotten-lands.jpg)

> **At a Glance:** Distant lands and island realms that lie at the edges of Faerunian knowledge and exploration.
> **Region:** Beyond the Map
> **Languages:** Various

The remote realm of Sossal, far to the north of Narfell, has little contact with the rest of Faerun. It is a land shrouded in mystery, known for its icy landscapes and the Great Glacier that dominates the region. The Sossrim, the people of Sossal, are known for their unique use of druidic magic, which allows them to manipulate the natural world in ways that are foreign to most Faerunians. Their culture is deeply intertwined with the harsh environment they inhabit, leading to a society that values resilience and resourcefulness.

Sossal's history is largely undocumented, but it is believed that the Sossrim have lived in harmony with their environment for centuries, developing a society that thrives despite the extreme cold. The Sossrim are skilled craftsmen, particularly noted for their ability to shape wood with magic rather than tools. This skill is evident in the beautiful and intricate furniture that they trade with southern lands, fetching high prices in places like Sembia.

## Notable Locations

### Naupau
Naupau is the only known settlement in Sossal that maintains semi-regular contact with the outside world. It serves as a trading hub where Sossrim merchants exchange goods with traders from Damara and other southern regions. The town is built from the sturdy wood of the northern forests, shaped by the magical talents of the Sossrim.

### The Great Glacier
The Great Glacier is a massive ice sheet that covers much of Sossal. It is both a natural barrier and a resource, providing the Sossrim with seal meat and furs. The glacier is also a place of spiritual significance, with many Sossrim believing it to be the home of ancient spirits and powerful elemental forces.

### The Ice Forest
This dense, frozen woodland is located near the heart of Sossal. It is known for its towering trees, which are said to be as hard as iron. The Sossrim use their druidic magic to navigate and harvest resources from this forest, crafting their renowned wooden goods.

### The Frostfire Peaks
A mountain range that lies to the east of Sossal, the Frostfire Peaks are known for their unusual geological activity. Despite the freezing temperatures, the mountains occasionally emit bursts of flame and smoke, a phenomenon that has puzzled scholars and adventurers alike. The peaks are considered sacred by the Sossrim, who believe they are the dwelling place of powerful elemental beings.

### The Sea of Moving Ice
To the north of Sossal lies the Sea of Moving Ice, a treacherous expanse of ice floes and frigid waters. It is a dangerous area, home to polar bears and other arctic creatures. The Sossrim occasionally venture into these waters for hunting and exploration, but few outsiders dare to follow.

### The Whispering Caverns
These caverns are located beneath the Great Glacier and are known for the eerie sounds that echo through their tunnels. The Sossrim regard the caverns with a mix of fear and reverence, believing them to be inhabited by the spirits of their ancestors. The caverns are also rumored to contain veins of precious metals and ancient relics.

### The Shimmering Plains
A vast expanse of tundra that stretches across southern Sossal, the Shimmering Plains are named for the way the sunlight reflects off the ice and snow. This area is home to herds of caribou and other wildlife, providing the Sossrim with vital resources for survival.

### The Crystal Caves
Located near the Frostfire Peaks, the Crystal Caves are renowned for their stunning natural formations. The walls of the caves are lined with crystals that catch the light in a dazzling display. The Sossrim occasionally harvest these crystals for use in their crafts and rituals.

### The Northern Lights Sanctuary
A sacred site for the Sossrim, this sanctuary is a place where the northern lights are particularly vivid. It is used for religious ceremonies and is considered a place of power, where the veil between the material world and the spiritual realm is thin.

Sossal remains an enigmatic land, its secrets guarded by the ice and its people. While contact with the outside world is limited, the unique culture and magical practices of the Sossrim continue to intrigue those who hear tales of this distant realm.
