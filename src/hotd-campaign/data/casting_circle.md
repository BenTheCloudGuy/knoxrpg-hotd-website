# Circle Magic

Millennia ago, elf mages researching alternative magical traditions developed a technique by which many spellcasters working together could cast spells beyond the limit of any individual. This technique became known as circle magic. When the wizards and archmages of Netheril learned the technique from the elves, they refined it far beyond its original scope. The Netherese called them simply "Circles," and through their mastery of this cooperative spellcraft they created some of the most powerful artifacts and magical works Faerun has ever known, from the mythallars that held their floating cities aloft to wards that could shield entire kingdoms. After the fall of Netheril, the knowledge of circle magic scattered across the Realms, surviving in fragments among the Red Wizards of Thay, the Witches of Rashemen, and isolated arcane traditions that trace their lineage back to the Netherese golden age.

## What Is a Circle Spell?

Any spell that can be augmented via metamagic can benefit from being cast as part of a Circle of 2 or more mages. A spell cast in this way is known as a Circle spell, and it allows cooperative spellcasters to push a single spell far beyond what any one of them could achieve alone.

## The Lead

One caster is designated "The Lead." The Lead is the one who must know the spell and is responsible for controlling the weave to cast it. The level of The Lead does not matter; a 3rd-level wizard can lead a circle just as well as a 15th-level archmage, provided they know the spell being cast. The Lead decides the spell's targets, maintains Concentration if required, provides the spell's components, and decides all options noted in the spell's description. The spell's effects originate from The Lead.

## Circle Members

The other members of the circle are lending their arcane power to The Lead in order for that person to apply one or more metamagic enhancements to the spell being cast. A circle member must have either the Spellcasting or Pact Magic feature to participate.

After The Lead initiates the spell, each circle member takes the Magic action to contribute. A circle member can take this action only while within 30 feet of The Lead and before the start of The Lead's next turn.

## Fueling the Circle

Circle members fuel the spell by expending spell slots. The slots expended by circle members serve two purposes: they can power Circle Enhancements (see below), and they can substitute for The Lead's own spell slots entirely.

**Casting Beyond Your Limits.** Through the circle, The Lead can draw on the arcane reserves of other members to cast spells even after they have expended all of their own spell slots. Circle members contribute slots equal to the spell's level, or an equivalent combination of lower-level slots using the following conversion:

| Equivalent Slot Cost | Slots Required |
|:-------------------:|:--------------:|
| 1st-level spell | 1 x 1st-level slot |
| 2nd-level spell | 2 x 1st-level slots |
| 3rd-level spell | 2 x 2nd-level slots, or 4 x 1st-level slots |
| 4th-level spell | 2 x 3rd-level slots, or equivalent combination |
| 5th-level spell | 2 x 4th-level slots, or equivalent combination |
| Higher levels | Follow the same doubling pattern |

The general rule: two slots of a given level combine to produce the effect of one slot of the next level up. Multiple circle members can split the cost between them.

*Example: The Lead wants to cast Fireball (3rd level) but has no slots left. Two circle members each expend a 2nd-level slot (2 x 2nd = 3rd), providing enough power for the spell. Alternatively, one circle member could expend four 1st-level slots.*

## Establishing and Breaking the Circle

Once the circle is established and the casting begins, The Lead has full control over the spell and the flow of arcane energy. Circle members cannot voluntarily withdraw their power once they have committed it.

The only way to break from a circle once established is to make a **Wisdom saving throw with a DC of 12 + the number of members in the circle** (including The Lead). On a success, the member tears free, losing their contributed spell slots but disrupting the circle. If any member breaks free, the spell fails.

*Example: A circle of 5 (The Lead + 4 members) has a break DC of 17 (12 + 5). A member who wants out must succeed on a DC 17 Wisdom save.*

**External Disruption.** If The Lead loses Concentration (from damage, incapacitation, or death), the circle collapses and the spell fails. All expended spell slots are lost. If a circle member is incapacitated or killed after contributing, the circle is not disrupted; their power has already been given.

## Eligible Spells

Any spell that has a casting time of an action or 1 minute or longer and is cast using a spell slot can be cast as a Circle spell. Cantrips cannot be cast as Circle spells. To cast a Circle spell, follow the usual rules for spellcasting as well as the rules described here.

### Completing the Casting

If the spell has a casting time of an action, the Circle spell's effects occur immediately after the final circle member takes the required action to contribute. The Lead decides which member is the final one.

If the spell has a casting time of 1 minute or more, The Lead and each circle member must take the Magic action on each of their turns for the entire casting time, and all must maintain Concentration while they do so. If any caster's Concentration is broken, the spell fails.

### Circle Save DC

When a spell is cast as a Circle spell, the combined arcane power of the circle makes the spell inherently harder to resist. The spell's save DC increases by +1 for every 2 circle members contributing (rounded down), to a maximum bonus of +3.

| Circle Members | DC Bonus |
|:--------------:|:--------:|
| 1 | +0 |
| 2-3 | +1 |
| 4-5 | +2 |
| 6+ | +3 (maximum) |

This bonus stacks with the Fortify enhancement, but the total combined DC increase from both Circle casting and Fortify cannot exceed +5.

*Example: A Wizard (base DC 17) leads a circle with 4 members. The base Circle DC bonus is +2 (for 4 members), making the save DC 19. If one member also applies Fortify (+1), the final DC is 20.*

### Spell Failure

If a Circle spell fails, all expended spell slots are lost. The Lead and every circle member who contributed have already committed their arcane power to manipulate the weave; that energy is spent whether the spell completes or not. However, Material components are not consumed by a failed casting. Components are only consumed as the spell takes effect, and a spell that fails never reaches that stage.

## Circle Enhancements

Each circle member who joins grants **one Circle Enhancement** chosen by The Lead. You can apply multiple enhancements to the same spell by having multiple circle members, and you can stack the same enhancement more than once unless noted otherwise. Each enhancement has a minimum slot cost that the contributing circle member must meet (in addition to any slots spent to fuel the base spell).

### Empower (Minimum: 1st-level slot)

Increase the spell's damage by damage die per circle member applying this enhancement spell slot. The extra damage is the same type as the spell's original damage. If the spell deals multiple damage types, the The Lead chooses which type the bonus damage uses.

*Example: Fireball with 2 casters applying Empower via a Level 1 spell slot = deals 8d6 + 2d6 fire damage. -or- Example: Fireball with 2 casters applying Empower via a Level 3 spell slot = deals 8d6 + 6d6 fire damage. etc etc*

### Heighten (Minimum: slot equal to the spell's level)

The spell is cast as though using a spell slot one level higher per circle member applying this enhancement. The circle member must expend a slot of a level equal to or higher than the spell's base level.

This increases damage, healing, targets, or any other effect that scales with slot level, following the spell's "At Higher Levels" text. The spell cannot be heightened above 9th level.


### Widen (Minimum: slot equal to the spell's level)

Increase one dimension of the spell's area of effect is doubled per circle member applying this enhancement. For a Sphere, this increases the radius. For a Cone, this increases the length. For a Line, the The Lead chooses whether to increase length and/or width.

*Example: Fireball (20-ft radius) with 2 casters applying Widen becomes a 80-ft radius sphere.*

### Extend (Minimum: slot equal to the spell's level)

Increase the spell's duration according to the table below, based on the total number of circle members applying this enhancement. The spell must have a duration of at least 1 minute. This enhancement cannot be stacked; use the highest tier that applies.

| Casters Applying Extend | Duration Increase |
|:-----------------------:|:-----------------:|
| 1 | Double the base duration |
| 2-3 | 1 hour |
| 4-6 | 8 hours |
| 7+ | 24 hours |

*Example: Hold Person (1 minute) with 1 caster applying Extend lasts 2 minutes. With 3 casters, it lasts 1 hour.*

### Reach (Minimum: slot equal to the spell's level)

Increase the spell's range by double per circle member applying this enhancement. A spell with a range of Touch becomes 30 feet instead, and additional casters applying doubles from there.


### Fortify (Minimum: 1st-level slot)

Increase the spell's save DC by +1 per spell slot level per circle member applying this enhancement.Additionally, creatures have Disadvantage on the first saving throw they make against this spell if at least 2 casters apply Fortify.

*Example: A Wizard with DC 17 casts Hold Monster with 1 caster using a 2nd level spell slot applying Fortify. The save DC is 19, and the target has Disadvantage on its first Wisdom save.*

### Quicken (Minimum: slot equal to the spell's level)

Reduce the spell's casting time by one step: 1 minute becomes 1 action, 1 action becomes 1 bonus action. A spell cannot be reduced below a bonus action. Only one circle member can apply Quicken to a given spell.

*Example: A Cleric initiates Prayer of Healing (casting time: 1 minute) with 1 caster applying Quicken. The spell completes as a single action.*

### Distribute (Minimum: slot equal to the spell's level)

When you cast a spell that requires Concentration, you can distribute the mental load of the spell among you and all circle members. As long as at least one caster who contributed to the spell maintains Concentration, the spell's effects remain active. Each caster maintaining Concentration can still concentrate on one other spell of their own as normal.

Only one caster needs to apply Distribute; all circle members share in the benefit.

### Safeguard (Minimum: 1st-level slot)

When you cast a spell that creates an area of effect, you can carve out a safe zone within that area. The safe zone consists of a number of 5-foot Cubes equal to your spellcasting ability modifier plus the number of circle members applying this enhancement (minimum of one Cube). You can arrange the Cubes as you like, but each Cube must be contiguous with at least one other Cube. Creatures and objects in the safe zone are unaffected by the spell.

If the spell's area of effect can be moved, the safe zone moves with it.

*Example: A Wizard (INT +5) casts Cloudkill with 2 casters applying Safeguard. The safe zone is 7 contiguous 5-ft Cubes (5 + 2), enough to shelter the party.*

### Supplant (Minimum: slot equal to the spell's level)

When you cast a spell that requires at least one Material component with a specified cost that is consumed by the spell, you can reduce the minimum cost of one such component by 100 GP per circle member applying this enhancement. The cost cannot be reduced below 0 GP.

*Example: Revivify (300 GP diamond) with 2 casters applying Supplant requires only a 100 GP diamond.*

## Circle Magic in the Game

Circle magic provides unique and strategic ways of altering spellcasting. Here are some general guidelines for implementing circle magic in your game.

**Balance.** Circle magic is powerful, but it costs the action economy of every participating caster plus their spell slots. A circle of 3 burns 4 total spell slots (1 Lead + 3 members) and 4 actions to produce one enhanced spell. This is a significant resource investment and should feel appropriately impactful.

**Character Level and Experience.** Though characters of any level can use circle magic, characters with access to higher-level spells have more avenues to make the most of it. Low-level parties can still benefit from Empower and Reach, while high-level circles can combine Heighten, Fortify, and Widen for devastating effects.

**Combat Pacing.** Circle spells are best planned before initiative. To avoid bogging down combat, the The Lead should announce the spell, enhancements, and which circle members are applying which effects before dice are rolled. Consider using circle magic for opening salvos or climactic moments rather than every round.

**Out of Combat.** Many enhancements shine outside of combat. Extend turns a 1-hour buff into an 8-hour one. Reach lets a healer work from across a chasm. Supplant makes expensive rituals affordable. Encourage creative non-combat uses.

**Limits.** A spell cannot be Heightened above 9th level. A save DC cannot be increased by more than +6 from Fortify. Only one caster can apply Quicken or Distribute per spell. These caps prevent circle magic from breaking bounded accuracy or action economy too severely.
