## Traits

**Shapechanger.** Emil can use her action to polymorph into a wolf-humanoid hybrid, a dire wolf, or back into her human form. Her statistics other than AC are the same in each form. Any equipment she is wearing or carrying isn't transformed. She reverts to human form if she dies.

**Keen Hearing and Smell.** Emil has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or smell.

**Pack Tactics.** Emil has advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least one of her allies is within 5 feet of the target and the ally isn't incapacitated.

**Shaman's Gift.** Emil has innate spellcasting through her connection to the primal spirits of Barovia. Her spellcasting ability is Wisdom (spell save DC 13, +5 to hit).

## Spellcasting

**At will:**
- *Spare the Dying* — Touch a creature at 0 HP. It becomes stable. No action needed beyond touch.

**2/day each:**
- *Cure Wounds* (2nd level) — Touch. Restore 2d8 + 2 hit points. Does not work on undead or constructs.
- *Healing Word* — Bonus action, 60 ft. range. Restore 1d4 + 2 hit points. Can attack and heal same turn.

**1/day:**
- *Lesser Restoration* — Touch. End one disease or one condition: blinded, deafened, paralyzed, or poisoned.

## Actions

**Multiattack (Hybrid Form Only).** Emil makes two attacks: one with her bite and one with her claws, or two with her greatsword.

**Bite (Wolf/Hybrid Only).** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d10 + 3) piercing damage. If the target is a humanoid, it must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or be cursed with lycanthropy.

**Claws (Hybrid Only).** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (2d4 + 3) slashing damage.

**Greatsword (Human/Hybrid Only).** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) slashing damage.

## DM Notes

- Emil leads the werewolf pack that arrives during the Wolf Pack Arrives phase.
- She does not want to continue the war with humans. She lost her mate Zuleika to Strahd's treachery.
- She has healing magic and will cast healing on injured players once she reaches the hilltop.
- Players will recognize her as the werewolf that jumped from the Wizard's Tower.
- Her wolves will attack the catapult and the ranged goblin archers, suffering casualties in the process.
- Emil pushes through the stitches to reach the players and offer her help directly.
